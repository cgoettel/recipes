# Bread dressing

Baked in a pan, so there's no need to stuff the bird. Very moist.

## Ingredients

- 2½ loaves bread, torn or cubed
- 1 lb onions, chopped
- ¼ bunch celery, chopped
- 2¼ sticks (18 tablespoons) butter
- 1 cup shortening
- ½ cup stock (or a bouillon cube dissolved in ½ cup water)
- 2 cups milk
- Salt, rubbed sage, and pepper, to taste
- 1 teaspoon baking powder

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 300°F.
2. Melt the butter and shortening in a large skillet and cook the onions and celery until soft.
3. In a very large bowl, toss the bread with the onion mixture, stock, and milk. Season with salt, sage, and pepper. Add water to moisten if needed.
4. Spread in a large baking pan and sprinkle the baking powder over the top.
5. Bake uncovered for 2½ hours.
