# Coconut almond cookies

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup shortening
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon almond extract
- 1½ cups flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 cups coconut

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
2. Cream together the sugar, shortening, egg, and almond extract.
3. Add the flour and salt and mix well. Fold in the coconut.
4. Shape into 1-inch balls and place on an ungreased cookie sheet. Flatten with a fork.
5. Bake 15–18 minutes, until slightly golden around the edges.
