# Tangy teriyaki-pineapple sauce

A sweet-sour, tomato-based cooking sauce from a newspaper clipping. Use it on pork or beef ribs, burgers, chicken, and steak.

## Ingredients

- 1 (29 oz) can tomato sauce
- 2 cups distilled vinegar
- 1 (10 oz) can pineapple chunks in unsweetened juice, drained and chopped (reserve the juice)
- 1 cup firmly packed brown sugar
- 1 cup chopped onion
- ¼ cup teriyaki sauce
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 2 cloves garlic
- ½ teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Combine all the ingredients in a large saucepan (at least 3 quarts).
2. Taste, and add a little of the reserved pineapple juice if you like. Keep in mind the flavors will concentrate as the sauce reduces.
3. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then simmer until thick and reduced to about 6 cups.
