# Bread — Baguette (Quick)

Makes 2 baguettes. Faster counterpart to the [Paul Hollywood version](bread-baguette-paul-hollywood.md).

## Ingredients

- 1½ C warm water
- 1½ T yeast
- 2 t sugar
- 3¼ C flour
- 2 t salt
- Melted butter, for brushing

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, mix the warm water with the yeast and sugar. Let sit ~5 minutes — foam should form on top.
1. In a large bowl, combine the flour and salt. Gradually add the yeast mixture. Mix until the dough becomes a smooth ball, then knead 5 minutes (KitchenAid with dough hook works).
1. Cut in half and shape into 2 baguettes. For smooth, pretty loaves, roll each half into a rectangle and roll up lengthwise. Pinch the edges to seal and place seam-down on a lightly greased cookie sheet (or Silpat).
1. Cover and let rise 30 minutes.
1. Preheat oven to 450°F. With a very sharp knife, gently slash down the length of each baguette.
1. Pour water into a cookie sheet and place on the bottom rack of the oven.
1. Bake 15 minutes. Halfway through, brush the bread with melted butter.
