# Salsa birria

- 8–10 chile de arbol
- 2 chile pasilla
- 10–12 tomatillos
- 2–3 cloves garlic
- ⅛–¼ white onion
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. In a heavy-bottom pot, add 1–2 L water (measure and report back) and bring to a boil.
2. Add chiles and tomatillos and boil for 13 minutes.
3. Kill the heat and let it steep for 10 minutes.
4. Transfer to a blender and add a ladleful of the steeped liquid and blend until smooth. Add more water as necessary to reach desired consistency. Remember that it will thicken slightly as it cools.
