# Oatmeal cornflake cookies

## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (1 cup) butter, softened
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1½ cups sugar
- 1 tablespoon milk
- 1½ teaspoons vanilla
- 2 eggs
- 1½ cups flour
- 1¼ teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon mace
- 1½ teaspoons cinnamon
- ¼ teaspoon nutmeg
- ⅛ teaspoon ground cloves
- 1 cup cornflakes, crushed
- 3 cups oatmeal
- 4 oz coconut
- 1 (12 oz) package chocolate chips
- 1 cup nuts

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Cream the butter, sugars, milk, and vanilla until smooth. Beat in the eggs.
3. Mix the flour, baking soda, salt, and spices, and stir them into the creamed mixture.
4. Mix in the crushed cornflakes, oatmeal, coconut, chocolate chips, and nuts.
5. Drop onto cookie sheets and bake for 10 minutes.
