# Chili — Mom's

From LeAnn Goettel. The longer it simmers, the better it gets. Sausage, green chiles, garlic, and diced tomatoes are all good additions — flavor it to your liking.

## Ingredients

- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 T butter
- 1½ lb hamburger
- ⅔ C ketchup
- ½ C water
- ¼ C celery, finely chopped
- 1 (16 oz) can kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (15 oz) can pinto beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 2 T lemon juice
- 1 T brown sugar
- 1½ t Worcestershire sauce
- 1 t white vinegar
- ½ t seasoned salt
- ¼ t dry mustard
- Chili powder (see Notes)

## Instructions

1. Sauté the onion in butter.
1. Add the hamburger and brown it. Drain off excess fat.
1. Add all remaining ingredients except the beans. Mix well and heat thoroughly.
1. Add the beans, an additional large can of diced tomatoes, and 3–6 t of chili powder to taste.
1. Cover and simmer ~30 minutes.

## Notes

- Mom likes it thick — adjust water accordingly.
