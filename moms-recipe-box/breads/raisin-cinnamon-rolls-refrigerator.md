# Raisin cinnamon rolls (refrigerator rise)

Shaped ahead and held in the refrigerator for 2 to 24 hours before baking.

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 2⅔ cups flour (1 cup, then ⅓ cup, then about 1⅓ cups)
- ¼ cup sugar
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon grated lemon peel
- 1 package (2¼ teaspoons) active dry yeast
- ¾ cup milk
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 egg

### Filling

- 2 tablespoons butter, melted
- ¾ cup sugar
- ½ cup raisins
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon

## Instructions

01. In a large bowl, mix 1 cup of the flour with the sugar, salt, lemon peel, and yeast.
02. Heat the milk and butter in a saucepan over low heat until the milk is very warm (120–130°F); the butter doesn't need to melt. Add to the dry ingredients.
03. Beat for 2 minutes at medium speed with an electric mixer, scraping the bowl occasionally. Add the egg and ⅓ cup flour and beat at high speed for 2 minutes.
04. Stir in enough additional flour (about 1⅓ cups) to make a soft dough. Turn out onto a lightly floured board.
05. Knead: shape the dough into a ball and flatten it slightly. Fold it toward you, push it away with the heels of your hands, and give it a quarter turn. Repeat until smooth and springy, 5–10 minutes.
06. Cover with plastic wrap, then a towel, and let rest 20 minutes.
07. Punch the dough down, turn it onto a lightly floured board, and roll into an 18×9-inch rectangle. Brush with the melted butter.
08. Combine the sugar, raisins, and cinnamon and sprinkle over the dough. Roll up from a long side and seal the edges firmly.
09. Cut into 18 pieces. Place them cut side up, about 1 inch apart, in 2 greased 8-inch round cake pans. Brush wax paper with oil and lay it loosely over the rolls.
10. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate 2 to 24 hours.
11. When ready to bake, preheat the oven to 375°F. Uncover the rolls carefully and let them stand at room temperature for 10 minutes.
12. Bake for 20–25 minutes, until done. Remove from the pans and cool on wire racks. Frost with powdered sugar frosting if you like.
