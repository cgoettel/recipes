# Chocolate-covered strawberries

Each berry is wrapped in a thin layer of almond buttercream before it is dipped. A little more work than plain dipped berries, but worth it.

## Ingredients

### Chocolate

- 1 (12 oz) package semisweet chocolate chips
- 1 tablespoon vegetable shortening
- 2 tablespoons butter

### Almond dough

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter, softened
- 3 cups powdered sugar
- 1 tablespoon milk
- 2 teaspoons almond extract

### Berries

- 1½ pints fresh strawberries, with pretty stems intact

## Instructions

1. In the top of a double boiler, melt the chocolate chips, shortening, and 2 tablespoons butter over hot, not boiling, water. Don't rush it: melt it slowly and gently, or the chocolate will seize.
2. Meanwhile, cream the stick of butter and the powdered sugar together. Beat in the milk and almond extract to form a stiff dough.
3. Pinch off a small portion of dough and pat it out on your hand into a thin round. Work quickly, or the dough will begin to melt.
4. Set a berry in the center and form the dough up around it, leaving the stem exposed. Repeat with the rest of the berries.
5. If possible, chill the wrapped berries so the chocolate hardens faster when you dip them.
6. Dip each berry in the melted chocolate to within ¼ inch of the top of the almond dough, which gives the prettiest look. Set on a waxed paper-lined cookie sheet and chill.
7. When the chocolate has hardened, move the berries to a covered container in the refrigerator.

## Notes

- The berries are best within 24 to 36 hours. After that they begin to ooze juice, but they still taste wonderful.
