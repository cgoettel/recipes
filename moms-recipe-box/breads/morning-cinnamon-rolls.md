# Morning cinnamon rolls

Made the night before, risen overnight, and baked in the morning.

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 1 package (2¼ teaspoons) active dry yeast
- ½ cup warm water
- 2½ cups water
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon salt
- ½ cup oil
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 10 cups flour

### Filling

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter, softened
- 1 cup brown sugar
- ½ cup sugar
- Cinnamon, to taste

## Instructions

1. In the evening, dissolve the yeast in the warm water and let it sit until foamy.
2. Mix in the water, sugar, salt, oil, eggs, and flour to make a soft dough.
3. Leave the dough in a covered bowl and punch it down every hour or so until bedtime.
4. Working with enough dough for a dozen rolls at a time, roll it out into a rectangle. Spread with softened butter and sprinkle with brown sugar, sugar, and cinnamon.
5. Roll up, cut into 12 slices, and place on a greased pan. Repeat with the rest of the dough.
6. Cover and let rise overnight.
7. In the morning, preheat the oven to 375°F and bake for 15 minutes.
8. Ice them if you like.

## Notes

- A handwritten note beside the filling suggests rolling out half the dough at a time.
