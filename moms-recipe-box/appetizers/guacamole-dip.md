# Guacamole dip

A blender guacamole, marked "Mexico" on the card.

## Ingredients

- 1 large ripe avocado, cubed
- 1 (8-inch) stalk celery, cut in 1-inch pieces
- ½ small onion
- 1 small clove garlic
- ¼ teaspoon Tabasco sauce
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon mayonnaise
- ½ small hot green pepper

## Instructions

1. Put all the ingredients into a blender container and cover.
2. Process on high until smooth, using a rubber spatula to keep the mixture flowing to the blades.
