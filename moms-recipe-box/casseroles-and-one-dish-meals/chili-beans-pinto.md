# Pinto bean chili

## Ingredients

- 1 lb dried pinto beans, soaked
- 1 teaspoon basil
- 1½ teaspoons mustard
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
- 1½ teaspoons vinegar
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 (6 oz) can tomato paste
- 5 teaspoons chili powder
- 1 (16 oz) can stewed tomatoes
- Cayenne pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Put the soaked beans in a pot with water to cover and add the basil, mustard, onion, brown sugar, garlic powder, and vinegar.
2. Cook over medium heat for 3 hours.
3. Halfway through, brown the ground beef and add it along with the tomato paste, chili powder, and stewed tomatoes. Season with cayenne pepper to taste.
4. Simmer over low heat until the beans are tender.
