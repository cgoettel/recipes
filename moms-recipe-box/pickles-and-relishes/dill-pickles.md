# Dill pickles

## Ingredients

- Cucumbers
- Fresh dill, 3 stems per jar

### Brine

- 6 cups water
- 3 cups brown sugar
- ½ cup salt
- ½ teaspoon powdered alum

## Instructions

1. Wash the cucumbers and let them drain.
2. Pack the cucumbers into clean jars and put 3 stems of fresh dill in each jar.
3. Bring the water, brown sugar, salt, and alum to a boil.
4. Pour the hot liquid over the pickles and seal.

## Notes

- The card lists no vinegar, which most dill pickle brines include, and calls for brown sugar, which reads more like a sweet pickle. It is written here as the card has it.
