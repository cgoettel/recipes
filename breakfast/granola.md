# Granola
## Ingredients
- 3 C old fashioned oats
- 1 C slivered or sliced almonds
- 1 C coconut
- 3 T flour
- &frac12; C maple syrup
- &frac13; C coconut oil
- 1 t almond extract
- 1 t cinnamon
- &frac18; t salt

## Instructions
- Preheat oven to 300&deg;F
- Line baking sheet with parchment
- Mix together oats, almonds, coconut, and flour
- In a separate bowl, whisk together maple syrup, coconut oil, almond extract, cinnamon, and salt
- Add liquid mixture to dry ingredients and stir until evenly coated
- Spread mixture onto baking sheet
- Bake 40&ndash;50 minutes, stirring halfway through
- Let cool and store in an airtight container
