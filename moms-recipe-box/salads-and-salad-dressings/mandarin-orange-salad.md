# Mandarin orange salad

## Ingredients

### Dressing

- ½ cup oil
- ¼ cup vinegar
- ¼ cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 dash pepper

### Candied almonds

- ½ cup sliced almonds
- 3 tablespoons sugar

### Salad

- 1 large head iceberg lettuce
- ½ head romaine lettuce
- 2 cups chopped celery
- 6 green onions, chopped
- 2 (11 oz) cans mandarin oranges, drained

## Instructions

1. Whisk together the dressing ingredients.
2. Place the almonds and sugar in a frying pan over medium heat. Stir until the sugar melts and the almonds brown, caramelizing them.
3. Pour onto wax paper, let cool, and break apart.
4. Tear the lettuces into pieces and refrigerate in a plastic bag to crisp.
5. Add the celery and green onions to the lettuce and toss.
6. When ready to serve, add the oranges, almonds, and dressing, and toss.

## Notes

- Scales well: the card is marked "12× for 196 people."
