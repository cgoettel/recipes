# Swiss steak

A pressure-cooker Swiss steak with pan gravy.

## Ingredients

### Steak

- Round steak, cut into serving-size pieces
- Flour, for dredging
- Shortening or oil, for browning
- Garlic salt
- Pepper
- Salt
- 1 large onion, sliced

### Gravy

- 2 cups stock (water added to the meat drippings)
- 4 tablespoons flour
- ¾ cup cold water
- Worcestershire sauce

## Instructions

1. Roll the steak pieces in flour.
2. Brown them in a small amount of fat in the pressure cooker. Take the meat out.
3. Make the gravy in the pressure cooker: add water to the drippings to make 2 cups of stock. Blend the flour with the cold water until smooth, then gradually stir it into the stock and cook until smooth and thickened. Add Worcestershire sauce to taste.
4. Season the gravy with garlic salt, pepper, and salt to taste.
5. Put the meat and the sliced onion into the gravy.
6. Pressure cook at 15 psi for 15 minutes. Let the pressure come down by itself (natural release).
