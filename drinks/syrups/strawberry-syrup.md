# Strawberry Syrup

A no-cook strawberry syrup — macerating equal weights of fruit and sugar draws a clean, bright syrup without dulling the flavor on the stove. Caster sugar dissolves faster into the cold maceration.

## Ingredients

- 250 g strawberries, chopped
- 250 g caster sugar

## Instructions

1. Mash the strawberries and sugar together, cover, and refrigerate 24 hours.
2. Strain through a nut-milk bag, pressing to get all the syrup.
