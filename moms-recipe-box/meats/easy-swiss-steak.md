# Easy Swiss steak

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb round steak, ¾ inch thick
- 2 tablespoons shortening
- 1 (10.5 oz) can cream of mushroom soup
- ½ cup canned tomatoes
- ¼ cup chopped onion
- ¼ cup water
- Dash pepper

## Instructions

1. Pound the steak and cut it into serving-size pieces.
2. In a frying pan, brown the steak in the shortening; pour off the fat.
3. Add the remaining ingredients. Cover and cook over low heat 1¼ hours, or until tender, stirring occasionally.
