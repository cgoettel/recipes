# Three-chile salsa

The card is a shopping-style list without amounts; season and balance to taste.

## Ingredients

- Yellow chiles
- Green chiles
- Red chiles
- Cilantro
- Green onions
- Whole canned tomatoes (Hunt's)
- Jalapeños
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Chop the chiles, cilantro, green onions, and jalapeños.
2. Chop or crush the tomatoes and stir everything together.
3. Season with salt to taste.
