# Swedish Pancakes (Åke Jon)

## Ingredients

- 3 cups flour (or 2 cups flour and 1 cup whole wheat flour)
- 0.5 tsp salt (1/2 tsp)
- 6 cups milk (1.42 liters)
- 3 pcs eggs
- 1 tablespoon butter for greasing the roasting pan

## Serving

Strawberry jam and whipped cream

## Equipment

1 x roasting pan 30x40 cm (11.8 x 15.7 inches)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 225 ° C (437 ° F).
2. Measure the flour and salt in a bowl. Mix.
3. Beat down the milk a little at a time to get lump-free batter.
4. Beat in the eggs.
5. Pour batter into greased roasting pan about 30x40 cm. You can also line it with parchment paper to avoid washing.
