# Strawberry nut salad

## Ingredients

- 1 large (6 oz) package strawberry or raspberry Jell-O
- 2 cups hot water
- 1 (10 oz) package frozen strawberries in juice, thawed
- 1 small (8 oz) can crushed pineapple, with juice
- 1 small package chopped walnuts

## Instructions

1. In a large pan, mix the Jell-O with the water and heat until dissolved.
2. Stir in the strawberries and pineapple with their juices.
3. Pour into a 1½-quart glass dish and put the nuts on top.
4. Chill until firm.
