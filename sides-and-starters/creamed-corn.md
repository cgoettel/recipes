# Creamed corn

## Ingredients

- 3 cups frozen corn
- ½ cup whole milk
- 4 oz cream cheese
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter
- 1½ teaspoons sugar
- ½ teaspoon pepper
- ½ teaspoon salt

## Instructions

- Throw everything into a crock pot. Stir until the cream cheese has melted in thoroughly.
