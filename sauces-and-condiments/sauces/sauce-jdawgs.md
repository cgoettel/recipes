# J Dawg's special sauce

The sauce that built the J Dawg's hot dog empire.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup ketchup
- ¾ cup BBQ sauce
- ¼ cup honey
- 1 teaspoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar
- ½ teaspoon onion powder

## Instructions

1. Mix and enjoy.
