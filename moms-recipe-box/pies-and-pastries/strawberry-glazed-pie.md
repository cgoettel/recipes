# Strawberry glazed pie

A vanilla cream pie topped with glazed strawberries. From a Jell-O and Dream Whip advertisement.

## Ingredients

- 1 (6-serving) package Jell-O vanilla pudding and pie filling (cook-type)
- 2½ cups milk
- 1 (9-inch) baked pie shell, cooled
- 1 pint strawberries, halved
- 3 tablespoons currant jelly or other red jelly, melted
- Dream Whip whipped topping, prepared

## Instructions

1. Prepare the pudding with the milk as directed on the package for pie.
2. Pour into the pie shell and chill 3 hours.
3. Arrange the strawberry halves on the filling.
4. Brush the berries lightly with the melted jelly. Chill 30 minutes longer.
5. Garnish with whipped topping.
