# Cream of broccoli soup

## Ingredients

- 1 lb broccoli
- 1 onion
- 2 cups water, divided
- 2 bouillon cubes
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- Cornstarch and milk, to thicken
- 2 tablespoons butter

## Instructions

1. Cut the tender florets off the broccoli and set them aside. Cook the remaining stems until soft enough to blend, discarding the hardest parts.
2. Blend the cooked stems with the onion.
3. Put the blended mixture in a pot with 1 cup water. Rinse the blender with the second cup of water and add that to the pot.
4. Bring to a boil, add the bouillon cubes, and stir until dissolved. Reduce to a simmer.
5. Cut the reserved florets into small pieces and cook them separately for 5 minutes.
6. Stir the evaporated milk into the soup. Mix cornstarch with a little milk and stir it in to thicken, mashing the broccoli as it thickens.
7. Add the florets and finish with the butter.
