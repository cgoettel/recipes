# Carrot and cheese salad

## Ingredients

- 1 lemon, juiced, rind finely grated
- 1 small (8 oz) can crushed pineapple
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup water
- 1 small (3 oz) package lemon Jell-O
- 1 cup grated cheddar cheese
- ½–¾ cup grated carrots
- 1 cup heavy cream, whipped

## Instructions

1. Bring the lemon juice and rind, pineapple, sugar, and water to a boil.
2. Add the Jell-O and stir to dissolve. Cool until thick and syrupy.
3. Add the cheese and carrots.
4. Fold in the whipped cream and chill until firm.
