# Curried duck

## Ingredients

- ½ cup chopped chive
- 1 tablespoon thyme
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 onion or ½ cup chopped onion
- 2-3 leaves chadon beni (cilantro)
- 1 small hot pepper or 2 tablespoons chopped pepper (optional)
- 1 duck, cut in serving pieces (4-5 lb)
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 dash of black pepper
- 1 tablespoon rum
- 6 tablespoons curry powder
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 3 cups hot water

## Instructions

1. Mince chive, thyme, garlic, onion, chadon beni and hot pepper.
2. Blend minced seasoning with duck together with salt, black pepper and rum.
3. Mix curry powder and ½ cup water to a paste.
4. Heat oil in a skillet and add curry paste; cook for a minute until thickened and browned.
5. Add meat and stir to coat well; continue to cook for 15 minutes stirring occasionally until liquid is absorbed.
6. Add about 3 cups of hot water; bring to the boil; lower heat, cover and simmer until meat is tender and most of the liquid is absorbed.
7. Add more water if needed to cook meat.
