# New hybrid method

From [Tetsu Kasuya](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4FeUp_zNiiY).

## Ratios

- 1:15 → 20 g coffee : 300 mL water

## Instructions

Using a Hario Switch:

01. Heat water to 90°C
02. Grind coffee to [865µ](https://onyxcoffeelab.com/pages/grind-size-calculator)
03. Close switch
04. Pour 40–50 g water, or enough to just soak grounds.
05. 0:40: Open switch and pour to 120 g
06. 1:30: Pour to 200 g
07. Lower water temperature to 70–80°C
08. 2:10: close switch and pour to 300 g
09. 2:45: open switch
10. 3:30: target finish
