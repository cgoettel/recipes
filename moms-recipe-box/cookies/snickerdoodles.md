# Snickerdoodles

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- ½ cup shortening
- 1½ cups sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2¾ cups flour
- 2 teaspoons cream of tartar
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ¼ teaspoon salt

### Coating

- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Cream the butter, shortening, and sugar, then beat in the eggs.
3. Stir together the flour, cream of tartar, baking soda, and salt, and mix into the creamed mixture.
4. Shape the dough by rounded teaspoonfuls into balls.
5. Mix the sugar and cinnamon and roll the balls in it.
6. Place 2 inches apart on an ungreased baking sheet.
7. Bake 8–10 minutes. Remove from the baking sheet immediately.
