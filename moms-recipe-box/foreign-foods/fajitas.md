# Fajitas

Serve with refried beans and guacamole. Wrap the meat and vegetables in a tortilla and eat them taco-style.

## Ingredients

### Fajitas

- 1 lb skirt steak, cut across the grain in ¼-inch strips, or cubed boneless, skinless chicken
- 2 tablespoons corn oil, plus 3 tablespoons for cooking
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon seasoned salt
- ½ teaspoon ground oregano
- ½ teaspoon pepper
- ⅛ teaspoon liquid smoke
- 1 cup green pepper strips
- 1 cup thin onion wedges
- 1 cup thin tomato wedges
- ½ cup Rosarita mild chunky taco salsa, plus more for serving
- 8 hot corn or flour tortillas

### Quick and easy guacamole

- 3 large avocados, mashed, pits saved
- 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons Rosarita mild chunky taco salsa
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon seasoned salt

## Instructions

### Fajitas

1. In a medium bowl, combine the meat, 2 tablespoons oil, lemon juice, garlic powder, seasoned salt, oregano, pepper, and liquid smoke. Cover and refrigerate 6–8 hours to marinate.
2. In a 10-inch skillet, heat 3 tablespoons oil over high heat until very hot.
3. Sauté half the meat until it begins to lose its redness, about 30 seconds. Add half the green pepper and onion and cook 1–2 minutes more, until crisp-tender. Remove everything from the skillet.
4. Repeat with the remaining meat, pepper, and onion, adding more oil if needed.
5. Return all the meat, pepper, and onion to the skillet. Add the tomato and ½ cup salsa and simmer, tossing, 1 minute longer.
6. Serve right away with the tortillas and more salsa.

### Quick and easy guacamole

1. In a small bowl, combine all the ingredients and mix well.
2. Set the pits in the guacamole to keep it from browning. Cover and refrigerate about 1 hour to blend the flavors.
3. Remove the pits and serve. Makes 3 cups.

## Notes

- The oil must be very hot to sear the meat quickly and seal in the juices.
