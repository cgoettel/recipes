# Pecan rolls

## Ingredients

- 6 cups sugar
- 1 quart light corn syrup (white Karo)
- 1½ cups evaporated milk
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ⅓ cup butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 lb pecans, chopped

## Instructions

1. Combine the sugar, corn syrup, evaporated milk, salt, and butter and cook to the firm-to-hard ball stage (about 250°F at sea level).
2. Remove from the heat, add the vanilla, and let cool.
3. Beat until it thickens, then stir in some of the pecans.
4. Shape into rolls and roll them in the remaining pecans.

## Notes

- The card says only "cook to hard firm ball"; the temperature is the standard one for that stage.
