# Bacon and cornmeal pancakes

## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups flour
- ¾ cup cornmeal
- ¼ cup sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1⅓ cups milk
- ¼ cup oil
- 1 egg
- ½ lb bacon

## Instructions

1. Combine flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
2. Separately, mix milk, oil, and beaten egg.
3. Combine and whisk. The batter is fine a little lumpy.
4. Chop 5–6 strips of bacon and toss into frying pan. Brown. Add to completed batter.
5. Cook over medium heat until golden brown.
