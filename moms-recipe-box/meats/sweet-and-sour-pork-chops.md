# Sweet and sour pork chops

## Ingredients

- 6 pork chops
- 2 tablespoons shortening
- Cooking spray
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon pepper
- 1 cup carrots, sliced thin
- ½ cup ReaLemon lemon juice
- ¼ cup honey
- ¼ cup onion, chopped
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon beef bouillon granules
- 1 (20 oz) can pineapple chunks, drained, syrup reserved
- 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 6 green pepper rings

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Brown the chops in the shortening.
3. Spray a shallow 3-quart baking dish with cooking spray. Move the chops to the dish and season with the salt and pepper. Add the carrots.
4. In a saucepan, combine the lemon juice, honey, onion, soy sauce, bouillon, and half the pineapple syrup.
5. In a small bowl, mix the remaining pineapple syrup and the cornstarch until smooth; stir into the lemon mixture.
6. Cook, stirring, until thickened. Pour over the chops.
7. Cover and bake 50–60 minutes, or until tender.
8. Add the pineapple chunks and top each chop with a pepper ring. Bake, covered, 10 minutes longer.

## Notes

- Refrigerate leftovers.
