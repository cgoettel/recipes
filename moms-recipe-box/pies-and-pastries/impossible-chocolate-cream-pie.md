# Impossible chocolate cream pie

## Ingredients

### Pie

- 2 eggs
- 1 cup milk
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter, softened
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 (1 oz) squares unsweetened chocolate, melted and cooled
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup Bisquick

### Sweetened whipped cream

- 1 cup chilled whipping cream
- 2 tablespoons sugar

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9×1¼-inch pie plate.
2. Put all the pie ingredients in a blender. Cover and blend on high for 1 minute.
3. Pour into the pie plate and bake about 30 minutes, until no indentation remains when you touch the center lightly.
4. Cool completely.
5. Beat the whipping cream and sugar until stiff and spread over the pie.

## Notes

- High altitude (3,500–6,500 feet): heat the oven to 375°F. Leave out the chocolate and add 6 tablespoons cocoa and 3 tablespoons flour instead. Decrease the Bisquick to ¼ cup.
