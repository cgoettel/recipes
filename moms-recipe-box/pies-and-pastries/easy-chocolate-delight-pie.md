# Easy chocolate delight pie

A frozen German-chocolate cream pie.

## Ingredients

- 2 (4 oz) packages Baker's German's sweet chocolate
- ½ cup milk
- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 2 tablespoons sugar (optional)
- 1 (8 oz) tub Cool Whip, thawed
- 1 (9-inch) chocolate wafer crumb crust

## Instructions

1. Set aside 3 squares of the chocolate. Melt the rest with ¼ cup of the milk over low heat.
2. Beat in the cream cheese, sugar, and remaining milk.
3. Gently stir in the Cool Whip until smooth.
4. Spoon into the crust and freeze until firm.
5. Remove from the freezer and let stand at room temperature about 30 minutes, until the pie can be easily cut.
6. Melt the reserved chocolate (about 15 seconds in the microwave) and drizzle over the pie.
