# No-cook ice cream

## Ingredients

- 6 eggs
- 1½ cups sugar
- 1 quart half-and-half
- 1 cup whipping cream (unwhipped)
- 2 cups milk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla
- Ice and coarse rock salt, for the freezer

## Instructions

1. Beat the eggs well. Add the sugar and beat until it dissolves.
2. Add the half-and-half, cream, milk, and vanilla.
3. Pour into an ice cream freezer. Pack it with layers of ice and coarse salt.
4. Crank until firm.

## Notes

- For variety, add crushed fresh fruit, or 1–2 drops oil of mint (tinted green).
- The eggs go in raw; pasteurized eggs are the safer choice.
