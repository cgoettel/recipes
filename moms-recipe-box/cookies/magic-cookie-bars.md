# Magic cookie bars

From a Borden Eagle Brand sweetened condensed milk advertisement.

## Ingredients

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- 1½ cups graham cracker crumbs
- 1 (14 oz) can Eagle Brand sweetened condensed milk
- 1 (6 oz) package semisweet chocolate chips
- 1 (3.5 oz) can flaked coconut
- 1 cup chopped nuts

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (325°F for a glass dish).
2. Melt the butter in a 9x13-inch baking pan in the oven.
3. Sprinkle the crumbs over the butter. Pour the condensed milk evenly over the crumbs.
4. Top evenly with the chocolate chips, coconut, and nuts, and press down gently.
5. Bake 25–30 minutes, until lightly browned.
6. Cool thoroughly before cutting. Store loosely covered at room temperature.
