# Sausage-zucchini skillet

## Ingredients

- 1 lb sausage
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 3 small zucchini, cut in ½-inch cubes
- 1 (8 oz) can stewed tomatoes
- ½ cup shredded cheese

## Instructions

1. Brown the sausage, breaking it up with a fork. Drain.
2. Add the onion and zucchini and sauté until tender.
3. Add the tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Sprinkle with the cheese.
