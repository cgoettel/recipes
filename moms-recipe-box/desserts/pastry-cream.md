# Pastry cream

## Ingredients

- 6 egg yolks
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup sifted flour
- 2 cups milk, scalded, plus more for thinning
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Instructions

1. Beat the egg yolks in a mixer, gradually adding the sugar, until thick and pale yellow.
2. Beat in the flour.
3. Set aside ½ cup of the hot milk for thinning at the end. Add the rest to the yolks in a thin dribble, beating all the while.
4. Return the mixture to the pot and bring it to a boil over high heat, stirring constantly. It goes lumpy at first, then smooths out; beat it with the mixer if it needs the strength.
5. Once thick, take it off the heat and stir in the vanilla. It will be thick: thin it with the reserved milk to the texture you want.

## Notes

- Pairs with puff pastry. The sheet also notes "canned vanilla creamy white" (canned vanilla frosting) alongside the puff pastry.
