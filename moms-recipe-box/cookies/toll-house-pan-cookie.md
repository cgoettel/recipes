# Toll House pan cookie

From a Nestlé Toll House recipe card: "Summertime… and the bakin' is easy."

## Ingredients

- 2¼ cups flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 sticks (1 cup) butter, softened
- ¾ cup sugar
- ¾ cup brown sugar, firmly packed
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 eggs
- 1 (12 oz) package Nestlé semisweet chocolate chips
- 1 cup chopped nuts

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F and grease a 15x10x1-inch baking pan.
2. In a small bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and salt; set aside.
3. In a large bowl, beat the butter, sugars, and vanilla until creamy. Beat in the eggs.
4. Gradually add the flour mixture and mix well. Stir in the chocolate chips and nuts.
5. Spread in the pan and bake 20 minutes.
6. Cool, then cut into 2-inch squares.

## Notes

- For a half batch (one 6 oz package of chips), spread into a greased 9x13x2-inch pan and bake 12–15 minutes. Makes about 24 squares.
