# Bran muffins

A big refrigerator batter: bake a few muffins at a time for up to 6 weeks.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups Nabisco 100% Bran cereal
- 2 cups boiling water
- 1 cup shortening
- 2½ cups sugar
- 4 eggs
- 1½ teaspoons salt
- 5 cups flour
- 4 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 quart buttermilk
- 4 cups Kellogg's All-Bran cereal

## Instructions

1. Mix the Nabisco bran cereal with the boiling water and let it stand.
2. Cream the shortening, then add the sugar.
3. Add the eggs, salt, flour, baking soda, and buttermilk, then the soaked Nabisco bran.
4. Fold in the Kellogg's All-Bran just until moistened.
5. Store the batter covered in the refrigerator for up to 6 weeks. Do not stir it.
6. To bake, preheat the oven to 375°F, spoon the batter into greased muffin tins, and bake for about 20 minutes.
