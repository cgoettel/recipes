# Holiday cheese cones

Cheese spreads shaped like pine cones for a party board. Serve with fruit and crackers.

## Ingredients

### Parmesan cone (makes 1¾ cups)

- 12 oz cream cheese, softened
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- ¼ cup mayonnaise
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano leaves, crushed
- ⅛ teaspoon garlic powder

### Cheddar cone (makes 2 cups)

- 4 cups (1 lb) finely shredded cheddar cheese
- ⅔ cup mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons horseradish
- Dash of Tabasco
- Salt and pepper

### Garnish

- Nut halves (such as pecans or almonds)

## Instructions

1. For either cone, combine all its ingredients and chill.
2. Shape the chilled mixture on a serving board to resemble a pine cone.
3. Garnish with nut halves pressed in like scales. Serve with fruit and crackers.
