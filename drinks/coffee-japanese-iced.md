# Japanese iced coffee

From [Black Tie Kitchen](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AdR5wZVH-H4&t=157) on YouTube

## Ingredients

- 30 g coffee beans, finely ground (similar to kosher salt)
- 250 g ice
- Kettle set to 96°C (205°F)

## Instructions

01. Put the kettle on to 96°C (205°F)
02. Grind the coffee beans. You will want a grind that is similar in size to kosher salt.
03. Pre-wet the filter
04. Add ice to the decanter
05. Put the decanter and pour over on the scale and tare
06. Add the coffee grounds
07. Begin by pouring in twice the amount of water as coffee grounds (30 g * 2 = 60 g water)
08. Allow to bloom for 45 seconds
09. _Slowly_ add water until 275 g have been added
10. Swirl to evenly cool
11. Ice and enjoy
