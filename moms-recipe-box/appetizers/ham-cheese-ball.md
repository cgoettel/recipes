# Ham cheese ball

## Ingredients

- 3 (8 oz) packages cream cheese, softened
- 1 package ham, diced
- 1–2 teaspoons seasoning salt
- 1 cup shredded Longhorn cheese

## Instructions

1. Mix everything together and shape into a ball.
2. Refrigerate for 24 hours before serving.
