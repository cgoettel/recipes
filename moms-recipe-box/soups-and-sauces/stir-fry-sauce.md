# Stir-fry sauce

A make-ahead sauce base, enough for three or four main dishes.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup dark corn syrup
- ½ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup cornstarch
- ¼ cup white vinegar
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 cups beef broth
- Dry sherry, for cooking (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a quart jar, stir the corn syrup, soy sauce, and cornstarch together until smooth.
2. Stir in the vinegar, garlic powder, and beef broth.
3. Add water to make 3⅔ cups. Cover the jar and store it in the refrigerator.
4. To use, shake well, marinate the meat in some of the sauce for 30 minutes, then stir-fry and add more sauce to the pan, cooking until the meat is glazed.

## Notes

- The card also calls for a splash of dry sherry with the meat.
