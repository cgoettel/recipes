# Shrimp dalkani

## Ingredients

- 1 lb large shrimp
- 1 tablespoon green seasoning
- ½ cup ground split peas or chick peas
- 1 tablespoon flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon geera
- ½ teaspoon garlic
- ½ teaspoon pepper
- ½ cup water (approx.)

## Instructions

1. Clean shrimp and season with green seasoning, steam for 5 minutes.
2. Combine ground split peas, flour, baking powder, salt, geera, garlic and pepper with water to make a batter.
3. Dip shrimp into batter and deep fry a few at a time.
