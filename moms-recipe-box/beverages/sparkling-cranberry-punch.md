makes: 25 (5 oz) servings

# Sparkling cranberry punch

## Ingredients

- 2 (16 oz) cans jellied cranberry sauce
- 1½ cups orange juice
- ½ cup lemon juice
- 2 (28 oz) bottles ginger ale (7 cups), chilled
- Ice cubes

## Instructions

1. Beat the cranberry sauce until smooth.
2. Stir in the orange and lemon juices.
3. Pour the mixture over ice cubes in a punch bowl.
4. Resting the bottle on the rim of the bowl, carefully pour in the ginger ale so it keeps its fizz.
