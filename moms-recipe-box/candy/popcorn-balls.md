# Popcorn balls

## Ingredients

- 1 cup light corn syrup
- 1½ cups sugar
- 1 (3 oz) package Jell-O (red, green, or orange)
- 9 cups popped popcorn

## Instructions

1. Combine the corn syrup and sugar in a saucepan and bring to a boil.
2. Remove from the heat, add the Jell-O, and stir until dissolved.
3. Pour over the popcorn, mix well, and shape into balls.
