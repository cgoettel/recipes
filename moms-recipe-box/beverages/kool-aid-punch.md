# Kool-Aid punch

## Ingredients

- 1 package unsweetened cherry Kool-Aid
- 1 package unsweetened orange Kool-Aid
- 1 cup pineapple juice
- 1 (6 oz) can frozen orange juice concentrate
- 1 banana
- Sugar, to taste
- Water
- 5 (12 oz) cans ginger ale, chilled

## Instructions

1. Mash the banana or purée it in a blender.
2. Stir together the Kool-Aid, pineapple juice, orange juice concentrate, and banana. Add sugar to taste.
3. Add water to make 1 gallon. Chill.
4. Just before serving, pour into a punch bowl and add the ginger ale.

## Notes

- Use unsweetened Kool-Aid; you control the sugar.
