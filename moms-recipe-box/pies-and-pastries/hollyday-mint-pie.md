# Hollyday mint pie

A Christmas pie of swirled white and green mint cream, decorated with holly made from candies. From a Keebler advertisement.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup crushed Keebler Grasshopper fudge mint cookies
- 3 tablespoons hot water
- 1 (6 oz) Keebler Ready-Crust graham cracker pie crust
- 1 (3 oz) package Philadelphia cream cheese, softened
- ⅓ cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons milk
- ¼ teaspoon peppermint extract
- 3½ cups (8 oz) Cool Whip, thawed
- 6–10 drops green food coloring
- Red hot cinnamon candies and spearmint leaves, for decorating

## Instructions

1. Mix the cookie crumbs and hot water. Spoon into the crust and gently spread to cover the bottom.
2. Beat the cream cheese until smooth; gradually beat in the sugar, milk, and peppermint extract.
3. Fold in the whipped topping. Divide the mixture in half and stir the food coloring into one half until evenly colored.
4. Fill the crust with alternating dollops of the white and green mixtures. Smooth the top with a spatula.
5. Chill 3 hours.
6. Make holly berries and leaves on top with the cinnamon candies and spearmint leaves.
