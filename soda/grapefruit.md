# Grapefruit soda

This recipe comes from [Glen and Friends Cooking](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xJc_q1ZNv48).

## Ingredients

### Syrup

- 680 g cane sugar
- 21 g citric acid
- zest and juice of 4 grapefruits (TODO weigh zest; juice should yield 400&ndash;500 mL)
- 1 L water

## Method

- In a heatproof container, mix together sugar, citric acid, and grapefruit zest (need to measure amount; Glen says to use 3 grapefruit's worth; I think I've previously used 4; need a weight here)
- Boil 1 L water and add to container
- Allow container to cool to room temperature
- Juice the grapefruits
- Mix the juice into the syrup. Allow to stand for 24 hours in a cool place.
- Mix 1 part syrup to 3 parts water and force carbonate. NOTE: I feel like previously I was doing 125 mL syrup to (3*125=375 mL) water and that was a good ratio. Need to try that before making an entire keg.
- Carbonate and serve at 30 PSI
