# Coffee Syrup

A coffee concentrate sweetened and bodied with rich demerara — built for the
Sleepwalker (Paul Ramirez, We Got Company, Brooklyn). The demerara rounds the
coffee's edges and gives the syrup enough body to pour like a sweetener.

## Ingredients

- 330 g coffee concentrate (cold brew concentrate works)
- 140 g [rich demerara syrup](demerara-rich-simple-syrup.md)

## Instructions

1. Stir the demerara syrup into the coffee concentrate until fully combined.
2. Store chilled.
