# Chocolate drop cookies

## Ingredients

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1½ cups flour
- ½ cup cocoa
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- ½ cup milk
- ⅓ cup chopped nuts

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Cream the butter and sugar together. Add the egg and beat it in.
3. Sift together the flour, cocoa, salt, and baking soda.
4. Add the dry ingredients, vanilla, milk, and nuts and blend well.
5. Drop onto an ungreased cookie sheet and bake 8–10 minutes.
