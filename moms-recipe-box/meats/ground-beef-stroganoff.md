# Ground beef stroganoff

## Ingredients

- 1 medium onion, chopped
- Butter, for sautéing
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 (8 oz) package mushrooms, sliced
- 1 tablespoon flour
- 1 (10.5 oz) can cream of chicken soup
- 1 (8 oz) container sour cream
- Chopped parsley, for topping

## Instructions

1. Sauté the onion in butter until soft.
2. Add the ground beef and brown it.
3. Add the sliced mushrooms and cook 10 minutes, uncovered.
4. Stir in the flour, then the soup, thinned with a little water, and cook 10 minutes more, uncovered.
5. Stir in the sour cream and heat through without boiling.
6. Top with chopped parsley.

## Notes

- Serve over egg noodles or rice.
