# Lasagna

From the back of a Lady Lee lasagna noodle box.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz lasagna noodles
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 clove garlic, chopped
- 2 (6 oz) cans tomato paste
- 2 cups water
- Parsley
- Salt and pepper
- 2 bay leaves
- ¾ lb mozzarella or Monterey Jack cheese
- ½ lb ricotta or small-curd cottage cheese
- Grated Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1. For the sauce, brown the ground beef in the oil. Add the onion, garlic, tomato paste diluted with the water, parsley, salt, pepper, and bay leaves. Simmer 30 minutes, then remove the bay leaves.
2. Bring 4 quarts water and 2 tablespoons salt to a rapid boil. Add the noodles without breaking them, stir to separate, and boil 10 minutes. Drain and rinse with cold water to keep them from sticking.
3. Preheat the oven to 375°F and butter a baking dish.
4. Layer the noodles in the dish, alternating with layers of sauce, mozzarella, and ricotta. Sprinkle with Parmesan.
5. Bake about 20 minutes, until the mozzarella is melted. Serve hot.
