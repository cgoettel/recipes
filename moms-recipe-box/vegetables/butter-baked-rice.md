# Butter baked rice

## Ingredients

- 2 cups water
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 cup Uncle Ben's converted rice
- ¾ stick (6 tablespoons) butter
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 big dash garlic powder
- 1¾ cups chicken broth
- Chopped parsley, for topping

## Instructions

1. Bring the water and salt to a boil and add the rice. Remove from the heat and let stand 30 minutes.
2. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
3. Rinse the rice with cold water and drain.
4. Melt the butter in a skillet, add the rice and onion, and stir-fry 5 minutes.
5. Turn the rice into a casserole dish and add the garlic and chicken broth.
6. Cover and bake 45 minutes.
7. Sprinkle with parsley and bake uncovered 10 minutes more.
