# Swirled herb rolls

## Ingredients

- Dough for a 12×14-inch rectangle (bread or roll dough, or refrigerated crescent dough)
- 2 tablespoons butter, melted
- 2½ tablespoons chopped chives
- 1 tablespoon minced parsley
- 1 tablespoon lemon thyme
- 1 tablespoon basil
- ¼ cup shredded Parmesan cheese
- ¼ teaspoon pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a baking sheet.
2. Roll the dough into a 12×14-inch rectangle.
3. Brush with the melted butter and sprinkle evenly with the chives, parsley, lemon thyme, basil, Parmesan, and pepper.
4. Roll up from a long side, pinch the seam closed, and cut into slices about 1 inch thick. Set them cut side down on the baking sheet.
5. Bake for 10–15 minutes, until golden.
