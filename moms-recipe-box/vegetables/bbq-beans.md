# Barbecue beans for a crowd

## Ingredients

- 2 (#10) cans pork and beans, or about 16 (16 oz) cans
- 2 cups brown sugar
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 cups ketchup
- 6 tablespoons prepared mustard
- 8–10 strips bacon, chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Mix everything together in a large roasting pan or several baking dishes.
3. Bake for 30–45 minutes.
