# Coleslaw dressing

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup vinegar
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon celery seed
- Miracle Whip

## Instructions

1. Stir the vinegar, sugar, and celery seed together until the sugar dissolves.
2. Whisk in Miracle Whip until the dressing is the consistency of salad dressing.

## Notes

- Keep the vinegar and sugar equal, about ¼ cup each, and scale from there.
