# Balsamic soy marinade

## Ingredients

- ½ cup Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 teaspoon garlic, minced
- ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
- ½ teaspoon pepper
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper

## Instructions

1. Whisk everything together.
2. Pour over the meat in a zip-top bag or shallow dish, turn to coat, and refrigerate several hours or overnight, turning once or twice.

## Notes

- The card doesn't say what it's for; it reads like a steak or tri-tip marinade.
