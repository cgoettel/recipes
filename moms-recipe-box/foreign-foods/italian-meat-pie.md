# Italian meat pie

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- ⅓ cup green pepper, chopped
- ¾ cup water
- 1 (6 oz) can tomato paste
- 1 (1.5 oz) package spaghetti sauce mix
- 1 unbaked pie crust
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan, divided
- 1½ cups shredded mozzarella, divided

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Brown the beef in a skillet and drain. Add the green pepper and cook 2 minutes.
3. Stir in the water, tomato paste, and sauce mix. Cover and simmer 10 minutes.
4. Sprinkle half the Parmesan over the bottom of the pie shell. Spread half the meat mixture in the shell and sprinkle with 1 cup mozzarella.
5. Layer on the remaining meat and Parmesan.
6. Bake 15 minutes. Sprinkle with the remaining mozzarella and return to the oven until the cheese melts.
