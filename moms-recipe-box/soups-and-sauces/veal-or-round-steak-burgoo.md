# Veal or round steak burgoo

## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless veal or round steak, in cubes
- 3 tablespoons cooking oil
- ¼ cup flour
- 2 tablespoons prepared mustard
- 1½ teaspoons salt
- 1 (18 oz) can (2¼ cups) tomato juice
- ½ cup water
- 6 small carrots
- 12 pearl onions
- 2 cups sliced fresh mushrooms
- Buttered noodles, for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Brown the meat in the hot oil. Remove the meat to a 3-quart casserole.
3. Stir the flour, mustard, and salt into the meat juices in the skillet. Add the tomato juice and water and cook, stirring, until thickened and bubbly.
4. Pour over the meat in the casserole. Cover and bake 1 hour.
5. Cut the carrots in half crosswise, then in quarters lengthwise to form sticks.
6. Add the carrots, mushrooms, and onions to the stew. Bake, covered, 45–60 minutes more, until the meat and vegetables are tender.
7. Stir before serving. Serve over buttered noodles.
