# Garden tomato sauce

A long-simmered tomato sauce for bottling, thickened with puréed zucchini, peppers, and onion.

## Ingredients

- Fresh tomatoes, enough to fill a 9×13-inch pan with strained pulp (repeat as needed)
- 1 cup tomato juice
- 2 large zucchini
- 2 large green bell peppers
- 2 large onions
- 3 cloves garlic, pressed
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice per quart jar
- 1 teaspoon salt per quart jar

## Instructions

1. Pull the stems off the tomatoes and bring them to a boil, taking them off before the skins crack.
2. Run the tomatoes through a food strainer (such as a Victorio) to remove skins and seeds. Each time a 9×13-inch pan fills with pulp, transfer it to a large pot.
3. In a food processor or blender, purée the tomato juice, zucchini, green peppers, onions, and garlic. Add to the tomato pulp in the pot.
4. Cook over medium heat until it comes to a good boil, then turn to low.
5. Simmer until it cooks down to a paste-like thickness, 3–5 hours, stirring every 20 minutes.
6. Pour into glass quart jars, adding 2 tablespoons lemon juice and 1 teaspoon salt to each quart.

## Notes

- The card gives no processing time. Process the jars according to current tested canning guidance for tomato sauce with added low-acid vegetables.
