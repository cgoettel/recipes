# Pizza pasta pie

A spaghetti crust layered with spinach, cheese, and pizza sauce. From a 1986 Tupperware "New Traditions" recipe card.

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon toasted wheat germ
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 3 oz spaghetti, cooked and drained (still hot)
- 2 eggs
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- ⅛ teaspoon salt
- 1 (10 oz) package frozen chopped spinach, thawed and well drained
- ¾ cup cream-style cottage cheese
- 1 (8 oz) can pizza sauce
- ½ cup finely shredded carrot
- 1 (2.5 oz) jar mushroom stems and pieces, drained and chopped
- ½ teaspoon dried basil, crushed
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano, crushed
- Dash garlic powder
- ½ cup shredded mozzarella cheese

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a ¾-quart baking dish or a small pie plate and sprinkle it with the wheat germ.
2. Stir the butter into the hot spaghetti. Beat 1 of the eggs and add it to the spaghetti with half the Parmesan and the salt. Mix well.
3. Press the spaghetti mixture into a crust in the prepared dish.
4. Squeeze the excess liquid from the spinach with paper towels and spread it over the pasta crust.
5. Drain the cottage cheese. Beat the remaining egg and stir in the cottage cheese and the rest of the Parmesan. Spoon over the spinach.
6. Combine the pizza sauce, carrot, mushrooms, basil, oregano, and garlic powder. Spoon over the cottage cheese layer.
7. Bake uncovered for 30–35 minutes, until heated through.
8. Top with the mozzarella and let stand 10 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- To microwave: cook uncovered on 50% power for 18–22 minutes, until heated through, giving the dish a quarter turn every 5 minutes. Top with the mozzarella and let stand 10 minutes.
