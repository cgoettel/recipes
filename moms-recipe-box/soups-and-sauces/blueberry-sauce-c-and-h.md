# Blueberry sauce

For pancakes, waffles, ice cream, parfaits, and puddings.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup C&H granulated cane sugar
- 1 pint fresh blueberries (or 2 cups thawed frozen blueberries), plus more for topping
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- ¼ cup cold water

## Instructions

1. In a medium saucepan, combine the sugar, blueberries, and lemon juice. Stir over medium heat until the sugar dissolves.
2. Stir the cornstarch into the cold water, add it to the pan, and cook, stirring, until thickened.
3. Chill before serving. Top with more blueberries if you like.
