# Upholstery shampoo

## Ingredients

- A general-purpose household detergent
- Water

## Instructions

1. Mix the detergent in water and whip the solution to a froth.
2. Skim off the bubbles (don't use the liquid) and apply them evenly to the fabric.
3. Wipe off. Since the froth is damp rather than wet, it cleans without soaking the upholstery.
