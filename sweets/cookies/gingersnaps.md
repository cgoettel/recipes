# King Arthur gingernsaps

## Ingredients

### Cookies

- ½C butter (shortening can be used for hard cookies)
- 1C sugar
- ½t salt
- 2t baking soda
- 1 egg
- ⅓C molasses
- 2⅓C flour
- 1–2t ground ginger
- ½t ground cloves
- 1t cinnamon

### Coating

- ¼C sugar
- 1t ground cinnamon

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 375°F
- Beat butter and sugar. Add sugar, salt, and baking soda.
- Beat in egg and molasses.
- Add flour and spices.
- Combine coating ingredients.
- Make balls and add cover in coating.
- Place on baking sheet.
- Bake for 11+ minutes.
