# Apple Cider Syrup

Reduced apple juice — no added sugar, the syrup is all concentrated fruit. The cinnamon stick rides along during the reduction and the pinch of salt at the end keeps it from reading flat-sweet.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups unfiltered apple juice
- 1 cinnamon stick
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Put the juice and cinnamon stick in a pot and bring to a boil.
2. Reduce by about 75% — down to roughly 1 cup, 60–90 minutes.
3. Remove the cinnamon stick and add a pinch of salt.
4. Strain and store chilled.
