# Iced tea

## Ingredients

- 1 teaspoon loose leaf tea for every cup of water
- Water

## Instructions

1. For a 2 L batch, use 8.5 teaspoons of loose leaf tea.
2. Put a lid on the container and put in the fridge overnight or for at least 12 hours.
3. Strain the liquid through a fine mesh sieve into another 2 L jug.
4. Ice and enjoy.
