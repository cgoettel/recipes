# Cherry crunch

## Ingredients

- 1 (15.25 oz) package white cake mix
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- 2 (20 oz) cans cherry pie filling
- ½ cup chopped walnuts
- Whipped cream or ice cream, for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Cut the butter into the dry cake mix. Set aside ½ cup of the mixture.
3. Pat the rest into a 9×13×2-inch pan, building it up ½ inch around the edge.
4. Spread the pie filling over the cake mixture to within ½ inch of the pan edge.
5. Blend the walnuts into the reserved mixture and sprinkle it over the top.
6. Bake 45 minutes. Serve warm or cold with whipped cream or ice cream.
