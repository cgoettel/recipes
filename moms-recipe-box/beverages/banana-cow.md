# Banana cow

## Ingredients

- 1 medium banana, peeled and cut into chunks
- ½ cup milk
- 1 tablespoon honey
- ⅛ teaspoon vanilla
- 1 cup crushed ice
- Lime wedges, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Put the banana, milk, honey, vanilla, and crushed ice in a blender and blend until smooth.
2. Pour into glasses and garnish with lime wedges.
