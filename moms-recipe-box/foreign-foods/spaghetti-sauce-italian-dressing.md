# Spaghetti sauce with Italian dressing mix

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 large (29 oz) can tomato sauce
- 1 envelope Good Seasons Italian dressing mix
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- Sliced mushrooms (optional)
- Spaghetti, for serving

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef and drain.
2. Add the tomato sauce, dressing mix, and sugar.
3. Simmer while the spaghetti cooks. Add mushrooms if you like.

## Notes

- Good as lasagna sauce.
- Make the sauce without meat, pour it over chicken, and bake.
- Add pinto and kidney beans and chili powder to make chili.
