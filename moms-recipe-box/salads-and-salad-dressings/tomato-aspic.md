# Tomato aspic

## Ingredients

### Aspic

- 1 large (6 oz) package lemon Jell-O
- 2½ cups hot water
- 2 (8 oz) cans tomato sauce
- 2 tablespoons vinegar
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon Lawry's seasoned salt
- 2 tablespoons red shrimp cocktail sauce
- ½ cup chopped celery

### Dressing

- Whipped cream
- Mayonnaise

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the gelatin in the hot water.
2. Add the tomato sauce, vinegar, seasonings, and celery.
3. Pour into a ring mold and chill until set.
4. For the dressing, mix equal parts whipped cream and mayonnaise. Put a dollop on each serving.
