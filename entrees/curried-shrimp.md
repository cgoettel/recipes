# Curried Shrimp

## Serves

8

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs. shrimp (shelled)
- 1 tsp. minced garlic
- 1/4 cup finely chopped onion
- 2 tbsp. green seasoning
- 2 tbsp. curry powder or more
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 tbsp. vegetable oil
- 1 tsp. salt
- hot pepper to taste

## Method

1. Season shrimp with garlic, onion and green seasoning.
2. Mix curry powder with 1/4 cup water.
3. Heat oil and add curry paste and sauté for 1 minute.
4. Add shrimp, salt, hot pepper, and stir to coat shrimp for a minute or two.
5. Add remaining water and cook on high heat for about 3 minutes. Do not overcook as shrimp will be tough.
6. Adjust salt and pepper.

## Variation

### Shrimp Biryani

Substitute 1 lb. medium-size shrimp for meat. Cook for only 3-4 minutes.
