# Mumble-rumble berry pie

A mixed-berry pie.

## Ingredients

- Pastry for a 9-inch two-crust pie
- 1–1½ cups sugar
- ⅓ cup flour
- ½ teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 cup blueberries
- 1 cup raspberries
- 2 cups blackberries or boysenberries
- 1½ tablespoons butter

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Mix the sugar, flour, and cinnamon and toss lightly through the berries.
3. Pour the berries into the pastry-lined pie plate and dot with the butter.
4. Cover with the top crust, seal and flute the edge, and cut a few slits for steam.
5. Bake 35–45 minutes, until the crust is golden and the juices bubble.
