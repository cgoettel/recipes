# Latkes

From a newspaper page of traditional latkes and contemporary potato pancakes.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb Idaho baking potatoes
- 1 medium onion
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons flour or matzo meal
- ¼ teaspoon baking powder
- Salt and fresh black pepper
- Vegetable oil, for frying
- Applesauce and sour cream, for serving

## Instructions

1. Peel the potatoes and finely grate them by hand or in a food processor.
2. Rinse the grated potatoes in a bowl of cold water and drain. Keep changing the water and rinsing until all the starch is gone and the water runs clear.
3. Drain the potatoes in a colander, then squeeze small handfuls in a dish towel to wring out all the water. Meanwhile, grate the onion.
4. Beat the egg in a bowl and gradually mix in the potatoes, onion, and remaining ingredients except the oil.
5. Heat ½ inch of oil to 375°F in an electric frying pan. Drop in large blobs of the potato mixture, flattening them with a soup spoon into 3-inch pancakes.
6. Fry 2–3 minutes per side, until golden brown. Drain on paper towels and serve at once with applesauce and sour cream.
