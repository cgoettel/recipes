# Swedish meatballs

## Ingredients

- ½ lb lean ground beef
- ½ lb ground pork
- 1½ cups bread crumbs
- 1½ cups milk
- 1 egg
- 3 tablespoons chopped onion
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 2 teaspoons salt
- ¼ teaspoon white pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Cook the onion in the butter until soft.
3. Soak the bread crumbs in the milk, then mix in the egg, onion, salt, white pepper, beef, and pork.
4. Shape into balls and set them on a baking sheet.
5. Bake until cooked through, about 20–25 minutes.
