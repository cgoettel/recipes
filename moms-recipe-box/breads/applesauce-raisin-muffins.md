# Applesauce raisin muffins

Very soft and spicy, with no added sugar. Delicious topped with cream cheese.

## Ingredients

- 1 large egg
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1½ cups unsweetened applesauce
- 2 cups unbleached white flour
- ¾ teaspoon baking soda
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon nutmeg
- ½ teaspoon cinnamon
- ¾ cup raisins

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Oil and flour a muffin tin.
2. Beat together the egg, oil, and applesauce.
3. Add the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and spices, and beat well.
4. Stir in the raisins.
5. Spoon the batter into the muffin cups and bake for 20–25 minutes, until firm to the touch and browned.
6. Cool on wire racks.
