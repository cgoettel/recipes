# Autumn pudding

An apple crumble baked in a soufflé dish.

## Ingredients

- 3 lb apples
- Fresh lemon juice, if the apples are dry
- 1 cup brown or white sugar
- 1 cup flour
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- Dash of cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Butter a baking dish (a soufflé dish works well).
2. Peel and core the apples and slice them into the dish. If they seem dry, toss them with a little fresh lemon juice.
3. Crumble the sugar, flour, butter, and cinnamon together and mound the topping over the apples.
4. Bake 1 hour. Serve as is or with ice cream.
