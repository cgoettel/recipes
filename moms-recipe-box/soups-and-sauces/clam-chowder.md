# Clam chowder

## Ingredients

- 1 cup chopped onion
- 1 cup celery, cut small
- 2 cups diced potatoes
- 2 (6.5 oz) cans clams, juice reserved
- ¾ cup flour
- 1½ teaspoons salt
- ½ teaspoon sugar
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter
- 1 quart half-and-half

## Instructions

1. Put the onion, celery, and potatoes in a pot with the clam juice and barely cover with water. Simmer about 10 minutes, until just done.
2. Combine the salt, sugar, and flour (add other seasonings if you like). Melt the butter in a large pot, stir in the flour mixture, and cook 2 minutes, stirring.
3. Add the half-and-half and stir constantly until thickened.
4. Pour in the vegetables with their liquid and the clams. Heat through.

## Notes

- Add more milk if you want it thinner.
