# Jan's cookies

From Aunt Jan

## Ingredients

- 1 cup margarine, soft
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1½ cups sugar
- 1 tablespoon milk
- 1½ teaspoons vanilla
- 2 eggs
- 1½ cups flour
- 1¼ teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon mace
- 1½ teaspoons cinnamon
- ¼ teaspoon nutmeg
- ⅛ teaspoon powdered cloves
- 1 cup corn flakes
- 3 cups oats
- 4oz coconut
- 1 package chocolate chips
- 1 cup nuts

## Instructions

1. Cream margarine, brown sugar, sugar, milk, vanilla, and eggs until smooth
2. Mix rest of the ingredients in
3. Bake at 350°F for 10 minutes.
