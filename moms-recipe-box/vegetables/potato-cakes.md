# Potato cakes

A way to use up leftover mashed potatoes.

## Ingredients

- Leftover mashed potatoes
- 1 egg
- 1 heaping teaspoon sugar
- Salt, to taste
- Cottage cheese, a spoonful or two
- Flour, enough to make the mixture stiff
- Butter or oil, for frying

## Instructions

1. Mix the egg, sugar, salt, and cottage cheese into the mashed potatoes.
2. Add enough flour to make the mixture stiff enough to shape.
3. Shape into patties and fry in butter or oil over medium heat until golden brown on both sides.

## Notes

- Add the cottage cheese before the flour.
