# Chocolate fondue

## Ingredients

- 1 (12 oz) package chocolate chips
- 3 tablespoons fresh cream
- ½ teaspoon Pero (or instant coffee)
- 1 pinch cinnamon
- 2 tablespoons honey

### For dipping

- Candied cherries
- Strawberries
- Apple wedges
- Pound cake cubes
- Ladyfingers
- Marshmallows
- Angel food cake, in bite-size pieces

## Instructions

1. Melt and stir everything together in a fondue pot slowly over low heat.
2. Serve with the dippers.

## Notes

- Pero is a caffeine-free roasted-grain coffee substitute.
