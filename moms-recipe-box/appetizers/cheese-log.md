# Cheese log

## Ingredients

- 2 (8 oz) packages cream cheese, softened at room temperature
- 2 cups shredded cheese (cheddar, Gouda, or your favorite)
- 1 (8 oz) can crushed pineapple, drained
- ¼ cup bacon bits
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- ⅓ cup chopped walnuts

## Instructions

1. Mix all the ingredients together with a blender.
2. Refrigerate until firm.
3. Form into a ball, or a long log for easy slicing.
4. Serve with crackers.
