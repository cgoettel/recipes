# Fruit combo

## Ingredients

- 1 (20 oz) can pineapple chunks
- 1 quart peaches or pears, canned
- 2–3 bananas
- 1–2 oranges
- 1 bunch grapes
- Raspberries or strawberries
- 7UP (optional)

## Instructions

1. Drain the pineapple and the peaches or pears, saving the juice.
2. Cut the fruit into bite-size pieces and combine it in a bowl.
3. Pour the juice from the peaches or pears and the pineapple over the fruit.
4. Add a splash of 7UP, if you like.
