# Lemonade pie

A frozen pie and a great summer dessert.

## Ingredients

- 1 graham cracker crust
- ½ gallon vanilla ice cream (or ice milk), softened
- 1 (6 oz) can frozen pink lemonade concentrate, thawed

## Instructions

1. Mix the ice cream with the lemonade concentrate.
2. Pour into the crust and swirl the top.
3. Freeze until firm.
