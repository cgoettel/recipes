# Persimmon delight

A steamed-style persimmon pudding, baked in a water bath.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons butter, softened
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup persimmon pulp
- 1½ cups milk
- 1 cup flour
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1½ teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
- ½ cup raisins
- ½ cup chopped nuts

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and butter an 8-inch square pan.
2. In a large bowl, cream the butter and sugar together, then mix in the persimmon pulp until blended.
3. Sift the flour, measure it, and sift it again with the baking soda and pumpkin pie spice.
4. Add the flour mixture to the persimmon mixture alternately with the milk.
5. Stir in the raisins and nuts and spread the batter in the pan.
6. Set the pan in a larger pan of hot water and bake for 1 hour 15 minutes.
7. Serve with whipped cream or your favorite hard sauce.

## Notes

- Hachiya persimmons make a moister, more pudding-like dessert; Fuyu persimmons make it more cake-like. Either way, the fruit should be soft and very ripe when you purée it.
