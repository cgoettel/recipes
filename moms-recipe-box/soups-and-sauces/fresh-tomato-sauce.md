# Fresh tomato sauce

A no-cook sauce for pasta or sandwiches.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb very ripe tomatoes (about 4 large), seeded and chopped
- ½ small onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 1 small bunch fresh basil, cut into strips (about 1 cup)
- ⅔ cup best-quality olive oil
- 8 oz Brie, rind removed, cut into chunks (optional)
- Salt and freshly ground pepper

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, mix all the ingredients, seasoning with salt and pepper to taste.
2. Let sit, covered, at room temperature for a couple of hours.
3. Serve over 1 lb cooked pasta or as a filling for sandwiches.
