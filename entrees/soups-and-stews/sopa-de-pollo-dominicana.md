# Sopa de Pollo Dominicana

From [Dominican Flavor](http://dominicanflavor.com/soupsstews/dominican-style-chicken-soupsopa-de-pollo-dominicana.html). Serves 5–6 in hearty portions.

## Ingredients

- 3–4 lbs chicken, skinless and bone-in (~8–10 pieces — thighs and drumsticks preferred)
- 2 t ground oregano
- 2–3 t salt
- ½ t ground black pepper
- 5 cloves garlic
- 3–4 habanero chiles (ají gustoso)
- 2 limes
- 2 T [achiote oil](http://dominicanflavor.com/miscellaneous/achiote-oilaceite-de-bija.html) (or 1 t ground achiote)
- 3 celery stalks
- 4 carrots
- 4 large potatoes
- 1 lb cassava (yucca)
- 1 C fresh cilantro
- 2 C angel hair noodles (fideos)
- 4 C chicken stock (optional)
- Water
- ½ C vinegar (or extra lime juice), for washing the chicken
- 2 T vegetable or olive oil, for cooking

## Instructions

01. Rinse the chicken, then soak in water with the vinegar (or lime) for a few minutes. Rinse and drain.
02. Season the chicken: salt, black pepper, oregano, mashed or chopped garlic, achiote oil. Seed and finely chop the habaneros and add them. Add the juice of 2 limes. Mix well. Marinate at least 1 hour — overnight is better.
03. In a large pot, heat 2 T oil over high heat. Add the chicken pieces one by one (set the marinade liquid aside), tongs help avoid splatter. Cook ~10 minutes.
04. While the chicken cooks, fill a separate pot with water (~3 quarts) and bring to a boil — you'll add it later.
05. Flip the chicken and half-cover the pot. Let it cook in its juices, stirring occasionally so it doesn't stick.
06. Cut the celery and peeled carrots into bite-sized pieces. Set aside.
07. After ~25–30 minutes total, when the released juices have reduced and the chicken is frying in the oil, let it go a few more minutes to develop color.
08. Add the reserved marinade. Gently stir in the celery and carrots (use a spatula so the chicken doesn't break apart). Cook ~5 minutes.
09. Add the hot water (~2½–3 quarts). Taste and adjust salt.
10. Peel and cube the potatoes and cassava (remove the root from the cassava). Add to the pot.
11. Tie the cilantro into a bundle with kitchen twine or a rubber band so it can be removed easily. Add to the pot.
12. Add the angel hair noodles. If the soup is getting too thick, add chicken stock or bouillon dissolved in water to thin.
13. Boil 15–20 minutes until potatoes, cassava, and noodles are fork-tender but not falling apart. Skim grease from the top with a wide spoon while it boils.
14. Turn off heat and keep covered until serving.

## Notes

- Serve with lime wedges and fluffy white rice.
