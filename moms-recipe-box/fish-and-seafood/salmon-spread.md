# Salmon spread

Adapted from *The Silver Palate Good Times Cookbook*.

## Ingredients

- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, at room temperature
- ¼ cup heavy or whipping cream
- 1 green onion (white part and 3 inches of green), thinly sliced
- 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice
- 1 dash Tabasco
- 4 oz smoked salmon, gently shredded
- 2 tablespoons red salmon caviar (optional)

## Instructions

1. Gently mix the cream cheese and cream in a mixing bowl.
2. Stir in the green onion, lemon juice, and Tabasco.
3. Gently fold in the smoked salmon and caviar until well combined, but don't overmix: the shreds of salmon and the caviar should stay whole.

## Notes

- The caviar can be left out; the handwritten card version skips it and the lemon juice.
- You can use less smoked salmon, up to half as much, and still have a flavorful spread, especially when making multiples for a crowd. Taste the salmon first, since its saltiness varies; use less rather than risk a salty spread.
