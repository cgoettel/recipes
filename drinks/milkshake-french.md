# French Milkshake

## Preparation

5 minutes

## Equipment

- Blender
- Milkshake or other serving glass

## Ingredients

- 2 scoops of vanilla ice cream
- 1 cup (120 ml) whole milk, cold

## Instructions

1. Remove ice cream from freezer 10 minutes in advance to soften.
2. Put scoops of ice cream in a blender and pour in the cold milk. Blend together until smooth.
3. Pour into serving glass and serve immediately.

## Variation

This recipe can be used with all flavours of ice cream: coffee, chocolate, caramel.
For fruit milkshakes, strawberry for example, add 1/2 cup (50 g) of fruit and blend with the milk and ice cream.

## Chef's Tip

If you do not have a stand blender, you can also prepare the milkshake using an immersion hand blender.
