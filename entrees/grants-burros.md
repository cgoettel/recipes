# Grant's burros

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs ground beef
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 16 oz beef broth
- 2&ndash;3 lb chuck roast, shredded
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 16 oz tomato sauce
- 1 can green chilis (short or tall, depends how you like it; I prefer more)
- 1&ndash;2 T flour
- Chili powder, to taste (see Notes)

## Directions

- Slow cook [chuck roast](https://github.com/cgoettel/recipes/blob/master/entrees/meat/chuck-roast.md). Shred.
- Cook ground beef and onion. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
- Add flour and cook down to remove flour flavor.
- Add beef broth and tomato sauce.
- Bring to boil, reduce heat, and let thicken.
- Add shredded beef.
- Season to taste with chili powder (see Notes).

## Notes

- I have never measured the chili powder before. For a full recipe, I sprinkle chili powder across the top of the mixture and stir it in. I do this like 6&ndash;8 times. After about the third time, you should be able to taste where this is going. Keep going until the flavor is full, but beware because you can go too far. Trial and error. Or I'll measure next time.
