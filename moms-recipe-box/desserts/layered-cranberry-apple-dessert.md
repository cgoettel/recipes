# Layered cranberry apple dessert

From a Jell-O box panel: a creamy cranberry-apple layer under a clear cranberry gelatin layer. Makes about 6½ cups.

## Ingredients

- 1 (6 oz, 8-serving) package or 2 (3 oz) packages raspberry Jell-O
- 2 cups boiling water
- 1½ cups cranberry apple juice drink
- 1 (8 oz) can whole berry cranberry sauce (¾ cup)
- 2 cups Cool Whip, thawed
- 1 unpeeled apple, finely diced

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the gelatin in the boiling water. Stir in the cranberry apple drink.
2. Measure out 2 cups of the gelatin and chill it until slightly thickened.
3. Blend the cranberry sauce into the remaining gelatin and set it aside at room temperature.
4. Blend the Cool Whip into the chilled, thickened 2 cups of gelatin. Stir in the apple and chill until thickened.
5. Pour the creamy layer into a 7- or 8-cup bowl and chill until set but not firm.
6. Meanwhile, chill the cranberry gelatin mixture until thickened, then spoon it over the creamy layer.
7. Chill until set, about 3 hours.
