# Niçoise salad

## Ingredients

- Shredded lettuce
- Diced leftover potatoes
- Canned green beans, drained
- 1 (5 oz) can tuna, drained
- Tomato wedges
- Hard-boiled eggs, diced
- Italian dressing
- Worcestershire sauce

## Instructions

1. Combine the lettuce, potatoes, green beans, tuna, tomatoes, and eggs.
2. Serve with Italian dressing and a dash of Worcestershire sauce.
