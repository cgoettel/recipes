# Chicken Salad — Mel's Kitchen

From [Mel's Kitchen Cafe](https://www.melskitchencafe.com/).

## Ingredients

- 4 C cooked, shredded (or small-chopped) chicken
- 1 C small-diced celery
- 1 C red grapes, halved or quartered
- ½ C chopped slivered almonds or cashews
- 2–3 T finely diced green onions or chives
- 2 T chopped fresh parsley

### Dressing

- 1 C mayonnaise
- 2 T sour cream
- 1 T freshly squeezed lemon juice or red wine vinegar
- 1 T Dijon mustard
- ½ t coarse kosher salt (less for table salt)
- ⅛ t coarsely ground black pepper
- ⅛ t dried dill
- ⅛ t garlic powder
- ⅛ t onion powder
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Whisk the dressing ingredients together.
2. Combine the chicken salad ingredients in a large bowl.
3. Toss with the dressing. Adjust salt and pepper to taste.
