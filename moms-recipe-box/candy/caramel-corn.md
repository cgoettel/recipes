# Caramel corn that stays separated

## Ingredients

- 2 cups brown sugar, light or dark
- ½ cup light corn syrup (dark works too)
- 2 sticks (1 cup) butter
- ¼ teaspoon cream of tartar
- ¼ teaspoon baking soda
- 6–7 quarts popped popcorn

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 250°F.
2. Bring the brown sugar, corn syrup, and butter to a boil.
3. Remove from the heat and add the cream of tartar and baking soda. Mix well.
4. Pour over the popcorn and stir until evenly coated.
5. Bake for 1 hour, stirring every 20 minutes.

## Notes

- A large stainless steel bowl works for both mixing and baking.
