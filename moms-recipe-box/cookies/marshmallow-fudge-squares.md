# Marshmallow fudge squares

## Ingredients

### Cake

- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter, melted
- ¼ cup cocoa
- 3 eggs
- ⅔ cup flour
- ⅛ teaspoon baking soda
- 1 cup chopped nuts
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 15 large marshmallows

### Frosting

- ¾ stick (6 tablespoons) butter
- ¼ cup cocoa
- 3½ cups powdered sugar
- Cream or milk

## Instructions

### Cake

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9x9-inch pan.
2. Combine the granulated sugar, melted butter, ¼ cup cocoa, eggs, flour, baking soda, nuts, and vanilla. Spread in the pan.
3. Bake 15–20 minutes.
4. Remove from the oven and set the marshmallows on top. Return to the oven just long enough to soften them, then flatten them with a spoon.

### Frosting

1. Melt the butter and blend in the remaining ¼ cup cocoa and the powdered sugar.
2. Add cream or milk until it's a spreading consistency.
3. Frost while the cake is hot.
