# Applesauce cake

Makes 4 small loaves, 3 bread-size loaves, or 2 large loaves.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup shortening
- 3 cups sugar
- 2 eggs
- 3 cups applesauce
- 4 cups flour
- 2 teaspoons cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon ground cloves
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 2 tablespoons very warm water
- 4 teaspoons vanilla
- 1 cup raisins
- 1 cup chopped nuts (optional), plus more for the tops

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F and grease the loaf pans.
2. Cream the shortening, sugar, and eggs together.
3. Stir in the applesauce and set the mixture aside.
4. Sift the flour, cinnamon, cloves, and salt together, then beat them into the applesauce mixture.
5. Dissolve the baking soda in the very warm water and add it with the vanilla, raisins, and nuts.
6. Pour the batter into the greased loaf pans and sprinkle chopped nuts on top.
7. Bake for about 60 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

## Notes

- The 60-minute time is for bread-size loaves; check small loaves sooner and large loaves later.
