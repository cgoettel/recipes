# Apple cake

## Ingredients

### Cake

- 3 eggs
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 cups sifted flour
- 2 teaspoons cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 4–5 cups thinly sliced cooking apples
- 1½ cups nuts

### Frosting

- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 5 tablespoons butter, melted
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 cups powdered sugar

## Instructions

### Cake

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9×13-inch pan.
2. Beat the eggs and oil until foamy.
3. Sift the sugar, flour, cinnamon, baking soda, and salt together. Add to the egg mixture, then add the vanilla.
4. Stir in the apples and nuts.
5. Spread in the pan and bake 45–60 minutes.

### Frosting

1. Mix the cream cheese, butter, vanilla, and powdered sugar together.
2. Frost the cooled cake.
