# Burros

A ground beef and red chili filling for burros.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb ground beef
- Garlic salt
- 1 whole onion
- 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
- 1 can beef broth
- 1 can beef
- 1 red chili pod
- Flour and cold water, for thickening
- Flour tortillas, for serving

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef, seasoning it with garlic salt.
2. Add the whole onion.
3. Add the tomato sauce, then the broth and canned beef.
4. Add the red chili pod.
5. Stir flour and cold water into a thick paste and pour it into the mixture, stirring until it thickens.
6. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
7. Roll the filling in warm flour tortillas.
