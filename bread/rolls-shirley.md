# Rolls — Shirley

Makes 24 dinner rolls.

## Ingredients

- 2 C warm milk (105–110°F)
- 2 T instant dry yeast
- ¼ C sugar
- 2 t salt
- 6 T salted butter, softened
- 2 large eggs
- 6 C all-purpose flour
- 1 T melted butter

## Instructions

1. In the bowl of a stand mixer with the dough hook, combine warm milk, yeast, sugar, salt, softened butter, eggs, and 5½ C of the flour.
1. Mix on low. Once the flour starts to incorporate, increase to medium. Slowly add the remaining ½ C of flour until the dough pulls away from the sides. The dough should be slightly sticky and soft — add more or less flour by feel.
1. Transfer to a large lightly greased bowl, cover, and let rise until doubled — about 90 minutes.
1. Lightly grease a large baking sheet (or two 9" × 13" pans). Punch down the dough and form into 24 rolls. Arrange in six rows of four. Cover and let rise again until doubled — about 1 hour.
1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Bake 12–14 minutes until lightly browned.
