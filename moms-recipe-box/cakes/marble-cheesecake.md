# Marble cheesecake

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 1 cup graham cracker crumbs
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 3 tablespoons butter, melted

### Filling

- 3 (8 oz) packages cream cheese, softened
- ¾ cup sugar
- 3 tablespoons flour
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 3 eggs
- 1 (1 oz) square unsweetened chocolate, melted
- Whipped cream, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

### Crust

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Combine the crumbs, sugar, and butter, and press onto the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan.
3. Bake 10 minutes. Raise the oven to 450°F.

### Filling

1. Combine the cream cheese, sugar, flour, and vanilla, mixing at medium speed with an electric mixer until well blended.
2. Blend in the eggs.
3. Blend the chocolate into 1 cup of the batter.
4. Spoon the plain and chocolate batters alternately over the crust. Cut through the batter with a knife several times for a marble effect.
5. Bake at 450°F for 10 minutes. Reduce the oven to 250°F and bake 30 minutes more.
6. Loosen the cake from the rim of the pan and cool before removing the rim.
7. Chill. Garnish with whipped cream, if you like.
