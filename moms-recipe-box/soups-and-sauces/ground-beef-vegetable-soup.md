# Ground beef vegetable soup

## Ingredients

- 1¼ lb ground beef
- Onion, chopped, to taste
- Potatoes, cut up
- Carrots, cut up
- Celery, cut up
- 2 (10.5 oz) cans cream of chicken soup

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef and onion in a large pot.
2. Meanwhile, put the potatoes, carrots, and celery in a separate pan with water to cover, and cook until tender.
3. Stir the soup into the browned beef.
4. Pour the vegetables and their cooking water into the beef mixture and heat through.

## Notes

- The card is marked "double," so the amounts here were likely doubled for a crowd.
