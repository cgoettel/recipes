# Batter-fried chicken

Coats one fryer.

## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- ⅔ cup water
- ¼ cup oil
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- ¼ cup flour
- 1 fryer chicken, cut up
- Oil, for frying

## Instructions

1. Beat the egg, water, and oil together.
2. Add the sugar, salt, paprika, pepper, and flour and mix until smooth.
3. Dip the chicken pieces in the batter and fry 23 minutes, until cooked through.

## Notes

- The card says ¼ cup flour, which makes a thin batter; whisk in more if it doesn't cling.
