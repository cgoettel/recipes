# Salsa for canning

## Ingredients

- 8 quarts tomatoes, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 5 cups chopped onion
- 6 yellow or green jalapeños, chopped
- 1½ cups green chiles
- 1½ cups chopped green pepper
- ½ cup vinegar
- 4 tablespoons mild ground chili pepper
- 2½ tablespoons salt
- 1½ tablespoons sugar
- 1½ teaspoons white pepper
- 1½ teaspoons oregano
- 1 tablespoon cumin

## Instructions

1. Combine all the ingredients in a large pot.
2. Cook and stir until thick, 1–2 hours.
3. Pack into hot pint jars and process 15 minutes in a boiling-water canner.

## Notes

- Tested canning recipes add bottled lemon juice or more vinegar to tomato salsas for safety. Check a current tested recipe before canning this one, or keep it refrigerated or frozen.
