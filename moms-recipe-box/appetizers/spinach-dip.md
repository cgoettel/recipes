# Spinach dip

## Ingredients

- 1 (8 oz) can water chestnuts, minced
- 1 (10 oz) package frozen chopped spinach, thawed and drained
- 1 (1.4 oz) package Knorr vegetable soup mix, crushed in the envelope
- ½ cup chopped onion
- 1 (16 oz) container sour cream
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1 round loaf sheepherder bread, for serving

## Instructions

1. Mix everything except the bread together.
2. Refrigerate for 4 hours.
3. Hollow out the bread and fill it with the dip to serve.
