# Chicken and ham lasagna

A first-place newspaper recipe: two cheeses, mushrooms, and asparagus.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz lasagna noodles
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon minced dried onion
- ⅛ teaspoon garlic powder
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 cup milk
- 1 cup grated Parmesan or Romano cheese, divided
- 1 (3 oz) can chopped mushrooms, drained
- 1 (10 oz) package frozen cut asparagus, thawed and drained
- 2 cups cubed cooked chicken
- 1 (6 oz) package sliced or shredded mozzarella
- 1 (6 oz) package thinly sliced cooked ham

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Cook the noodles according to the package directions and drain.
2. In a saucepan, melt the butter. Blend in the flour, onion, garlic powder, and pepper.
3. Add the chicken broth and milk; cook and stir until bubbly.
4. Stir in ½ cup of the grated cheese and the mushrooms.
5. In a 13×9×2-inch baking dish, layer half the noodles, all of the asparagus, chicken, and mozzarella, and a third of the sauce.
6. Top with the ham, the remaining noodles, and the remaining sauce. Sprinkle with the remaining ½ cup grated cheese.
7. Bake 35 minutes, or until heated through. Let stand 10 minutes before cutting.
