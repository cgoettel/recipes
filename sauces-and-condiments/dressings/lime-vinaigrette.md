# Lime vinaigrette

From [CDKitchen](http://www.cdkitchen.com/recipes/recs/731/Lime-Vinaigrette-Dressing115018.shtml).

## Ingredients

- 1 C extra virgin olive oil
- ½ C fresh lime juice
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 t salt
- 2 t Dijon mustard
- ½ t white pepper
- 3 T white wine vinegar

## Instructions

1. Whisk everything together.
