# Blender hollandaise

## Ingredients

- 3 egg yolks
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- Pinch of cayenne
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter, melted and hot

## Instructions

1. Put the egg yolks, lemon juice, cayenne, and salt in the blender.
2. Cover and turn the blender to high. After 3 seconds, remove the lid and pour the hot butter over the eggs in a steady stream.
3. By the time the butter is poured in, about 3 seconds, the sauce should be finished. If not, blend on high about 5 seconds longer.
