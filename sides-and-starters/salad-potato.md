# Potato salad

From Grandma Leavitt

## Ingredients

- 6–8 potatoes
- 1 C mayonnaise
- Pickle juice (sweet, not dill)
- Mustard (French's)
- Salt
- Pepper
- Paprika (smoked is better, but not required)
- Evaporated milk (or substitute cream)
- Then add onion (yellow or red)
- Sweet and dill pickles (need both)
- 6–12 hard boiled eggs

## Instructions

1. Peel potatoes and boil whole until soft, about 45 minutes
1. Mix together pickle juice, mustard, salt, pepper, paprika, and evaporated milk.
