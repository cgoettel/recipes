# Bacon cheese ball

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons minced green onion
- 12 oz cream cheese, softened
- ¼ cup Best Foods mayonnaise
- 5 slices crisp bacon, crumbled, or 3 heaping tablespoons bacon bits
- ½ teaspoon dill weed
- ½ teaspoon pepper
- Chopped pecans, for rolling

## Instructions

1. Mix all the ingredients and chill.
2. Roll into a ball, then roll the ball in pecans.
