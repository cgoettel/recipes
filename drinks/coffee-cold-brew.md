# Coffee — Cold Brew

- 1:8 coffee:water
- 70 g coffee, ground at a 9.1 on the Fellow Ode Gen 2
- Should be about 560 g water in
- Stir for 30 seconds
- Let sit for 20–24 hours
- Pour into a container; should yield around 433 g out
- Add creamer until you're right around 700 g (about 270 g creamer)
