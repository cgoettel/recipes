# Potato galette

A galette is a crisp potato pancake. The secret is wringing the starch out of the potatoes and using a nonstick frying pan.

## Ingredients

- 1 large baking potato
- 3 tablespoons butter
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Grate the potato on the julienne disk of a food processor. Squeeze handfuls between your palms to extract all the liquid you can.
2. Melt 2 tablespoons of the butter in a 10-inch nonstick frying pan over medium-low heat. Add the potato and press it into a flat cake. Sprinkle with salt and pepper.
3. Fry 6–8 minutes, until the bottom is crisp and browned but not burned, lowering the heat if needed.
4. Flip the pancake. If flipping makes you nervous, put a large round platter over the pan, invert the pancake onto it, and slide it back into the pan.
5. Cut the remaining butter into 3 or 4 pieces and add them at the edge of the pancake. Sprinkle with more salt and pepper.
6. Cook the second side 6–8 minutes, until browned and very crisp.
7. Slice into wedges like a pie to serve.

## Notes

- Or quickly brown both sides, then finish the galette in a 400°F oven for 15–20 minutes.
