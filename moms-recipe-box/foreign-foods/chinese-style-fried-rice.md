# Chinese-style fried rice

A quick side for chicken, pork chops, or even burgers. Makes about 3 cups.

## Ingredients

- 1⅓ cups water
- 1⅓ cups Minute Rice
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1 egg, beaten
- ⅓ cup chopped onion
- 2–3 tablespoons soy sauce

## Instructions

1. Bring 1 cup of the water to a boil in a saucepan. Stir in the rice, remove from the heat, cover, and let stand 5 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, cook the egg in the butter in a 10-inch skillet until set.
3. Add the onion and rice, and cook and stir over medium heat until the rice and onion are lightly browned, about 5 minutes.
4. Combine the remaining ⅓ cup water with the soy sauce and stir it into the rice.
