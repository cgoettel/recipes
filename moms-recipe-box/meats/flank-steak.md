# Flank steak

## Ingredients

- 1 flank steak
- ½ cup Kikkoman barbecue teriyaki and soy sauce
- ⅓ cup soy sauce
- ½ cup lemon juice
- 3–5 shakes Worcestershire sauce
- ¼ cup oil
- Garlic powder and pepper

## Instructions

1. Sprinkle the steak with garlic powder and pepper.
2. Mix the teriyaki sauce, soy sauce, lemon juice, Worcestershire, and oil, and pour over the steak.
3. Marinate 3 hours in the refrigerator.
4. Grill to the doneness you like, over hot coals or a gas grill on high. Slice thinly across the grain.

## Notes

- The marinade works for boneless, skinless chicken too. Bake the chicken at 350°F for 20 minutes before finishing it on the grill.
