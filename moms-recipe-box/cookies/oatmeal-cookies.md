# Oatmeal cookies

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup shortening
- 1 cup brown sugar
- ½ cup granulated sugar
- 1 egg
- ¼ cup water
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 cup flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- 3 cups oats

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease cookie sheets.
2. Beat the shortening, sugars, egg, water, and vanilla until creamy.
3. Mix together the flour, salt, and baking soda; add and beat well.
4. Stir in the oats.
5. Drop by teaspoonfuls onto the cookie sheets and bake 12 minutes.

## Notes

- For variety, add 1 cup chopped nuts, 1 cup raisins, or ½ cup coconut, or a combination of all three.
