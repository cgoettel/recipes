# Bill Jordan's green salsa

## Ingredients

- 4 tomatillos
- 1.5-2 jalapenos (1 for medium) Can also use 3-4 serranos (2 for medium), stemmed.
- 2 large cloves of garlic, pealed.
- 6 sprigs of cilantro, rough chop.
- ¼ of a small onion, fine chop.
- ¼ teaspoon of coarse salt

## Instructions

1. Put the tomatillos, peppers, and garlic on a baking sheet under a broiler until they get roasted, they will soften and get black splotches. Flip. about 5-6 mins a side does it. If you dont have a broiler you can do it on a hot pan cast iron is best.
2. Let them cool.
3. Throw that all in a blender with ¼ cup of water.
4. That makes a cup of salsa, you can add in some more water if it is too thick for you. I know she also puts in some lemon juice, but that is not on her recipe card....
