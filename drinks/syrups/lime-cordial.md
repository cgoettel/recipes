# Lime Cordial

An acid-adjusted lime cordial adapted from *Tropical Standard*. Simmering the juice with sugar and peel pulls oil and depth out of the skins; the malic and citric acid stirred in off-heat rebuild the fresh-lime tartness that cooking would otherwise dull. Use anywhere a recipe calls for lime cordial — it keeps far longer than fresh juice.

The acid blend is what makes it read as lime rather than sweet syrup, so measure by mass.

## Ingredients

- 250 g lime juice
- 250 g white sugar
- 12.5 g lime peel
- 2.5 g malic acid
- 5 g citric acid

## Instructions

1. Simmer the lime juice, sugar, and peel together, covered, on low for 15 minutes.
2. Pull off the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 6 hours so the peel keeps infusing as it cools.
3. Strain out the peel, then stir in the malic and citric acid until dissolved.

## Notes

Keeps about 2 weeks in the fridge.
