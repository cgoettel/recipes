# Sweetened Heavy Cream

A lightly sweetened, barely-whipped cream cap — the float for the Tipsy Jitters
(Alice Howard). The Angostura gives it a baking-spice lift so it doesn't read
flatly sweet over a coffee drink.

## Ingredients

- Heavy cream
- Simple syrup, to taste
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters

## Instructions

1. Combine the heavy cream, a little simple syrup, and the Angostura.
2. Lightly whip — just to loosely thicken, still pourable — and float over the
   drink.
