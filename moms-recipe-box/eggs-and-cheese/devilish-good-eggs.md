# Devilish good eggs (deviled eggs)

## Ingredients

- 6 hard-boiled eggs
- ¼ cup mayonnaise
- 2 teaspoons pickle relish
- ¼ teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Halve the eggs. Remove the yolks and mash them.
2. Blend the mayonnaise, relish, and salt together and fold in the yolks.
3. Refill the whites with the mixture.

## Notes

- The card lists relish twice (1 teaspoon pickle relish and 1 teaspoon relish). A teaspoon of mustard in place of the second is a common way to make these.
