# Cream of broccoli soup (restaurant style)

A rich double-boiler soup modeled on the one served at the Cork 'n Cleaver steakhouse chain.

## Ingredients

### Soup

- 1½ lb broccoli with stems
- ¾ cup diced celery
- 1 quart milk
- 1 quart half-and-half
- ¼ cup dried parsley flakes
- 1½ tablespoons garlic salt
- 1½ tablespoons lemon juice
- 3 tablespoons chicken base
- ½ teaspoon white pepper
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter

### Thickening

- ¾ cup flour
- ½–¾ cup dry sherry
- 1 cup water

### To serve

- Bacon bits, shredded cheese, or sliced mushrooms

## Instructions

1. Cook the broccoli until tender and chop it.
2. In the top of a double boiler, combine the celery, milk, half-and-half, parsley, garlic salt, lemon juice, chicken base, white pepper, and butter. Heat until steaming; let it simmer but never boil.
3. Whisk the flour, sherry, and water together until smooth, then stir into the soup and cook until thickened.
4. Add the broccoli and heat through.
5. Serve topped with bacon bits, cheese, or mushrooms.
