# Raw cauliflower salad

## Ingredients

### Salad

- Lettuce
- Onion
- Fresh raw cauliflower
- Raw broccoli
- Celery
- Bacon, cooked and crumbled

### Dressing

- 2 cups mayonnaise
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- ⅓ cup sugar
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon pepper

## Instructions

1. Chop all the vegetables fine and layer them in a bowl with the bacon.
2. Stir the dressing ingredients together and spread over the salad.
3. Refrigerate for 6 hours before serving.
