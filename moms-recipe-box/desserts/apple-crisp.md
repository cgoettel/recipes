# Apple crisp

## Ingredients

- 4 lb tart apples
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup light brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter, softened

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Peel, core, and quarter the apples and place them in a baking pan.
3. Combine the flour, brown sugar, and cinnamon. Add the butter and cut it in with a pastry blender to make coarse crumbs.
4. Sprinkle the crumbs evenly over the apples.
5. Bake 30–40 minutes, or until the apples are as tender as you like.
