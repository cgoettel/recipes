# Bread — Whole Wheat (Angie Peffer)

From Angie Peffer. Makes 6 loaves (half quantities in parens for 3 loaves).

## Ingredients

- ⅔ C vegetable oil (⅓ C)
- ⅔ C honey (⅓ C)
- 1–2 T liquid lecithin (optional)
- 6 C hot tap water (3 C)
- 3 T instant yeast (2 T)
- 16–18 C ground whole wheat flour (8–9 C)
- ½ C potato flakes (¼ C)
- 2 T vital wheat gluten (1 T)
- 2 T dough enhancer (1 T)
- 2 T salt (1 T)
- Butter spray

## Instructions

1. In a mixer, combine oil, honey, lecithin, water, yeast, and flour. On top of the flour add the potato flakes, gluten, dough enhancer, and salt.
1. Blend until smooth and gradually add the rest of the flour until the dough no longer sticks to the sides of the bowl.
1. Use the mixer to knead (2½–3½ minutes) or hand-knead 10 minutes.
1. Divide into 6 loaves (or 3 for a half recipe). Add flavor ingredients now if making variants.
1. Shape and place in greased 8½" × 4½" bread pans. Cover with a towel and let rise 2–3 hours.
1. Bake at 350°F for 29 minutes.
1. Remove from pans immediately and cool on a rack.

## Variants

- **Apple cranberry** — Hand-knead in 2 T sugar + 1 T brown sugar + 1 T cinnamon (mixed) plus ½ C diced apples and ¼ C dried cranberries.
- **Sunflower** — Hand-knead in 2 T sunflower seeds and ½ T flaxseed. Garnish the top of the loaf with 1 T pumpkin seeds.
- **Cheese** — Hand-knead in 3 T ranch dressing mix and 1 C sharp cheddar.
