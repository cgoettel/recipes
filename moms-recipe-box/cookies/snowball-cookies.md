# Snowball cookies

## Ingredients

- 4 cups flour
- 4 sticks (2 cups) butter
- 4 tablespoons sugar
- 4 teaspoons vanilla
- Dash salt
- 2 cups chopped walnuts
- Powdered sugar, for rolling

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
2. Cream the flour, butter, sugar, vanilla, and salt together with your hands.
3. Add the nuts.
4. Roll into balls the size of a large marble.
5. Bake 30 minutes.
6. Roll in powdered sugar while still warm.
