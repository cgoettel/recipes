# Marinade — El Pollo Loco Chicken

Copycat marinade for El Pollo Loco-style grilled chicken.

## Ingredients

- 6 oz pineapple juice
- 2 T lime juice
- 1 T white vinegar
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ t salt
- ½ t dried oregano, crumbled
- ⅛ t ground pepper
- ¼ t mild chili pepper (Anaheim or California), stem and seeds removed, finely minced
- 8 drops yellow food coloring (optional)
- 1 T vegetable oil
- 4 lbs frying chicken, cut up

## Instructions

1. Marinate the chicken in a zip-lock bag overnight.
2. Reserve ¼ C of the marinade for basting. Make sure the chicken cooks on both sides after the final basting — no salmonella risk.
3. Grill over indirect heat, turning 4 times, ~12 minutes per turn.
4. Serve with tortillas, rice, and borracho beans.
