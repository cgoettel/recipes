# Hawaiian butter mochi (mochi blondies)

This recipe is from [SBS](https://www.sbs.com.au/food/article/2022/07/15/hawaiian-butter-mochi-truly-underrated-dessert).

## Ingredients

- 3 cups glutinous rice flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 stick (8 tablespoons) butter, melted
- 3 eggs
- 2 cups coconut milk
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Combine all ingredients in a bowl and whisk until smooth.
3. Pour into a lightly greased 13x9 pan.
4. Bake at 350°F for 45–60 minutes, or until golden brown and cooked through.
