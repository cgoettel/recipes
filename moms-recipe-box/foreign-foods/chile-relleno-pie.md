# Chile relleno pie

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 green pepper, chopped
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- ¼ teaspoon oregano
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- Salt and pepper
- 1 (7 oz) can whole green chiles
- ½ lb Monterey Jack cheese, grated
- ½ lb sharp cheddar cheese, grated
- 4 eggs, slightly beaten
- 1 cup half-and-half
- 1 tablespoon flour
- 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F and grease a baking dish.
2. Brown the beef with the green pepper, onion, oregano, garlic, salt, and pepper.
3. Seed the chiles and spread them over the bottom of the baking dish. Cover with the browned meat and sprinkle with the cheese.
4. Combine the eggs, half-and-half, and flour, and pour over the top.
5. Bake 45 minutes. Pour the tomato sauce on top and bake 10 minutes longer.

## Notes

- Just as good made without the beef mixture.
