# Pound cake

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sugar
- 2 sticks (1 cup) butter, at room temperature
- 6 eggs, at room temperature
- 2 cups flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 teaspoon baking powder

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a tube or Bundt pan.
2. Dump everything in the mixer and beat for 10 minutes.
3. Pour into the pan and bake for 1 hour.
