# Cocoa mounds

## Ingredients

- 1¾ cups flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ cup shortening
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 6 tablespoons cocoa
- ½ cup milk

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Cream the shortening and sugar, then beat in the eggs and vanilla.
3. Stir together the flour, salt, baking soda, and cocoa, and add alternately with the milk.
4. Drop in mounds onto a cookie sheet and bake 12 minutes.
