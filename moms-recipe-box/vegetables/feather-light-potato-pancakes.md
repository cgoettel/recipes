# Feather-light potato pancakes

Soufflé-like in consistency, for anyone who finds latkes too heavy.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb red potatoes
- ⅓ cup flour
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 2 eggs
- 1 egg yolk
- ½ cup milk
- Salt, pepper, and cayenne pepper, to taste
- 1 stick (½ cup) clarified butter, for frying
- ½ cup crème fraîche or sour cream
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh chives

## Instructions

1. Boil the potatoes in their skins until soft. Cool and remove the skins.
2. Purée the potatoes through a ricer or food mill. Gently stir in the flour, cornstarch, eggs, egg yolk, milk, and seasonings.
3. Heat 2 tablespoons of the butter in a heavy skillet over medium heat.
4. Spoon mounds of batter into the pan to form 2-inch pancakes. Fry about 30 seconds per side, until lightly browned. Repeat with the rest of the butter and batter.
5. Serve with dollops of crème fraîche or sour cream and a sprinkle of chives.
