# Mushroom pot roast

## Ingredients

- 3–4 lb boneless beef pot roast
- Shortening, if needed
- 1 (10.5 oz) can cream of mushroom soup
- ¾ cup water, divided
- 2–4 tablespoons flour

## Instructions

1. In a large heavy pan, brown the meat, using shortening if necessary. Pour off the fat.
2. Pour the soup and ½ cup water over the roast. Cover and simmer 2½–3 hours, stirring often.
3. Remove the roast to a platter.
4. Gradually blend the remaining ¼ cup water into the flour, then slowly stir it into the pan juices. Cook, stirring, until thickened.
5. Serve the gravy with the roast.
