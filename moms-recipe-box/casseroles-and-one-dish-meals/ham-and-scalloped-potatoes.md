# Slow-cooker ham and scalloped potatoes

## Ingredients

- 8–10 medium potatoes, peeled and sliced medium
- ½ teaspoon cream of tartar
- 1 cup water
- 2 onions, peeled and sliced
- 6–8 slices ham, chopped
- 1 cup grated cheddar cheese
- 1 (10.5 oz) can cream of chicken soup
- Salt and pepper
- Paprika

## Instructions

1. Stir the cream of tartar into the water and rinse the sliced potatoes in it to keep them from darkening. Drain.
2. Layer half the ham, potatoes, and onions in a slow cooker. Sprinkle with salt and pepper, then top with the grated cheese.
3. Add the remaining potatoes, onions, and ham.
4. Spoon the soup over the top without diluting it. Sprinkle with paprika.
5. Cover and cook on low 8–10 hours or on high 4 hours.
