# Easy chicken salad sandwich

## Ingredients

- 1 (5 oz) can Swanson chunk white chicken
- ¼ cup sliced celery
- 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon chopped pimiento
- ¼ teaspoon lemon juice
- 2 long rolls, about 6 inches each
- Lettuce leaves

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, combine everything except the rolls and lettuce. Chill.
2. Halve the rolls horizontally. Arrange lettuce on the bottom halves and top each with ½ cup of the chicken mixture and the top half of the roll.
3. Cut the sandwiches diagonally in half.
