# Melissa's pizza dough

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups (≈340 g) water (110°ish degrees)
- 1 envelope 2¼ teaspoons instant yeast
- ¼ cup (≈55 g) room temperature water
- 2 tablespoons (≈25 g) virgin olive oil
- 4 cups (≈480 g) flour, more for dusting
- 1½ teaspoons (≈9 g) salt (lean on the less side, can be salty)

## Instructions

1. Let yeast stand in warm water 5 min. Add rest of water + oil, stir.
2. Combine flour + salt in mixer, slowly add liquid until mass forms. Replace paddle w/ dough hook. Knead for 5 min. or until smooth + elastic. Put dough into greased bowl, cover, let rise 1½ - 2 hours or until double in size.
3. Separate dough, let relax 10 min. Transfer to parchment paper dusted with cornmeal or semolina.
4. Bake 8-12 minutes at 500°.
