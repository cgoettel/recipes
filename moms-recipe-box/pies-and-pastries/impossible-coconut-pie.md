# Impossible coconut pie

The batter separates as it bakes into its own crust, custard, and coconut top.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups milk
- ½ cup flour
- 1 cup shredded coconut
- 4 eggs
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- ¾ stick (6 tablespoons) butter, melted

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 10-inch pie pan.
2. Blend everything together until smooth.
3. Pour into the pan and bake 45 minutes, until set and golden.
