# Poppy seed cake

## Ingredients

- 1 (15.25 oz) box yellow cake mix
- 1 (3.4 oz) package instant lemon pudding
- 4 eggs
- ¼ cup poppy seeds
- ¾ cup oil
- 1 cup water

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a Bundt pan or 2 loaf pans.
2. Mix everything together for 3 minutes.
3. Pour into the pan and bake for 40–45 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
