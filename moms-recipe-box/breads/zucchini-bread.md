# Zucchini bread

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 2 cups peeled, grated zucchini
- 1 cup oil
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 teaspoons vanilla
- 3 cups flour
- ¼ teaspoon baking powder
- 1 tablespoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking soda

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F and grease two loaf pans.
2. Mix the zucchini, oil, sugar, and vanilla together.
3. In a large bowl, beat the eggs until fluffy, then add the zucchini mixture to the eggs.
4. In another bowl, mix the flour, baking powder, cinnamon, salt, and baking soda. Stir them into the zucchini mixture just until combined.
5. Pour into the pans and bake about 1 hour 15 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

## Notes

- The card doesn't give a pan or baking time. The time here comes from the other zucchini bread card in the box, which bakes at 325°F for 1 hour 15 minutes.
