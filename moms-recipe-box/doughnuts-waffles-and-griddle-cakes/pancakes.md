# Pancakes

## Ingredients

- 2½ cups flour
- 2 tablespoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup oil
- 2 cups milk, or more if needed

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar together.
2. Add the eggs, oil, and milk and stir just until combined. It's fine to leave lumps in. Add more milk if the batter is too thick.
3. Heat a lightly greased griddle over medium heat. Pour about ¼ cup of batter for each pancake and cook until bubbles form and the edges look set, then flip and cook until golden.
