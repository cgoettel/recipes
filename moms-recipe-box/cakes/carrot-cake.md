# Carrot cake

## Ingredients

### Cake

- 4 eggs
- 1½ cups oil
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 cups flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon allspice
- 3 cups grated raw carrots
- 1 cup nuts

### Icing

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter, softened
- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 1 lb powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Instructions

### Cake

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9×13-inch pan.
2. Beat the eggs, oil, and sugar.
3. Mix the dry ingredients and add them, then add the carrots and nuts.
4. Pour into the pan and bake 40–45 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool.

### Icing

1. Cream the butter and cream cheese together.
2. Add the powdered sugar gradually, then add the vanilla.
3. Spread over the cooled cake.
