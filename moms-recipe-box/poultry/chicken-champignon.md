# Chicken champignon

## Ingredients

- 4 chicken breast halves
- 2 tablespoons flour
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper
- 1 small clove garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 1 (10.5 oz) can cream of mushroom soup
- ½ cup green pepper strips

## Instructions

1. Dust the chicken with the flour, salt, and pepper.
2. In a frying pan, brown the chicken and garlic in the butter.
3. Add the onion and soup. Cover and cook over low heat about 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Add the green pepper. Cover and cook 15 minutes, or until tender.
