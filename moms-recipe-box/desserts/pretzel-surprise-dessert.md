# Pretzel surprise dessert

From a Town House gelatin box.

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 2 cups pretzels, broken
- 1½ sticks (¾ cup) butter, melted
- 3 tablespoons sugar

### Filling

- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 1 (8 oz) container Cool Whip, thawed
- 1 cup sugar

### Topping

- 1 (6 oz) package strawberry-banana gelatin, or 2 (3 oz) packages
- 2½ cups boiling water

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Mix the pretzels, butter, and 3 tablespoons sugar, and spread in a 9×13-inch pan.
3. Bake 8 minutes, then cool completely.
4. Cream together the cream cheese, Cool Whip, and 1 cup sugar, and spread evenly over the entire crust, sealing it to the edges. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.
5. Dissolve the gelatin in the boiling water and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
6. Pour the gelatin slowly over the cream cheese layer.
7. Chill until set, about 3 hours.
