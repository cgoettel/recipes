# Frozen fruit cups

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sour cream
- 1½ cups sugar
- 1 (8 oz) can crushed pineapple, drained
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- ⅛ teaspoon salt
- 1 banana, diced
- 3 tablespoons chopped maraschino cherries
- ¼ cup chopped nuts

## Instructions

1. Stir the sour cream, sugar, lemon juice, and salt together until the sugar dissolves.
2. Fold in the pineapple, banana, cherries, and nuts.
3. Spoon into paper-lined muffin cups and freeze until firm. Let them stand a few minutes before serving.
