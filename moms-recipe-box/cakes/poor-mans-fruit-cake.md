# Poor man's fruit cake

## Ingredients

- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1¼ cups water
- ⅓ cup shortening
- 2 cups raisins
- ½ teaspoon nutmeg
- 2 teaspoons cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon cloves (optional)
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons water
- 2 cups sifted flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder

## Instructions

1. In a saucepan, mix the brown sugar, 1¼ cups water, shortening, raisins, nutmeg, cinnamon, and cloves. Boil for 3 minutes, then cool.
2. Preheat the oven to 325°F and grease a 9-inch square pan or a loaf pan.
3. Mix the baking soda, salt, and 2 teaspoons water, and stir into the cooled raisin mixture.
4. Sift the flour and baking powder together and blend in.
5. Pour into the pan. Bake a 9-inch square pan 55 minutes, or a loaf 75 minutes.
