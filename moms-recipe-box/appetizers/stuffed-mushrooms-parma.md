# Stuffed mushrooms Parma

## Ingredients

- 1 lb fresh mushrooms
- ½ cup (2 oz) Kraft grated Parmesan cheese
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter, melted
- 1 tablespoon chopped green onion

## Instructions

1. Preheat the broiler.
2. Remove the stems from the mushrooms and chop them.
3. Combine the chopped stems, Parmesan, butter, and green onion.
4. Fill the mushroom caps with the mixture.
5. Place them on the rack of a broiler pan and broil for 2–3 minutes, until the top of the filling is slightly crusty.
6. Serve hot.
