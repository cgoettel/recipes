# Canned pinto beans

Dry beans pressure-canned straight into the jar, no soaking.

## Ingredients

Per quart jar:

- 1⅓ cups dry pinto beans
- 1 teaspoon salt
- Boiling water

## Instructions

1. Put the dry beans and salt into each quart jar.
2. Fill with boiling water, leaving headspace, and seal.
3. Process in a pressure canner at 10 lb pressure for 75 minutes.
