# Cream fondant

Centers for hand-dipped chocolates.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup cream
- ½ cup milk
- 4 cups sugar
- 3 tablespoons light corn syrup (white Karo)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- ¾ stick (6 tablespoons) butter

### Flavorings (optional)

- 2 squares baking chocolate, for chocolate centers
- 4 drops oil of peppermint, for peppermint centers
- Nuts, coconut, or other flavorings and colorings

## Instructions

1. Combine the cream, milk, sugar, and corn syrup and boil to the soft-ball stage, 236°F at sea level.
2. Pour out onto a marble slab and let it cool.
3. Starting from the outside edges, work the fondant completely with a wide-blade putty knife (about 6 inches).
4. While working it, add the vanilla and butter. Keep kneading until creamy and light colored.
5. Let it rest until the mixture sets up.
6. Form into balls, tinting and flavoring as you like.

### Variations

1. **Chocolate:** add the baking chocolate and knead it in while the fondant is still warm on the slab.
2. **Peppermint:** add the oil of peppermint while kneading.
3. Divide one batch and add nuts, coconut, or different flavorings and colorings to make a variety of candies.

## Notes

- Oil of peppermint is much stronger than peppermint extract; the card says it's available from a pharmacist.
- 236°F is the sea-level soft-ball temperature. At altitude, subtract about 2°F for every 1,000 feet.
