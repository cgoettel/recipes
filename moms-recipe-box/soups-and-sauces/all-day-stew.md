# All day stew

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb stew meat, cubed
- 1 (14.5 oz) can stewed tomatoes
- 1 (15 oz) can peas
- 4 carrots, cut up
- 4 onions, cut up
- 4 potatoes, cut up
- 4 celery stalks, cut up
- 1 (4 oz) can mushrooms
- 1–1½ packages Schilling beef stew seasoning mix
- ½ cup Minute tapioca
- Water, to barely cover

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 250°F.
2. Combine everything in a large covered casserole or Dutch oven and mix well.
3. Bake, covered, 8 hours or longer, until tender.
