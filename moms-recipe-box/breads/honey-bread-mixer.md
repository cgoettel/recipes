# Honey bread (stand mixer)

Written for a mixer with kneading arms.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups warm water
- ¼ cup oil
- ¼ cup honey
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 tablespoons dough enhancer
- 1 tablespoon instant yeast
- 6–7 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Put the water, oil, honey, salt, dough enhancer, and 6 cups of the flour in the mixer bowl. Add the yeast last.
2. Jog (pulse) the mixer to combine, then turn on the kneading arms and add the last cup or so of flour, just until the dough pulls away from the sides of the bowl. Knead until smooth and elastic.
3. Let rise until doubled, shape into 2 loaves in greased 9×5 pans, and let rise again until doubled.
4. Bake at 350°F for 30–35 minutes, until golden and hollow-sounding when tapped.

## Notes

- The slip stops at the kneading; the rising, shaping, and baking steps are standard for a two-loaf batch.
