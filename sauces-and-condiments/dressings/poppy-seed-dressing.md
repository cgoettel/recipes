# Poppy seed dressing

From [Whitney Bond](https://whitneybond.com/poppy-seed-dressing/).

## Ingredients

- ½ C honey (or granulated sugar)
- 1 t salt
- 2 t dry mustard (or 2 T Dijon)
- ½ C white wine vinegar (or apple cider vinegar)
- 1 C canola oil (or vegetable oil, or light olive oil)
- 1 T poppy seeds
- ¼ C minced shallots (or red onion, or 2 t onion powder)

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk the honey, salt, dry mustard, and white wine vinegar together.
1. Slowly drizzle in the oil, whisking as you add it.
1. Stir in the poppy seeds and minced shallots.

## Notes

- For a smoother dressing, blend everything except the poppy seeds, then stir those in before serving.
