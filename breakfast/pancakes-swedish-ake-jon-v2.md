# Swedish Pancakes (Åke Jon) - Version 2

## Ingredients

- 0,3 liter of wheat flour (1.27 cups)
- 1 teaspoon of sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 0,6 liter milk (3%) (2.54 cups)
- 3 eggs
- butter, to melt in the oven plate.

## Instructions

1. Put the oven at 225 ° Celsius (437 ° F).
2. Mix the flour with sugar and salt in a bowl.
3. Add half of the milk and whisk to a smooth, thick batter.
4. Stir the eggs down.
5. Whip the remaining milk and continue whipping to a lump-free smear. (if time is left let the batter swell about 10 minutes).
6. Pour the batter in a butter greased oven-proof form, about 20 x 30 cm (7.9 x 11.8 inches).
7. Bake in the middle of the oven for 30-40 minutes.

## Notes

- Just double everything if you have friends for dinner (Not the oven temperature;).
