# Fruit pizza

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 1 cup flour
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter

### Filling

- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup powdered sugar

### Glaze

- 1 cup fruit juice
- 4 tablespoons sugar
- 4 teaspoons cornstarch

### Topping

- Fresh fruit, sliced

## Instructions

### Crust

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Mix the flour, sugar, and butter together and flatten into a pizza pan.
3. Bake 10–12 minutes. Let cool.

### Filling and glaze

1. Mix the cream cheese with the powdered sugar and spread it over the cooled crust.
2. Add the fruit juice to the cornstarch and mix until smooth. Add the sugar and heat, stirring, until clear.
3. After the glaze cools, arrange the fruit over the filling and spoon the glaze over it.
