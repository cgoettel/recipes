# French onion soup au gratin

## Ingredients

- 4 large onions, thinly sliced
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 4 (10.5 oz) cans condensed beef broth
- ½ cup dry sherry (optional)
- 2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
- Dash of pepper
- 6 slices French bread, ½ inch thick, toasted
- ¾ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 6 slices Gruyère or Swiss cheese

## Instructions

1. In a large saucepan or Dutch oven, cook the onions in the butter until tender but not brown, about 20 minutes.
2. Add the broth, sherry, Worcestershire, and pepper. Bring to a boil.
3. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Pour the soup into 6 individual ovenproof casseroles or bowls.
4. Float a slice of toasted French bread in each and sprinkle generously with Parmesan. Top each bowl with a slice of Gruyère or Swiss.
5. Bake 15–20 minutes, or broil about 5 minutes, until the cheese is bubbly.
