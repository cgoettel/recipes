# Blueberry sauce

## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons cornstarch
- ½ cup water
- ¾ cup frozen blueberries, thawed and crushed
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice

## Instructions

1. In a saucepan, mix the cornstarch with a small amount of the water and stir until smooth.
2. Add the remaining water, the blueberries, and the honey.
3. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly, and cook until thickened.
4. Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice.
5. Serve over pancakes.
