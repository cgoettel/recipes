# Baked chicken

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts (4 halves)
- Garlic salt
- Paprika
- 1 (10.5 oz) can cream of mushroom soup
- ¼ can (about ⅓ cup) evaporated milk
- Onion rings (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Place the chicken in a pan and sprinkle with garlic salt and paprika.
3. Heat the soup and evaporated milk together and pour over the chicken.
4. Top with onion rings, if you like.
5. Bake 1 hour.
