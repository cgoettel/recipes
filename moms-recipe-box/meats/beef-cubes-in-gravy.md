# Beef cubes in gravy

## Ingredients

- 1 large onion, chopped
- 4 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 3 lb cubed beef
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 3 teaspoons ketchup
- 1 cup water
- 1 tablespoon flour
- ½ cup water
- Noodles, for serving

## Instructions

1. Brown the onion in the oil.
2. Add the paprika, beef, salt, ketchup, and 1 cup water. Simmer until tender, about 2 hours.
3. Mix the flour and ½ cup water together. Add to the meat mixture to thicken the liquid.
4. Serve over noodles.
