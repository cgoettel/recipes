# Fettuccine Alfredo

A simple main dish for two, or a side dish for four.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz dried fettuccine
- 5 tablespoons butter
- 2 cloves garlic, pressed or minced
- ½ cup milk
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch
- ½ cup finely grated fresh Parmesan cheese
- ¼ teaspoon salt (optional, to taste)
- ⅛ teaspoon black pepper
- ⅛ teaspoon nutmeg
- 1 tablespoon minced fresh chives

## Instructions

1. Cook the fettuccine according to the package directions in a large pot of boiling water. Drain.
2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and sauté until golden.
3. In a cup, stir the milk into the cornstarch to make a slurry, then stir it into the butter mixture.
4. Add the cream and stir until thickened, then cook 1 minute more.
5. Add the Parmesan, salt, pepper, and nutmeg, and stir.
6. Add the cooked fettuccine and toss to coat.
7. Garnish with chives and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Use half-and-half in place of the milk and cream, or use all milk for a thinner sauce.
- The recipe was written for unsalted butter. With salted butter, taste before adding any salt.
