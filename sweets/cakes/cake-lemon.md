# Lemon cake

## Ingredients

- 1 lemon cake mix
- 1 lemon instant pudding mix
- 1 C sour cream
- ½ C oil
- ½ C water
- 4 eggs
- 1 lemon, juiced

### Frosting

- Zest of 1 lemon
- 1 container of lemon flavored frosting

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Zest the lemon and set aside.
3. Mix everything together.
4. Pour into greased bundt cake pan.
5. Bake at 350°F for 50–60 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.
6. Once cooled, add lemon zest to frosting and coat cake.
