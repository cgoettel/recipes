# Thai peanut sauce

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups peanut butter
- ¼ cup cream
- 1 can coconut milk (next time, buy two and try only coconut milk)
- 2 tablespoons sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 4 tablespoons water
- 5 tablespoons brown sugar
- juice of 2 limes
- 1-1½ tablespoons minced cilantro
