# Rich Coconut Cream

A sweetened coconut cream for Piña Coladas and other tiki drinks — full-fat coconut milk cut with sugar and a pinch of salt. A fresher, less cloying make-your-own alternative to canned cream of coconut.

## Ingredients

- 1 ¾ cups sugar
- 13.5 oz (400 ml) unsweetened full-fat coconut milk
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. In a medium saucepan, combine everything and stir until the sugar is completely dissolved. Warm gently if it needs help dissolving, then cool and store chilled.
