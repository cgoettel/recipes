# Raspberry cranberry gelatin salad

A layered holiday salad: raspberry Jell-O with cranberry-raspberry sauce, under a sweet cream cheese topping and pecans.

## Ingredients

### Gelatin

- 1 large (6 oz) package raspberry Jell-O
- 2 cups boiling water
- 1 (10 oz) package frozen raspberries
- 1 large (20 oz) can crushed pineapple, not drained
- 1 can cranberry-raspberry sauce (1½ cans if they're small)

### Topping

- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup sour cream
- ⅓ cup powdered sugar
- Chopped pecans

## Instructions

1. In a 9×13-inch dish, dissolve the Jell-O in the boiling water.
2. Add the frozen raspberries, the pineapple with its juice, and the cranberry-raspberry sauce. Mash it up and stir it around.
3. Refrigerate until set.
4. Beat the cream cheese, sour cream, and powdered sugar until smooth, and spread over the set gelatin.
5. Top with chopped pecans.
