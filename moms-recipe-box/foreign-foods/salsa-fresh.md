# Fresh salsa

## Ingredients

- 3–4 lb tomatoes, chopped
- 2 yellow chiles, seeded and chopped
- ½–¾ bunch cilantro, leaves only, chopped fine
- 2 bunches green onions, chopped
- 2 (4 oz) cans diced green chiles
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic

## Instructions

1. Combine everything in a large bowl and stir well.
2. Taste and adjust the salt, then chill before serving.
