# J.R.'s salsa

## Ingredients

- 28 oz cans crushed tomatoes
- 1 can diced jalapeños
- 1 habanero blended with 3 oz water
- 1-2 serranos
- ½ large white onion
- ¼–½ bundle cilantro
- 3 oz water
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
- ¼ teaspoon oregano
