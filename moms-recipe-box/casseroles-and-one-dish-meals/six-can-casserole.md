# Six-can casserole

A Sunday afternoon treat.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 (10.75 oz) can condensed tomato soup
- 1 can mushrooms
- 1 can spaghetti
- 1 small can kidney beans
- 1 can chili con carne without beans
- 1 can cream-style corn
- Grated cheese (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Cook the ground beef and onion together until browned.
3. Mix in the tomato soup, mushrooms, spaghetti, kidney beans, chili, and corn, and pour into a casserole dish.
4. Bake for 30 minutes. Top with cheese, if you like.
