# Potato gratin with Swiss cheese

## Ingredients

- 4 lb russet potatoes, peeled and cut into ¼-inch slices
- 4 teaspoons minced garlic
- 2 teaspoons salt
- ½ teaspoon white pepper
- ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 2 cups whipping cream
- 2 cups grated Swiss cheese

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Place half the potato slices in a 9×13-inch baking dish. Sprinkle with half the garlic, salt, pepper, and nutmeg.
3. Cover with the remaining potatoes, then the remaining garlic, salt, pepper, and nutmeg.
4. Pour the cream over the potatoes. Cover tightly with foil and bake until the potatoes are tender, about 1 hour.
5. Raise the oven to 450°F. Uncover the potatoes and press them down with a spatula to an even thickness.
6. Top with the cheese and bake uncovered until it melts, about 10 minutes. Cool slightly before serving.
