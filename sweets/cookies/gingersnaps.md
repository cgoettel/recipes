# King Arthur gingernsaps

## Ingredients

### Cookies

- 1 stick (8 tablespoons) butter (shortening can be used for hard cookies)
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 egg
- ⅓ cup molasses
- 2⅓ cups flour
- 1–2 teaspoons ground ginger
- ½ teaspoon ground cloves
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon

### Coating

- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 375°F
- Beat butter and sugar. Add sugar, salt, and baking soda.
- Beat in egg and molasses.
- Add flour and spices.
- Combine coating ingredients.
- Make balls and add cover in coating.
- Place on baking sheet.
- Bake for 11+ minutes.
