# Wassail

## Ingredients

- 1 quart water
- 2¼ cups sugar
- 6 whole cloves
- 4 cinnamon sticks
- 10 whole allspice berries
- 2 quarts apple juice
- 1 (16 oz) can frozen orange juice concentrate

## Instructions

1. Boil the water and sugar for 5 minutes.
2. Reconstitute the orange juice concentrate and set aside half of it.
3. Add the cloves, cinnamon sticks, allspice, apple juice, and the other half of the orange juice to the syrup.
4. Simmer for 15–20 minutes and serve hot.
