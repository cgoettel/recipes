# Quinoa salad dressing

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup lime juice
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1¼ teaspoons ground cumin
- ⅓ cup olive oil

## Instructions

1. Whisk the lime juice, salt, and cumin together.
2. Add the oil in a stream while whisking.
3. Drizzle over the salad and toss with salt and pepper to taste.
