# Salmon luncheon platter

A chilled salmon and rice salad, packed in a fish mold and decorated as a fish.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup long-grain white rice
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1 (14.75 oz) can salmon
- 2 tablespoons minced onion
- ¼ cup capers (optional)
- ½ cup chopped celery
- 3 tablespoons chopped pimiento
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon pepper

### Garnish

- 1 slice Spanish olive
- Cheese, cut in triangles
- Pitted black olives, halved
- Parsley sprigs

## Instructions

1. Cook the rice in boiling water according to the package directions. Rinse and drain well.
2. Stir in the mayonnaise and chill.
3. Flake the salmon and remove the bones.
4. Combine the rice, salmon, onion, capers, celery, pimiento, salt, and pepper.
5. Pack into a fish mold and chill 1 hour or more.
6. At serving time, turn the mold out onto a large platter. Garnish it as a fish: a slice of Spanish olive for the eye, triangles of cheese for the fins, and halved black olives for the scales, following the indentations of the mold.
7. Tuck parsley sprigs around the loaf and serve.
