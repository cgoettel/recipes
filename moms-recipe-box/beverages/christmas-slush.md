# Christmas slush

## Ingredients

- 2 bananas
- 1 (6 oz) can frozen orange juice concentrate, not reconstituted
- 1 (6 oz) can frozen lemonade concentrate, not reconstituted
- 1 cup pineapple juice
- 5 cups water
- 2–3 cups sugar
- 1 (2 L) bottle 7-Up or Sprite

## Instructions

1. Mash the bananas in a large bowl.
2. Add the juice concentrates, pineapple juice, water, and sugar, and stir until the sugar dissolves.
3. Freeze to a slush.
4. Add the 7-Up or Sprite when ready to serve.
