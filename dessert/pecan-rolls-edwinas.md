# Edwina's Pecan Rolls

From [Aunt Edwina](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/KWZQ-VN9) (Uncle Verl's wife)

## Ingredients

- 6 c. sugar
- 1 qt. Karo Syrup (white)
- 1½ c. canned milk
- 1 t. salt
- ⅓ c. butter
- 1 t. vanilla
- 2 lb. pecans

## Directions

- Cook to hard firm ball.
- Cook and beat.
- Add nuts and roll in nuts.
