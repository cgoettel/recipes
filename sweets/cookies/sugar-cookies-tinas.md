# Tina's sugar cookies

## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (1 cup) butter
- 1½ cups powdered sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon soda
- 1 teaspoon cream of tartar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2½ cups flour

## Instructions

1. Mix butter, egg, powdered sugar and vanilla.
2. Blend in flour, soda and cream of tartar.
3. Chill dough for 1 hour.
4. Roll out and cut into desired shapes.
5. Place on lightly greased cookie sheet and bake at 375 for 7 to 8 minutes.
