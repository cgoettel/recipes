# Angie's whole wheat bread

From Angie. Makes 6 loaves (half quantities in parens for 3 loaves).

## Ingredients

- ⅔ cup (≈130 g) vegetable oil (⅓ cup)
- ⅔ cup (≈225 g) honey (⅓ cup)
- 1–2 tablespoons liquid lecithin (optional)
- 6 cups (≈1360 g) hot tap water (3 cups)
- 3 tablespoons (≈27 g) instant yeast (2 tablespoons)
- 16–18 cups (≈1810–2035 g) ground whole wheat flour (8–9 cups)
- ½ cup potato flakes (¼ cup)
- 2 tablespoons vital wheat gluten (1 tablespoon)
- 2 tablespoons dough enhancer (1 tablespoon)
- 2 tablespoons (≈36 g) salt (1 tablespoon)
- Butter spray

## Instructions

1. In a mixer, combine oil, honey, lecithin, water, yeast, and flour. On top of the flour add the potato flakes, gluten, dough enhancer, and salt.
2. Blend until smooth and gradually add the rest of the flour until the dough no longer sticks to the sides of the bowl.
3. Use the mixer to knead (2½–3½ minutes) or hand-knead 10 minutes.
4. Divide into 6 loaves (or 3 for a half recipe). Add flavor ingredients now if making variants.
5. Shape and place in greased 8½" × 4½" bread pans. Cover with a towel and let rise 2–3 hours.
6. Bake at 350°F for 29 minutes.
7. Remove from pans immediately and cool on a rack.

## Variants

- **Apple cranberry** — Hand-knead in 2 tablespoons sugar + 1 tablespoon brown sugar + 1 tablespoon cinnamon (mixed) plus ½ cup diced apples and ¼ cup dried cranberries.
- **Sunflower** — Hand-knead in 2 tablespoons sunflower seeds and ½ tablespoon flaxseed. Garnish the top of the loaf with 1 tablespoon pumpkin seeds.
- **Cheese** — Hand-knead in 3 tablespoons ranch dressing mix and 1 cup sharp cheddar.
