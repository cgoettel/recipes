# Australian damper bread

## Ingredients

- 4 C flour
- 1 t salt
- ⅛ C baking powder
- ⅛ C sugar
- 1 ¼ C milk

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Grease an 8"x8" pan.
3. Whisk the flour, salt, baking powder, and sugar together.
4. Add milk and stir until a stiff dough forms.
5. Press into the pan. Score the dough with a sharp knife, making a grid pattern.
6. Bake at 425°F for 20-25 minutes, or until golden brown. Serve immediately.
