# Easy meat and potatoes

## Ingredients

- 2 lb ground beef
- 1 (32 oz) package frozen Tater Tots or french fries
- 2 (10.5 oz) cans cream of chicken soup

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Press the uncooked ground beef into the bottom of a 9×13×2-inch pan.
3. Top with the potatoes, then the soup.
4. Bake for 1 hour.
