# Pumpkin chiffon pie

A magazine clipping: a light pumpkin chiffon filling in a toasted coconut and graham cracker crust, crowned with whipped cream.

## Ingredients

### Filling

- ½ cup milk
- 2 envelopes unflavored gelatin
- 1 (16 oz) can solid-pack pumpkin
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon ground ginger
- ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 4 eggs, separated
- ½ cup sugar, for the yolk mixture
- ¼ cup sugar, for the egg whites

### Crust

- 1 (3.5 oz) can flaked coconut
- ¾ cup graham cracker crumbs
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter, softened
- 1 tablespoon sugar

### To serve

- ½ cup heavy cream

## Instructions

### Filling

1. Early in the day, mix the milk and gelatin in a heavy 3-quart saucepan and let stand 1 minute.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the gelatin is completely dissolved.
3. Stir in the pumpkin, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, salt, egg yolks, and ½ cup sugar.
4. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture is very thick, about 10 minutes. Do not let it boil.
5. Refrigerate until chilled but not set, about 45 minutes.

### Crust

1. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Spread the coconut in a jelly-roll pan and bake until lightly browned, about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. In a 9-inch pie plate, mix the toasted coconut, cracker crumbs, butter, and 1 tablespoon sugar by hand.
4. Press the mixture firmly onto the bottom and side of the pie plate, just to the rim.

### Finish

1. In a small bowl, beat the egg whites with a mixer at high speed until soft peaks form.
2. Gradually sprinkle in ¼ cup sugar, beating until the sugar is completely dissolved. The whites should stand in stiff, glossy peaks.
3. With a wire whisk, gently fold the beaten whites into the pumpkin mixture.
4. Spoon into the crust and refrigerate until set, about 2 hours.
5. To serve, beat the cream until soft peaks form and use it to garnish the top of the pie.

## Notes

- Dissolve the gelatin thoroughly, or the chiffon won't hold its shape and will be hard to cut. Sprinkling it over a small amount of cold liquid first lets the granules swell, so they dissolve more easily over heat.
- Beat the egg whites only after the gelatin mixture is ready; if they wait, they deflate.
- To fold, use circular motions: slide the whisk across the pan bottom and up over the surface. Don't stir or beat, so the filling stays fluffy.
