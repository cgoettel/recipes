# Marinated vegetables

## Ingredients

### Marinade

- ½ cup red wine vinegar
- ½ cup water
- 1 teaspoon Accent (MSG)
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- Lawry's seasoned salt and pepper, to taste

### Vegetables

- 1 (14.5 oz) can green beans, drained
- ½ cup mushrooms
- 3 tablespoons dried onion flakes
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- ½ cup pimientos, sliced
- ½ cup radishes, sliced
- 1 cup zucchini, sliced
- 2 cups cauliflower, in small pieces

## Instructions

1. Mix the marinade ingredients.
2. Combine the vegetables, pour the marinade over them, and marinate overnight in the refrigerator.
