# Enchilada pie

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons butter, plus more for the tortillas and dish
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon pepper
- 1 tablespoon chili powder, or more
- 1 (4.5 oz) can chopped ripe olives
- 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
- 6 corn tortillas, lightly buttered
- ½ lb (2 cups) cheddar cheese, grated
- ⅔ cup water

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F and butter a 2-quart casserole.
2. Brown the ground beef, onion, and garlic in the butter.
3. Add the salt, pepper, chili powder, olives, and tomato sauce.
4. Set aside ½ cup of the cheese for the top.
5. In the casserole, alternate layers of buttered tortillas, meat sauce, and cheese, ending with a tortilla. Sprinkle the reserved cheese over the top tortilla.
6. Just before baking, pour the water over the casserole.
7. Cover and bake for 30 minutes.
