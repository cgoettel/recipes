# Poppy seed cake

From [Aunt Kay](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/K26F-HCV)

## Ingredients

- Yellow cake mix
- 1 package lemon instant pudding
- 4 eggs
- ¼ cup poppy seeds
- ¾ cup oil
- 1 cup water

## Instructions

1. Mix all for 3 minutes.
2. Pour into greased bundt cake pan or two loaf pans.
3. Bake at 350°F for 40–45 minutes.
