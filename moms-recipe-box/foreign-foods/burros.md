# Burros

## Ingredients

- 2–3 lb chuck roast, slow cooked and shredded
- 2 lb ground beef
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1–2 tablespoons flour
- 16 oz beef broth
- 16 oz tomato sauce
- 1 can green chiles
- 7–9 tablespoons chili powder, to taste

## Instructions

1. Slow cook the chuck roast, shred it, and set it aside.
2. Separately, cook the ground beef and onion. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Add the flour and cook it down to remove the raw flour taste.
4. Add the beef broth, tomato sauce, and green chiles.
5. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat, and let it thicken.
6. Add the shredded beef.
7. Season with chili powder to taste.
