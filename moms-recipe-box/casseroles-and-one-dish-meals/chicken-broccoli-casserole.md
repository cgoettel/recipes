# Chicken broccoli casserole

## Ingredients

- Cooked chicken
- 2 (10.5 oz) cans cream of chicken soup
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 2 (10 oz) packages frozen broccoli
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 1 cup grated cheese
- Hot cooked rice, for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Cook the broccoli and drain it.
3. Bone the chicken and cut it into bite-size pieces.
4. Combine the broccoli, chicken, soup, mayonnaise, and lemon juice in a casserole dish, and top with the grated cheese.
5. Bake until hot and bubbly and the cheese has melted.
6. Serve over rice.

## Notes

- The card gives no amount for the chicken; a whole cooked chicken, or about 3 cups, suits the rest of the recipe.
