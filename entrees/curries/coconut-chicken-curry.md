# Kerala-Style Coconut Chicken Curry

Coconut milk based, glossy, bone-in chicken thighs. Tested at the 3-thigh scale for one person to eat across multiple meals. Scales up cleanly to a full can of coconut milk and 1.5–2 lb of chicken. ~45 minutes total, mostly hands-off after the initial ~20 minutes of masala building.

Serves 1–2 across multiple meals at this scale.

## Aromatics & Whole Spices
- 1 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 small cinnamon stick (or ½ a regular one)
- 2 green cardamom pods, bruised
- 2 cloves
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 small onion (or ½ large), thinly sliced
- 1½ tsp grated ginger
- 2–3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 green chile, slit
- 6–8 fresh curry leaves

## Ground Spices
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1½ tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp Kashmiri chile
- ½ tsp coarsely cracked black pepper
- ¼ tsp garam masala (at the end)

## Body
- 1 small/medium tomato, finely chopped (or ¼ cup crushed)
- ½ can (~7 oz) full-fat coconut milk + ¼ cup water
  - OR: 5 oz Mae Ploy coconut cream + 3 oz water
- 3 bone-in skinless chicken thighs
- salt

## Finish
- cilantro
- lime wedge

## Method

Use a 10-inch sauté pan or small Dutch oven — too much surface area and the sauce reduces too fast.

Heat oil over medium-high. Bloom the whole spices 20–30 seconds. Add onion and salt; cook to deep golden brown, ~8 minutes — don't rush.

Add ginger, garlic, green chile, curry leaves; 1–2 min. Drop to medium. Add the ground spices and bloom 30 seconds — oil will redden and go fragrant.

Add tomato. Cook down hard until the oil separates from the masala, ~5 min. This is the *bhuna* — same move as in the dals ([whole](masoor-dal-whole.md), [red lentil](masoor-dal-red-lentil.md)).

Add chicken and a good pinch of salt. Stir to coat in the masala, sear briefly, 3–4 min. Pour in the coconut milk plus water. Bring to a *bare* simmer — don't hard boil coconut milk or it'll split. Partially cover, simmer 25–30 minutes until the chicken is fork-tender and the gravy has thickened.

Off heat: garam masala, lime, cilantro. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Bone-in thighs are doing real work here. Boneless thighs are a fine second. Breast will be sad and stringy — skip it.
- Curry leaves matter here in a way they don't always. If you don't have them, the dish is still good; just don't try to sub bay leaves and call it even.
- For a glossier finish: splash of coconut cream off-heat right before serving.
- Day 2 is markedly better — flavors deepen, gravy tightens up.
- Scales up cleanly — double everything for 6 thighs and a full can of coconut milk. Move to a larger Dutch oven.

## Scaling Up to Full Batch

For 1.5–2 lb bone-in skinless chicken thighs (5–6 thighs), serves 3–4:

- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 cinnamon stick, 4 cardamom pods, 4 cloves, 1 bay leaf
- 1 large onion
- 1 tbsp ginger, 5 garlic cloves, 1–2 green chiles, 10–12 curry leaves
- Double the ground spices
- 1 large tomato (or ½ cup crushed)
- 1 full can (14 oz) coconut milk + ½ can water
- Onion browning closer to 10–12 min, masala bhuna closer to 8 min

## Serve With
- Basmati rice (jeera rice or light pulao)
- Naan or roti
