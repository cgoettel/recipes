# Yellow Jell-O banana cake

## Ingredients

### Cake

- 1 (15.25 oz) box white cake mix, plus what the box calls for
- 1 (3 oz) package orange Jell-O
- ¾ cup hot water
- ¾ cup cold water

### Frosting

- 1 (3.4 oz) package instant banana pudding
- 1 (2.6 oz) package Dream Whip
- 1–2 bananas, sliced

## Instructions

1. Bake the cake in a 9×13-inch pan according to the box. Let it cool.
2. Dissolve the Jell-O in the hot water, then stir in the cold water. Let it stand at room temperature while the cake bakes.
3. Poke holes all over the cooled cake and pour the Jell-O over it.
4. For the frosting, mix the pudding and Dream Whip together with the milk their boxes call for, and add the banana slices.
5. Frost the cake and refrigerate.
