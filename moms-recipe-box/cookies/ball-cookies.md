# Ball cookies

## Ingredients

- 1 cup shortening
- ½ cup sugar, plus more for rolling
- ½ cup brown sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 teaspoon cream of tartar
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 2 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Cream the shortening and sugars, then beat in the egg, salt, and vanilla.
3. Mix in the cream of tartar, baking soda, and flour.
4. Roll into balls and roll them in sugar.
5. Bake 8 minutes.
