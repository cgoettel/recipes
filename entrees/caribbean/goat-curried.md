# Curried goat

## Ingredients

- 2 lb lean goat meat
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- ½ cup chopped onion
- 2 teaspoons minced garlic
- 3 tablespoons green seasoning
- 2 tablespoons cooking oil
- 3 tablespoons curry powder
- 2 bay leaves
- 1½ cups hot water

## Instructions

1. Trim, wash and cut meat in bite-size pieces.
2. Season well with salt, black pepper, onion, garlic and green seasoning.
3. Heat oil in a heavy pot.
4. Mix curry powder and bay leaves in ¼ cup water; add to hot oil and fry for a minute.
5. Add meat and sauté for 10 minutes stirring occasionally to coat meat well.
6. Add water to meat and bring to a boil; cover and simmer on low heat until meat is tender.
7. Adjust salt and hot pepper.

Serve with boiled rice or roti.
