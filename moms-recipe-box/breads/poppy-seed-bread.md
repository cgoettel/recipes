# Poppy seed bread

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- 2 cups sugar
- 1½ cups vegetable oil
- 3 cups flour
- 1½ teaspoons baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 (13 oz) can evaporated milk
- 1 small box poppy seeds

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F. Do not grease the loaf pans.
2. Mix all the ingredients together.
3. Divide between two loaf pans and bake for 1 hour 15 minutes.
4. Let the loaves cool in the pans.

## Notes

- Evaporated milk now comes in 12 oz cans, which works fine.
