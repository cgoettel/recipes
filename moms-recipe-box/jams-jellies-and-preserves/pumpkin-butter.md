# Pumpkin butter

## Ingredients

- 1 (29 oz) can pumpkin
- 1 tablespoon pumpkin pie spice
- 1 package powdered pectin
- 4½ cups sugar

## Instructions

1. Put the pumpkin in a large saucepan. Add the spice and pectin and mix well.
2. Stir over high heat until the mixture comes to a hard boil.
3. Stir in the sugar all at once. Bring to a full rolling boil and boil 1 minute, stirring constantly.
4. Remove from the heat and ladle into hot jars.

## Notes

- Pumpkin butter is too dense to can safely at home. Store the jars in the refrigerator or freezer rather than on the shelf.
