# Au gratin potatoes

## Ingredients

- 3 lb potatoes
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- ½ cup flour
- 1 quart milk
- ½ lb cheese, grated
- 1 small onion, grated
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Boil the potatoes, cool, and dice. Place in a greased baking dish.
2. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
3. Cook the butter and flour together. Heat the milk and add it to the flour mixture. Cook until thick.
4. Stir the cheese, salt, pepper, and grated onion into the white sauce, and pour over the potatoes.
5. Bake 25 minutes.
