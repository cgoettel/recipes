# Whole wheat hotcakes

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups sifted whole wheat flour
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons raw sugar
- 2 egg yolks
- 1½ cups milk
- 3 tablespoons oil
- 2 egg whites, beaten

## Instructions

1. Combine the ingredients in order, folding in the beaten egg whites last.
2. Cook on a hot, lightly greased griddle.

## Notes

- These are sweeter than the usual hotcake. Cut back the sugar if you like.
