# American cheese spread

A sandwich spread.

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon French dressing
- 6 oz American cheese, softened
- ¼ cup finely chopped black olives
- ¼ teaspoon Worcestershire sauce

## Instructions

1. Blend the French dressing into the softened cheese.
2. Add the olives and Worcestershire and mix thoroughly.
3. Refrigerate for 30 minutes to blend the flavors.

## Notes

- For a cream cheese version, use a 3 oz package of cream cheese in place of the American cheese, and ¼ cup chopped pimiento-stuffed green olives in place of the black olives.
