# Whole wheat bread (big batch)

A large mixer batch.

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons yeast
- 5 cups warm water
- 1 cup oil
- 1 cup honey
- 2 tablespoons salt
- 12 cups whole wheat flour

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water.
2. Add the oil, honey, salt, and 6 cups of the flour, and mix for 8 minutes.
3. Add the remaining 6 cups of flour and knead in the mixer for 5–10 minutes, until smooth and elastic.
4. Shape into loaves in greased loaf pans and let rise until doubled.
5. Bake at 350°F for 40 minutes.

## Notes

- The card doesn't give a yield; 12 cups of flour makes about 5 standard loaves.
- The card says "6 cups more flour" for the second addition without naming the kind; use whole wheat, or all-purpose for a lighter loaf.
