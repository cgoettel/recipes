# Lamb with Chick Peas (Channa)

## Serves

8

## Ingredients

- 1/2 lb. chick peas
- 2 tbsp. vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1 tsp. minced garlic
- 1" fresh ginger root, crushed
- 1 tsp. garam massala
- 1 tsp. ground cumin seeds
- 1 tsp. chilli powder
- 1 1/2 lbs. boneless lamb, cubed
- 1/2 cup chopped tomatoes
- 2 cups water
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- salt to taste

## Method

1. Soak chick peas in water overnight.
2. Parboil in water and drain.
3. Heat oil in a heavy pot and fry the onion until softened.
4. Add garlic and ginger and fry for another minute.
5. Add the massala, cumin seeds, chilli powder, lamb and tomatoes.
6. Cook for 15 minutes.
7. Add water, lemon juice, chick peas and salt; stir well.
8. Lower heat, cover and simmer until meat is tender and sauce is thickened.

## Method

1. Soak chick peas overnight. Drain.
2. Pressure cook or boil until tender.
3. Heat oil in a heavy pot; add onion, garlic and ginger and fry until onion is tender.
4. Add garam massala, cumin and chilli powder. Stir and fry for a minute.
5. Add lamb and cook until browned.
6. Add tomatoes, chick peas, water and salt to taste.
7. Bring to a boil, lower heat, cover and simmer until lamb is tender.
8. Add lemon juice and mix well.

Serve with rice or roti.
