# Vanilla Syrup

A simple 1:1 syrup infused with a whole vanilla bean. Pull the bean once it's cool so the infusion doesn't turn bitter, and save the spent pod for vanilla sugar.

## Ingredients

- 1 vanilla bean, split
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup water

## Instructions

1. Combine everything in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the sugar has dissolved.
2. Remove from the heat, let cool, then lift out the vanilla bean.
