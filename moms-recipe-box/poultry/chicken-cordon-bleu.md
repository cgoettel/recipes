# Chicken cordon bleu

A ham and cheese sandwich with chicken as the bread.

## Ingredients

- 2 large chicken breast halves, skinned and boned
- 2 slices boiled or baked ham
- 2 slices Swiss cheese
- 1 egg, beaten
- ⅓ cup crushed cornflakes
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice, plus more if needed
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Sandwich each chicken breast between 2 pieces of wax paper and pound until the cutlets are ¼ inch thick.
2. Put a slice of ham and cheese on each and roll up like a jelly roll.
3. Dip each roll into the egg, then the crumbs, pressing them on to make them stick.
4. Melt the butter with a little of the lemon juice in a skillet. Brown the rolls slowly, 20–25 minutes, seasoning to taste.
5. Remove the cutlets. Add the rest of the lemon juice to the pan juices, boil, and scrape up all the browned bits.
6. Pour the pan sauce over the cutlets.

## Notes

- Wiener schnitzel variation: make a thicker crust by dipping the cutlet into egg, then flour, egg again, and finally bread crumbs. Brown in butter and serve with lemon juice and capers.
