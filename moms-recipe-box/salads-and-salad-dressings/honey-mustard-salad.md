# Honey mustard salad

The card lists ingredients without amounts.

## Ingredients

### Dressing

- Mayonnaise
- Milk
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- Honey

### Salad

- Romaine and red leaf lettuce
- Chopped bacon
- Chopped hard-boiled egg

## Instructions

1. Start with the Dijon mustard, then whisk in mayonnaise, a splash of milk, and honey to taste.
2. Toss the lettuces with the dressing and top with the bacon and egg.
