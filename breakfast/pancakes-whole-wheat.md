# Whole wheat pancakes

From Grandma Grover

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup whole wheat
- 1 cup milk
- 2 eggs
- 4 tablespoons oil
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- about ¼ cup white flour

## Instructions

1. Blend milk and wheat for four minutes
2. Add other ingredients
3. Cook on skillet
