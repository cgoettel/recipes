# Chicken rolls

Crescent rolls stuffed with creamy chicken and mushrooms, rolled in a walnut-stuffing crust, and served with a cream-of-chicken gravy.

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 2 (3 oz) packages cream cheese
- 2 tablespoons butter, melted
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper
- ¼ teaspoon seasoned salt
- 2 cups cooked cubed chicken
- 2 (4 oz) cans mushrooms, drained and diced
- 2 (8 oz) packages crescent rolls

### Coating

- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter, melted
- ½ cup walnuts, chopped
- 1 cup Pepperidge Farm seasoned stuffing, crushed

### Gravy

- 2 (10.5 oz) cans cream of chicken soup
- ½ soup can milk
- Sweet basil
- Poultry seasoning

## Instructions

### Filling

1. Melt the cream cheese, butter, pepper, and seasoned salt together over low heat.
2. Add the chicken and mushrooms and set aside.
3. Preheat the oven to 325°F and grease a cookie sheet.
4. Separate the crescent rolls and roll each one out slightly with a rolling pin.
5. Fill each with about ¼ cup of the filling, fold the dough over, and seal the edges.

### Coating and baking

1. Mix the walnuts and stuffing crumbs.
2. Roll each chicken roll in the melted butter, then in the walnut-stuffing mixture.
3. Lay the rolls on the cookie sheet and bake for about 30 minutes, until golden brown.

### Gravy

1. Heat the soup, milk, and a pinch each of basil and poultry seasoning over low heat, stirring until smooth.
2. Pour the gravy over the rolls to serve.
