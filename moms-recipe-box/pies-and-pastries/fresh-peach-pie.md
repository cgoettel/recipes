# Fresh peach pie

## Ingredients

- 1 (9-inch) baked pie shell
- 1 cup sugar (or as little as ½ cup)
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 3 tablespoons peach Jell-O
- 1 cup water
- Dash salt
- ¼ teaspoon orange food coloring
- Fresh peaches, sliced

## Instructions

1. Combine the sugar, cornstarch, gelatin, water, salt, and food coloring in a saucepan.
2. Bring to a boil and boil for 1 minute.
3. Set the pan in a Pyrex bowl of ice for about 10 minutes, stirring every few minutes, until it is cool enough to put your finger in. Make sure it is at room temperature.
4. Arrange the sliced peaches in the pie shell and spoon the glaze over them.
5. Chill for 1 hour, then serve.

## Notes

- The card does not name a crust. The press-in crust from the fresh strawberry pie, whose glaze is nearly the same, suits it.
