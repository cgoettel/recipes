# Zucchini bread with pecans

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 2 cups zucchini, cut in chunks
- 1 cup oil
- 2 cups brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon vanilla
- 3 cups flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 tablespoon cinnamon
- 1 cup pecans, chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F and grease three small loaf pans.
2. Beat the eggs until foamy.
3. Purée the zucchini in a blender with the oil, adding the zucchini a little at a time.
4. Add the brown sugar, vanilla, and zucchini mixture to the eggs.
5. Mix the flour, salt, baking powder, baking soda, and cinnamon, and stir them in with the pecans.
6. Pour into the pans and bake for 1 hour 15 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
