# Poppy seed sugar cookies (Haman's hats)

**Source:** http://www.ou.org/life/food/recipes/guide-delicious-hamantaschen-norene-gilletz/

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- ¾ cup sugar
- ½ cup oil
- ¼ cup orange juice or water
- 3 cups flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- Additional sugar for dipping

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Mix eggs with sugar, oil, and juice until blended.
3. Add baking powder and flour. Mix. Do not over-process.
4. Divide dough into 4 pieces. Roll each piece on a lightly floured surface into a rectangle about &# x215B;" thick. Using assorted cookie cutters, cut in different shapes. Dip each cookie lightly in sugar. Place sugar-side up on parchment-lined cookie sheets.
5. Bake at 375°F for 8–10 minutes, until golden.

Variation:

- Add ¼ cup poppy seeds with dry ingredients. Roll out dough thinly, then cut in triangles using a fluted pastry wheel or pizza cutter. Dip in sugar and bake as directed. Perfect for Purim!
