# Peppermint supreme dessert

## Ingredients

- 1 pint whipping cream
- 1 package chocolate chip cookies
- 1 cup crushed peppermint candy
- 2 cups miniature marshmallows

## Instructions

1. Crush the cookies. Spread a thin layer of crumbs in the bottom of a 9×9-inch pan, saving the rest.
2. Whip the cream. Fold in the crushed candy and marshmallows.
3. Spread over the crumbs very carefully so they aren't disturbed.
4. Sprinkle the remaining crumbs over the top and freeze.
