# Bisquick dumplings

## Ingredients

- 2 cups Bisquick baking mix
- ⅔ cup milk

## Instructions

1. Mix the Bisquick and milk until a soft dough forms.
2. Drop by spoonfuls onto boiling stew.
3. Cook uncovered over low heat for 10 minutes, then cover and cook 10 minutes more.

## Notes

- For no-cholesterol dumplings, use skim milk.
- No adjustments needed at high altitude.
