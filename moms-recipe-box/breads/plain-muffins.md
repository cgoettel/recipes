# Plain muffins

A small basic batch, with variations on the back of the card.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sifted flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1½ teaspoons baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 egg, well beaten
- ½ cup milk
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F and grease a muffin tin.
2. Sift the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt together into one bowl.
3. In another bowl, beat the egg, then beat in the milk and oil.
4. Stir everything together just until combined; the batter should be lumpy.
5. Fill the muffin cups two-thirds full and bake for 20–25 minutes.

## Notes

- Raisin, date, nut, or blueberry: stir ¼ cup of one of them quickly into the batter.
- Jelly: make a small hole in each muffin and spoon in 1 teaspoon jelly before baking.
- Apple: lay an apple slice on top of each muffin before baking.
- Orange: dip a sugar cube quickly into orange juice and press it into the top of the batter before baking.
- Cheese: add ¼ cup grated cheese to the dry ingredients.
