# Hot artichoke dip

## Ingredients

- 1 C Parmesan cheese, grated
- ½ C mayonnaise
- ½ C sour cream
- 1 can artichoke hearts in water, drained, chopped
- 1 t garlic
- 1½ t dill weed

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Mix everything together.
3. Pour into a small, greased casserole dish (9"x9" works).
4. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes or until the top is golden brown.
