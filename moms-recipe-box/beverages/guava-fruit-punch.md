# Guava fruit punch

## Ingredients

- 2 (7.1 oz) cans guava juice
- 1½ cups unsweetened pineapple juice
- 1 cup fresh orange juice
- ¾ cup fresh lemon juice
- ¼ cup sugar
- 3 tablespoons grenadine syrup
- 1 (28 oz) bottle ginger ale, chilled
- Assorted fresh fruit, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Combine everything except the ginger ale in a 2½-quart container and stir until the sugar dissolves. Chill.
2. Just before serving, add the ginger ale.
3. Pour over an ice ring in a punch bowl and garnish with fresh fruit.
