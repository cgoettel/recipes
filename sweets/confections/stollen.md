# Stollen

From [Patrick Ryan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YFEa1QMaBoo&t=317s). 4 hours 40 minutes total. Makes 3 large or 6 small stollen.

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 1 kg strong white bread flour
- 20 g fresh yeast
- 500 g milk (at body temperature)
- 200 g unsalted butter
- 50 g caster sugar
- 10 g salt
- 220 g egg (shelled — ~4 large eggs)
- 1 batch frangipane (below)
- 400 g natural, uncolored marzipan

### Filling

- 180 g sultanas
- 100 g natural glacé cherries
- 200 g mixed peel
- 60 g toasted flaked almonds
- 4 T rum
- 1 t cinnamon

### Frangipane

- 200 g ground almonds
- 200 g caster sugar
- 200 g unsalted butter
- 2 large eggs
- 50 g flour
- 2 T rum

### Glaze

- 100 g unsalted butter
- 2 capfuls of rum
- Icing sugar for dusting

## Instructions

1. Place the flour in a mixing bowl and crumble in the yeast with your fingers. Mix the milk, butter, sugar, salt, and eggs together and add to the mixer with the dough hook. Mix on low for 4 minutes, then increase a speed for another 10 minutes.
1. Combine the filling ingredients in a bowl, then add to the dough and mix for a final minute to incorporate.
1. Turn out onto a work surface and briefly work to ensure even distribution. Place in a bowl, cover, and prove 45 minutes.
1. While the dough proves, make the frangipane: beat the sugar and butter in a bowl with a wooden spoon until pale. Add the ground almonds and beat until fully incorporated. Beat in the flour, then the eggs one at a time. Finally add the rum. Set aside.
1. Portion the marzipan into 12 sausage-shape pieces.
1. Turn the proven dough onto a lightly floured surface and divide into six pieces.
1. Flatten each piece into a rectangle. Spread a generous spoonful of frangipane on each, then lay 2 pieces of marzipan over the top.
1. Fold the top long edge over one piece of marzipan and the other long edge over the other. Seal at the edges and gently shape into a loaf.
1. Place on a parchment-lined baking tray, cover, and prove 45 minutes.
1. A wood-fired oven hovering around 200°C is ideal — adjust time and check often if your oven fluctuates.
1. Brush each stollen with egg wash and bake ~30–35 minutes.
1. Transfer to a rack and cool fully before glazing.
1. Melt the butter and rum together and pour into a large deep dish. Roll each cooled stollen in the rum butter, shake off any excess, then roll in icing sugar.

## Notes

- Patrick Ryan's [fruit mincemeat recipe](https://www.ilovecooking.ie/recipe-items/patrick-ryans-fruit-mincemeat/) pairs with this — the filling above is a streamlined version.
- Wrap and store, eat fresh, or freeze.
