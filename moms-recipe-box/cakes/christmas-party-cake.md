# Christmas party cake

## Ingredients

- 1 (15.25 oz) box white cake mix, plus what the box calls for
- 1 (3 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream, whipped

## Instructions

1. Bake the cake in a 9×13-inch pan according to the box, and cool.
2. Beat the cream cheese, then add the powdered sugar.
3. Blend in the whipped cream.
4. Frost the cake and refrigerate.

## Notes

- For the party, a raspberry topping went on at the church just before serving.
