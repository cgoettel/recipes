# Hot pastrami and Swiss dip

Serve with relishes and crackers.

## Ingredients

- 2 (8 oz) packages cream cheese
- Whole milk
- 2 bunches green onions, chopped
- 2 packages thin-sliced deli meat (dried beef and pastrami), cut in small pieces
- 1 lb Swiss cheese, shredded

## Instructions

1. Melt the cream cheese down with a little milk over low heat.
2. Add the green onions and meat.
3. Add the Swiss cheese and stir until melted.
4. Stir in more milk until the mixture is creamy.
