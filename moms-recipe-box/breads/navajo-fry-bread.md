# Navajo fry bread

## Ingredients

- 4 cups flour
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1¾ cups warm water
- Oil or shortening, for frying

## Instructions

1. Mix the dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
2. Stir in the warm water, then mix and knead with your hands for 5 minutes.
3. Heat about an inch of oil in a heavy skillet to 350–375°F.
4. Pull off balls of dough, pat or stretch them into thin rounds, and fry one or two at a time, turning once, until puffed and golden on both sides. Drain on paper towels.
