# Banana pie

## Ingredients

- 1 baked pie shell
- 1 (3.4 oz) package instant banana pudding mix
- 1½ cups milk
- 1 cup heavy cream
- ½ cup powdered sugar
- 4–5 bananas
- ½ cup water
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Prepare the pudding mix according to the package, using 1½ cups milk.
2. Whip the cream and add the powdered sugar. Fold it into the pudding.
3. Slice the bananas and dip them in the lemon juice and water. Drain on a paper towel and blot dry.
4. Line the bottom of the crust with bananas, fill with the pudding mixture, then arrange more bananas on top.

## Notes

- The card sketches the top: a ring of overlapping slices around the edge and a small cluster in the center.
