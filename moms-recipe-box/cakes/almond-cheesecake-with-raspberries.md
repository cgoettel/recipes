# Almond cheesecake with raspberries

A no-bake cheesecake from a Philadelphia cream cheese wrapper. Prep time is 30 minutes plus chilling.

## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups graham cracker crumbs
- ⅓ cup butter, melted
- ¼ cup sugar
- 2 (8 oz) packages Philadelphia cream cheese, softened
- 1 (16 oz) can ready-to-spread vanilla frosting
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon grated lemon peel
- 3 cups Cool Whip, thawed
- Raspberries
- Sliced almonds

## Instructions

1. Stir together the crumbs, butter, and sugar in a small bowl. Press onto the bottom and ½ inch up the sides of a 9-inch springform pan or pie plate. Chill.
2. Beat the cream cheese, frosting, lemon juice, and lemon peel in a large bowl with an electric mixer on medium speed until well blended.
3. Fold in the Cool Whip and pour over the crust. Chill until firm.
4. Arrange raspberries and almonds on top.
