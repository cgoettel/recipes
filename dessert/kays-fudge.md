# Kay's fudge
This recipe is from a tombstone in Utah.

## Ingredients
- 2 oz chocolate
- 2 T butter
- 1 C milk
- 3 C sugar
- 1 T vanilla
- pinch salt

## Process
- Get your mise en place; this is essential
- Melt butter and chocolate on low heat
- Once melted, stir in milk and bring to a boil
- Add sugar, vanilla, and salt. Stir to incorporate. Once the mixture reaches around 190F, stop stirring. If you keep stirring, sugar crystals will form and the fudge will be gross.
- Cook to softball stage (234-240F)
- Pour on a marble slab and let cool to 110F
- Once cooled, beat the fudge until it is no longer glossy, shape it, and let it set and cool completely.
- Cut into pieces
- Fudge can be stored in an airtight container on the counter for one week
