# Souper burgers

Sloppy-joe-style ground beef in mushroom soup.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- ½ cup chopped onion
- 1 (10.5 oz) can cream of mushroom soup
- 2 tablespoons ketchup
- 1 tablespoon mustard
- 4 buns, split and toasted

## Instructions

1. In a frying pan, brown the beef and cook the onion until tender, adding a little shortening if needed. Stir to break up the meat.
2. Add the soup, ketchup, and mustard.
3. Heat, stirring often.
4. Serve on the toasted buns.
