# Ginger Syrup

A steeped fresh-ginger syrup. The short simmer extracts ginger quickly; the long cold steep afterward builds heat and depth without cooking the brightness out. Slicing thin (rather than juicing) keeps it clear and lets you control intensity by steep time.

## Ingredients

- 1 ½ cups (360 ml) white sugar
- 1 cup (240 ml) water
- 250 g fresh ginger

## Instructions

1. Peel the ginger with the edge of a spoon (optional) and slice into small pieces.
2. In a medium saucepan, combine the sugar and water and stir over heat until the sugar is completely dissolved.
3. Add the sliced ginger and simmer 3–5 minutes.
4. Off the heat, seal and steep in the fridge for up to 24 hours.
5. Strain through a fine-mesh strainer once it's done steeping.
