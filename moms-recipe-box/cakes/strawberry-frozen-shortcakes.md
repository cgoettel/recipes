# Strawberry frozen shortcakes

From a Dream Whip advertisement.

## Ingredients

- 1 pint strawberries
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 envelope Dream Whip whipped topping mix, plus milk as the package directs
- ⅛ teaspoon almond extract
- 6 individual sponge cake shells

## Instructions

1. Slice the strawberries, sprinkle with the sugar, and chill.
2. Prepare the Dream Whip as directed on the package, using the almond extract in place of the vanilla.
3. Spoon about ⅓ cup of the whipped topping into each sponge cake shell, forming a cone shape (a pastry bag works well).
4. Freeze until firm, about 1½ hours.
5. About 15 minutes before serving, take the shortcakes out of the freezer. Top with the strawberries.
