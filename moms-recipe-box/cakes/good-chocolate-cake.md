# Good chocolate cake

## Ingredients

- 1 large package cook-and-serve chocolate pudding, plus the milk it calls for
- 1 (15.25 oz) box chocolate cake mix
- ½ cup chopped nuts
- ½ cup chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a square pan.
2. Cook the pudding according to the package directions.
3. Stir the dry cake mix into the hot pudding.
4. Spread in the pan and sprinkle with the nuts and chocolate chips.
5. Bake for 30 minutes.

## Notes

- A whole cake mix makes a thick cake in a square pan; a 9×13-inch pan works too and may bake a little faster.
