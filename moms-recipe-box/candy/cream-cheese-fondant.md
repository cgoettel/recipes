# Cream cheese fondant

## Ingredients

- 3 cups powdered sugar
- 1 (3 oz) package Philadelphia cream cheese
- 2 tablespoons butter
- Flavorings, nuts, and maraschino cherries

## Instructions

1. Mix the powdered sugar, cream cheese, and butter together and form into a ball.
2. Divide the ball into 3 balls.
3. Press a thumbprint into each ball and add a flavoring, nuts, or chopped maraschino cherries.
4. Knead each ball, roll into marble-size pieces, and freeze.
