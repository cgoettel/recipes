# Easy white bread

A no-knead batter bread, baked in a loaf pan or a round casserole.

## Ingredients

- 4–4½ cups unsifted all-purpose flour
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 packages (4½ teaspoons) active dry yeast
- 1 cup water
- 1 cup milk
- ¼ cup vegetable oil
- 1 egg
- 1 tablespoon melted butter (optional)
- 2 tablespoons coarse salt (optional)

## Instructions

1. Grease a 9×5×3-inch loaf pan or a 2-quart round casserole dish.
2. Combine 2 cups of the flour with the sugar, salt, and yeast.
3. Heat the water, milk, and oil until very warm (120–130°F).
4. Add the warm liquid and the egg to the flour mixture. With a mixer, blend, then beat for 3 minutes.
5. With a wooden spoon, beat in the remaining 2–2½ cups flour to form a stiff batter.
6. Cover and let rise until doubled, about 50–60 minutes.
7. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
8. Stir the dough down vigorously and spoon it into the prepared pan. Bake for 45–50 minutes.
9. Remove from the pan immediately. Brush with melted butter and sprinkle with coarse salt if you like.
