# Pork chop bean bake

## Ingredients

- 4 pork chops, ¾ inch thick
- 1 (10.5 oz) can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 (10 oz) package frozen green beans, thawed
- ⅓ cup milk or cream
- Dash of crushed thyme leaves
- ¼ teaspoon pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Brown the pork chops in a skillet.
3. Place the green beans in a casserole and top with the pork chops.
4. Mix the soup, milk, thyme, and pepper, and pour over the chops.
5. Cover and bake for 1 hour.
