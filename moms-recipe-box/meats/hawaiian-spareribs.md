# Hawaiian spareribs

Good served with fluffy rice.

## Ingredients

- 2 large packages country-style spareribs
- 1 cup puréed canned peaches
- ⅓ cup ketchup
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- ½ cup brown sugar
- 2 cloves garlic, grated
- 2 teaspoons ginger
- 1 teaspoon salt
- Pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 450°F. Roast the ribs 30 minutes and drain off the fat.
2. Meanwhile, mix the peaches, ketchup, sugar, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, salt, and pepper.
3. Turn the oven down to 350°F. Pour the sauce over the ribs and bake 1–1½ hours, turning once or twice.
