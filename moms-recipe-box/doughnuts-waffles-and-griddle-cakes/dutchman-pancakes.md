# Dutchman pancakes (Dutch baby)

Makes one round cake pan.

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- ½ cup flour
- ½ cup milk
- Nutmeg
- 3 tablespoons butter

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Put the butter in a round cake pan and set it in the oven to melt.
2. Mix the eggs, flour, milk, and a grating of nutmeg with a fork. The batter will be lumpy.
3. Pour the batter into the hot pan of butter.
4. Bake 15 minutes.
