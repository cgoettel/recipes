# Wheat quick mix

A homemade whole wheat baking mix, with biscuits, pancakes, and waffles made from it.

## Ingredients

### Mix

- 4 cups whole wheat flour
- 4 cups white flour
- ⅓ cup baking powder
- 4 teaspoons salt
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 cups shortening
- 1¼ cups dry milk

### Biscuits

- 2 cups wheat mix
- ⅔ cup milk
- Oil, for dipping

### Pancakes

- 2 cups wheat mix
- 1 egg
- 2 cups water

### Waffles

- 2 cups wheat mix
- 1 egg
- 2 cups water
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

## Instructions

### Mix

1. Combine the flours, baking powder, salt, sugar, shortening, and dry milk in a large mixing bowl, and knead until well blended.
2. Store covered.

### Biscuits

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F and grease a cookie sheet.
2. Stir the milk into the mix with a fork to make a soft dough.
3. Turn out onto a floured board, knead gently, and roll 1 inch thick.
4. Cut with a biscuit cutter and place on the cookie sheet. Dip the top of each biscuit in oil and turn it right side up.
5. Bake for 10–15 minutes. Makes 12 biscuits.

### Pancakes

1. Put everything in a bowl and blend.
2. Cook on a heated griddle. Makes sixteen 4-inch pancakes.

### Waffles

1. Put everything in a bowl and mix.
2. Bake in a hot waffle iron. Makes 2 large waffles.
