# Rolled Sugar Cookies

## Ingredients

- 1/2 C. butter - softened
- 1 C. sugar
- 1 egg
- 1/4 C. milk
- 1/2 teas. vanilla
- 2 1/4 C. flour
- 2 teas. baking powder

## Instructions

1. Cream butter and sugar together.
2. Add egg, milk & vanilla and beat well.
3. Blend in flour and baking powder.
4. Chill dough.
5. Roll out dough 1/4 inch thickness on flour surface.
6. Cut & ...and serve with love!
7. Bake 11-12 mins at 350.
