# Pineapple coconut sherbet dessert

## Ingredients

- ½ gallon pineapple sherbet
- 1 (8 oz) tub Cool Whip
- 2 cups chopped pecans
- 1 package Archway coconut macaroons (or another brand), crumbled

## Instructions

1. Soften the sherbet.
2. Mix in the Cool Whip, pecans, and crumbled macaroons.
3. Spread in a 9×13-inch pan and freeze. Make it at least a day ahead so it's sure to be frozen.
