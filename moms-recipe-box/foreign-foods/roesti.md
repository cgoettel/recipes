# Rösti (Swiss potato pancakes)

Made from cooked, not raw, potatoes. The recipe works best when the potatoes are cooked 24 hours ahead.

## Ingredients

- 2 large baking potatoes
- 2 scallions
- Salt and fresh black pepper
- 6 tablespoons clarified butter, or 3 tablespoons oil and 3 tablespoons butter

## Instructions

1. Put the potatoes in a pan with cold water to cover. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer 20–30 minutes, until a skewer pierces them easily.
2. Run cold water over the potatoes to cool them, then refrigerate at least 24 hours (up to 4 days).
3. Peel the potatoes and coarsely grate them. Finely chop the scallions and add them with salt and pepper to taste.
4. Heat the fat in two 6-inch cast-iron or nonstick skillets (or one large pan) over high heat. Divide the potato mixture between the pans.
5. Lower the heat and press the potatoes into cakes. Cook over medium-low heat 5–6 minutes per side, shaking the pans now and then so they don't stick.

## Notes

- Temperature control is the secret: the potatoes must go into hot fat or they will stick, but they must cook over low heat or they will burn.
