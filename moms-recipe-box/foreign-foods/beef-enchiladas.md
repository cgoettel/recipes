# Beef enchiladas

## Ingredients

### Tortillas

- 2 dozen corn tortillas
- 1 cup oil per dozen tortillas, for frying

### Sauce

- A little oil
- 1 (19 oz) can enchilada sauce
- 1 can water (more if the sauce is too spicy)
- Salt and garlic powder, to taste
- ½ cup flour
- Salt and pepper

### Filling

- 1–2 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 (2.25 oz) can sliced olives, chopped
- 2 cups grated cheese

## Instructions

### Sauce

1. Put a little oil in the bottom of a saucepan. Add the enchilada sauce, water, salt, and garlic powder, and heat until hot but not boiling.
2. Stir the flour, salt, pepper, and enough water together to make a slightly thick but not runny gravy mixture.
3. Pour the gravy mixture slowly into the sauce, stirring, until the sauce is just thick enough to cling to a tortilla.

### Filling and assembly

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Brown the ground beef and drain it, then mix in the onions and olives.
3. Fry the tortillas in the oil and drain them right before assembling.
4. Dip each tortilla in the sauce, fill it with the beef mixture, roll it, and place it in a baking pan. Repeat until finished.
5. Top with the remaining sauce and the cheese.
6. Bake until the cheese is melted.
