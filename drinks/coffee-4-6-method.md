# 4:6 method

From [Tetsu Kasuya](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmCW8xSWGZY), 2016 World Brewers Cup champion. A single, deliberate cup that shines with clean, bright, washed coffees.

## Ratios

- 1:15 → 20 g coffee : 300 mL water
- First 40% (120 g, two pours) sets acidity vs. sweetness; last 60% (180 g, three pours) sets strength.

## Instructions

Using a Hario Switch with the valve open the whole time — or a V60; the brew is the same:

1. Heat water to 92–96°C (hotter for lighter roasts)
2. Grind a step finer than the [hybrid method](coffee-new-hybrid-method.md); dial by drawdown, aiming to finish near 3:30
3. 0:00: Pour to 70 g and let it bloom
4. 0:45: Pour to 120 g
5. 1:30: Pour to 180 g
6. 2:15: Pour to 240 g
7. 3:00: Pour to 300 g
8. 3:30: target finish

## Notes

- The 70 g / 50 g split of the first two pours leans bright — the right call for clean, high-acid coffees. For a sweeter, rounder cup, flip it to 50 g / 70 g. The split is the dial; trust the cup over the numbers.
- The last three pours (60 g each) set strength: fewer, larger pours build body; even, gentle pours keep clarity.
- Finishes well under 3:30? Grind finer. Drags past it? Grind coarser.
- Scale up for two cups at the same 1:15 — 30 g coffee : 450 mL water (40% = 180 g, last 60% = 270 g).
