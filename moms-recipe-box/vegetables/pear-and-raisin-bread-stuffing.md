# Pear and raisin bread stuffing

## Ingredients

- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter
- 1 large celery stalk, diced
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 (16 oz) can diced pears, drained, ½ cup juice saved
- 1 (16 oz) loaf raisin bread, cubed and dried
- ½ teaspoon salt
- Pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and butter a baking dish.
2. Melt the butter in a skillet and sauté the celery and onion until tender.
3. Toss the vegetables with the pears, bread cubes, saved pear juice, salt, and pepper until well mixed.
4. Place in the baking dish and bake for 40–45 minutes.

## Notes

- A second card in the box doubles it for a crowd: 1 stick (½ cup) butter, 2 celery stalks, 1 large onion, a 32 oz can of pears with 1 cup of their juice, 2 loaves of cinnamon raisin bread, and 1 teaspoon seasoned salt in place of the salt. Bake it in its own dish alongside the bird, same time and temperature.
