# Taco soup

## Ingredients

### Soup

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 (1 oz) packet taco seasoning
- 2 (15 oz) cans kidney beans
- 2 (15 oz) cans corn, with juice
- 1 (2.25 oz) can sliced olives, drained
- 1 (29 oz) can tomato sauce
- 1 (14.5 oz) can stewed tomatoes

### Toppings

- Shredded cheese
- Chopped onion
- Tortilla chips

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef with the onion and drain.
2. Add the taco seasoning, beans, corn, olives, tomato sauce, and stewed tomatoes.
3. Heat through.
4. Serve topped with cheese, onion, and tortilla chips.
