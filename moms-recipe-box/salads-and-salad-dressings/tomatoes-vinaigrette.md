# Tomatoes vinaigrette

## Ingredients

- 12 thick tomato slices
- 1 cup olive oil
- ⅓ cup red wine vinegar
- 2 teaspoons crushed dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon pepper
- ½ teaspoon dry mustard
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 6 lettuce cups
- Minced green onion
- Minced parsley

## Instructions

1. Arrange the tomato slices in an 8×8-inch baking dish.
2. Combine the oil, vinegar, oregano, salt, pepper, mustard, and garlic, and spoon over the tomatoes.
3. Cover and chill 2–3 hours, spooning the dressing over the tomatoes occasionally.
4. To serve, arrange the tomato slices in the lettuce cups and sprinkle with green onion and parsley. Drizzle each salad with a little of the dressing.
