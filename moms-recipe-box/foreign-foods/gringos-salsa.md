# Baldwin gringo salsa

A fresh small batch on the front of the card, and a large canning batch on the back.

## Ingredients

### Small batch

- 1 (28 oz) can whole tomatoes
- 1 (4 oz) can diced green chilies
- 2 yellow hot peppers
- ¼ bunch cilantro
- ½ bunch green onions
- ¾ teaspoon garlic
- ¾ teaspoon salt, or less

### Canning batch

- 1 large mixing bowl full of prepared tomatoes
- 8 jalapeños, chopped
- 5 yellow chilies, chopped
- 6 bunches green onions, chopped
- 2 bunches cilantro, chopped
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon vinegar
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 1 tablespoon fresh garlic (jarred)

## Instructions

### Small batch

1. Crush the tomatoes through your fingers into a bowl.
2. Cut out the stems and seeds of the yellow peppers and dice them.
3. Finely chop the cilantro and green onions.
4. Stir everything together with the chilies, garlic, and salt.

### Canning batch

1. Mix everything into the bowl of prepared tomatoes.
2. Fill jars. The batch made 10 pints and 1 half-pint.

## Notes

- The card gives no processing time for the canning batch; follow a tested water-bath method for salsa.
- The box holds more than one copy of this salsa. Another is just a list with no amounts: yellow, green, and red chiles, cilantro, green onions, whole canned tomatoes, jalapeños, and salt.
