# Rice — Moro de Guandules con Coco

Dominican rice cooked with pigeon peas and coconut milk.

## Ingredients

- 4 C rice
- 3 C boiled green pigeon peas (gandules)
- 3 C coconut milk
- 4 C water
- 2 T oil (divided)
- 4 t tomato paste (optional)
- ¼ C chopped green peppers
- 1 pinch of oregano
- ½ t mashed garlic
- ¼ C capers (optional)
- ¼ C chopped celery
- 1 t finely chopped coriander (cilantro)
- ½ t thyme leaves
- 1 t salt
- 12 pitted olives, cut into halves

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 T of oil in an iron pot and add the cilantro, celery, capers, garlic, olives, thyme, peppers, oregano, and salt.
1. Stir while adding the tomato paste.
1. Add the peas, also while stirring.
1. Once well heated, add the water and coconut milk and bring to a boil.
1. Add the rice and stir regularly, removing as much as you can of the rice that sticks to the bottom.
1. When all the water has evaporated, cover with a tight-fitting lid and simmer over very low heat.
1. Wait 15 minutes, uncover, add the remaining oil, and stir.
1. Move the rice from the bottom to the top so it cooks uniformly.
1. Cover again and wait another 5 minutes.
1. The rice should be firm but tender inside.
1. If necessary, cover and leave another 5 minutes on very low heat.
