# Vegetable dip

## Ingredients

- ½ pint (1 cup) sour cream
- ⅔ cup mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon chopped onion
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
- 1 heaping teaspoon dill weed
- 1 heaping teaspoon Beau Monde seasoning (Spice Islands)

## Instructions

1. Mix everything together and serve with raw vegetables.

## Notes

- IMO, a cultured sour cream substitute, works in place of sour cream.
