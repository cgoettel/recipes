# Snicker cake

## Ingredients

- 1 (15.25 oz) box German chocolate cake mix, plus what the box calls for
- 1 large bag caramels, unwrapped
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- ⅓ cup milk
- 1 (6 oz) package chocolate chips
- 1 cup unsalted peanuts or other nuts, chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and spray a 9×13-inch pan with cooking spray.
2. Prepare the cake mix according to the directions on the box.
3. Pour half the batter into the pan and bake for 20 minutes.
4. While it bakes, melt the caramels, butter, and milk together.
5. Pour half the caramel over the baked layer and sprinkle on half the nuts and chocolate chips.
6. Pour on the remaining batter and bake 20 minutes more.
7. Pour on the rest of the caramel and sprinkle with the rest of the nuts and chips. Cool before cutting.
