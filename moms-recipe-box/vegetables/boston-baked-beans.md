# Boston baked beans

## Ingredients

- 1 large (28 oz) can pork and beans, or 3 small cans
- 1 small (8 oz) can tomato sauce
- ½ onion, grated
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar
- 3 slices raw bacon, chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°F and grease a casserole dish.
2. Combine everything in the dish.
3. Bake uncovered about 4 hours.
