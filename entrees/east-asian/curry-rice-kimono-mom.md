# Curry Rice (Kimono Mom)

## Servings

4

## Ingredients

- 1 onion
- 1 clove of garlic
- 1 clove of ginger
- 300g chicken legs
- 300g potatoes
- 1 carrot
- 20g butter
- 4 tablespoons of flour
- 3 tablespoons of curry powder
- 1 bouillon cube
- 2 tablespoons of ketchup
- 1 teaspoon of instant coffee
- 1 teaspoon of sugar
- 1 tablespoon of Worcestershire sauce (Lea & Perrins sauce or BBQ sauce)
- 200 mL vegetable juice
- 600 mL water
