# Bread — Honey Wheat (LeAnn's)

From LeAnn. Makes 3 loaves.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups (≈680 g) hot water
- ¼ cup (≈50 g) oil
- ¼ cup (≈85 g) honey
- 2 tablespoons dough enhancer
- 2 teaspoons (≈12 g) salt
- 4 cups (≈450 g) whole wheat flour
- 2–4 cups all-purpose flour (added gradually)

## Instructions

1. Combine wet ingredients, salt, dough enhancer, and the whole wheat flour in a mixer. Mix on high for 2 minutes.
2. Add AP flour slowly until the side of the mixer is clean.
