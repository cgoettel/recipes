# Oatmeal cookies

The original recipe, from King Arthur flour, did not include steel cut oats, had fewer overall oats, and included chocolate chips. I prefer this recipe without the chocolate chips and I like the texture that the steel cut oats bring.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup brown sugar
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 egg yolk
- 1 tablespoon vanilla
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 2 cups flour
- 1½ cups oats
- ⅛ cup steel cut oats

## Instructions

- Preheat oven to 325°F.
- Cream butter and sugars.
- Beat in egg, egg yolk, and vanilla.
- Mix in salt, baking soda, and baking powder.
- Mix in flour.
- Add oats. The original recipe called for 1 cup oats, but it can easily take 1½ cups oats. Maybe more. I don't measure.
- Drop onto greased cookie sheet.
- Bake 12-15 minutes (it's usually around 14; you want the cookies still soft)
