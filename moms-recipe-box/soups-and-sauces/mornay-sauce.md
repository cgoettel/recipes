# Mornay sauce

## Ingredients

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- 6 tablespoons flour
- Salt and white pepper
- Creole seasoning
- 1½ cups milk or half-and-half
- 1½ cups chicken broth (Knorr chicken bouillon works well)
- Sharp cheddar cheese (such as Cracker Barrel), shredded

## Instructions

1. Use a wire whisk throughout.
2. Melt the butter over low heat. Add the flour, salt, and pepper, and stir at least 2–3 minutes, raising the heat until it bubbles.
3. Whisk in the milk or half-and-half, the broth, and Creole seasoning.
4. When the sauce is creamy, add the cheese and stir until melted.

## Notes

- If making it ahead of time, press plastic wrap onto the surface of the sauce. Any skin that forms will lift off with the wrap.
