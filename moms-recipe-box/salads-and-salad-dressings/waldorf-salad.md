# Waldorf salad

## Ingredients

- 2 cups diced unpeeled apple (about 2 medium)
- 1 cup diced celery
- ⅓ cup coarsely chopped nuts
- ½ cup mayonnaise or salad dressing, plus more if needed
- ¾ cup seedless grapes (optional)
- Lettuce leaves
- Maraschino cherries

## Instructions

1. Combine the apple, celery, nuts, and mayonnaise, and toss.
2. Add the grapes, and more mayonnaise if necessary.
3. Mound the salad in a lettuce-lined bowl and garnish with maraschino cherries.
