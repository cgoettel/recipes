# Shirley's rolls

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (≈455 g) warm milk (105–110°F)
- 2 tablespoons (≈18 g) instant dry yeast
- ¼ cup (≈50 g) sugar
- 2 teaspoons (≈12 g) salt
- ¾ stick (6 tablespoons, ≈85 g) salted butter, softened
- 2 large eggs
- 6 cups (≈720 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon (≈14 g) melted butter

## Instructions

1. In the bowl of a stand mixer with the dough hook, combine warm milk, yeast, sugar, salt, softened butter, eggs, and 5½ cups of the flour.
2. Mix on low. Once the flour starts to incorporate, increase to medium. Slowly add the remaining ½ cup of flour until the dough pulls away from the sides. The dough should be slightly sticky and soft — add more or less flour by feel.
3. Transfer to a large lightly greased bowl, cover, and let rise until doubled — about 90 minutes.
4. Lightly grease a large baking sheet (or two 9" × 13" pans). Punch down the dough and form into 24 rolls. Arrange in six rows of four. Cover and let rise again until doubled — about 1 hour.
5. Preheat oven to 375°F. Bake 12–14 minutes until lightly browned.
