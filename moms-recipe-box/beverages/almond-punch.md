# Almond punch

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 3 tablespoons lemon-lime juice
- 1½ teaspoons almond extract
- 1 L 7-Up or other lemon-lime soda

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the sugar in the lemon-lime juice and almond extract.
2. Add the mixture to the liter of 7-Up and serve cold.

## Notes

- A penciled note beside the sugar suggests simple syrup, which dissolves more easily.
