# Cream puffs

## Ingredients

- 1 cup water
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- 1 cup flour
- 4 eggs

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Bring the water and butter to a boil. Remove from the heat and stir in the flour.
3. Beat in the eggs until smooth.
4. Drop by tablespoons, 3 inches apart, onto a baking sheet.
5. Bake 35–40 minutes. Cool, then split and fill.

## Notes

- For small puffs (petits choux), drop by teaspoons onto an ungreased cookie sheet and bake 25–30 minutes at 400°F.
- The card doesn't give a filling. Whipped cream or [pastry cream](../desserts/pastry-cream.md) is the usual.
