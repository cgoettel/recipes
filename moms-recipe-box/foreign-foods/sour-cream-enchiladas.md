# Sour cream enchiladas

## Ingredients

- 12 tortillas
- 2 (10.5 oz) cans cream of chicken soup
- 1 (7 oz) can chopped green chiles
- 1 cup sour cream
- 2 cups grated longhorn or cheddar cheese
- ½ cup sliced green onions, green tops only

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a baking dish.
2. Stir the soup, green chiles, and sour cream together in a saucepan and warm gently.
3. Toss the cheese with the green onions.
4. Soften the tortillas by dipping each one for a second in simmering water.
5. Fill each tortilla with some of the cheese mixture and a spoonful of the sauce, roll it up, and set it seam side down in the baking dish.
6. Cover with the remaining sauce and cheese.
7. Bake 25–30 minutes, until the cheese melts and the sauce is bubbling.
