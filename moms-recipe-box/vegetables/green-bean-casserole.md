# Green bean casserole

## Ingredients

- 2 (14.5 oz) cans whole green beans, drained
- 1 (8 oz) can water chestnuts, drained and thinly sliced
- 1 (10.5 oz) can cream of chicken soup
- 1 (6 oz) can French fried onions

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a casserole dish.
2. Combine the beans and water chestnuts. Pour the undiluted soup over and stir gently until well coated.
3. Spread a layer in the casserole dish and sprinkle with onions. Repeat.
4. Bake 30 minutes.
