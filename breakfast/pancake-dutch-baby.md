# Dutch Baby (German Pancakes)

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- ½ cup flour
- ½ cup milk
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- Pinch of nutmeg (optional)
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter
- Syrup (for serving)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. In a bowl, mix all ingredients except butter and syrup.
3. Once the oven is preheated, put the butter in an oven-safe pan (I use a pie dish) and put it in the oven until the butter is melted.
4. Remove pan from oven, pour batter into the melted butter, and bake for 8–10 minutes, or until poofy and golden brown.
5. Remove, top with syrup or whatever, and enjoy.
