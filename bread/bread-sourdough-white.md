# Bread — Sourdough (White)

Makes 2 loaves. Halve all amounts for a single loaf.

## Ingredients

- 800 g strong white flour
- 10 g salt
- 460 mL water
- 320 g sourdough starter

## Instructions

1. Add the flour to a clean mixing bowl. Mix the salt through the flour. Add the water and starter and combine into a rough dough.
1. Turn the dough out onto a clean surface and knead ~10 minutes until smooth, soft, and elastic — windowpane stage. If the dough is wet or sticky, resist adding extra flour.
1. Return to the bowl, cover with cling film, and prove 4 hours at room temperature.
1. Turn out and knock back to equalize the temperature in the dough. Form into a tight round ball.
1. Lightly dust a proving basket with flour. Place the dough seam-side up. Cover loosely with a tea towel and prove 3–3½ hours. Alternatively, refrigerate overnight for slower flavor development.
1. Preheat oven to 230°C / 210°C fan (445°F / Gas 8) with a shallow baking tray on the bottom rack.
1. Carefully turn the dough out onto a floured baking tray (domed side up, seam side down).
1. Score the surface up to ½ cm deep with a sharp knife.
1. Boil a kettle of water and pour into the preheated tray to create steam.
1. Bake 30–35 minutes until a good crust has formed and the loaf sounds hollow when tapped on the base.

## Notes

- Longer proves break down gluten further, giving more flavor and easier digestibility.
