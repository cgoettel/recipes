# Hawaiian Sweet Bread

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup pineapple juice
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup softened butter
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup instant potato flakes
- 3 cup bread flour
- 1 1/2 tsp yeast

## Method (Bread Machine)

- Sweet Cycle
- 2 lb loaf
- Light Crust Setting
