# Cinnamon rolls

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 1 cup shortening
- ¾ cup sugar
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 cup boiling water
- ½ cup warm water
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 2 packages (5 teaspoons) active dry yeast
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup water
- 8½ cups flour

### Filling

- Softened butter, for spreading
- 1 cup brown sugar
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon nutmeg

## Instructions

### Dough

1. In a stand mixer, mix the shortening, ¾ cup sugar, salt, and boiling water. Let it cool to lukewarm.
2. In a small bowl, mix the warm water, 1 teaspoon sugar, and yeast, and let it stand until foamy.
3. Add the yeast mixture to the mixer along with the eggs.
4. Add the 1 cup water and the flour alternately, and mix well.
5. Cover and let rise until doubled.

### Filling and baking

1. Mix the brown sugar, sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
2. Roll the dough into a rectangle. Spread it with soft butter and sprinkle the filling over it.
3. Roll it up from the long side and cut into rolls with a piece of thread.
4. Set the rolls in greased pans and let them rise until puffy.
5. Bake at 400°F for 15 minutes.

## Notes

- To cut with thread, slide a length of plain thread under the roll, cross the ends over the top, and pull. It cuts cleanly without squashing the roll.
- The card doesn't mention a second rise; a short one in the pan gives softer rolls.
