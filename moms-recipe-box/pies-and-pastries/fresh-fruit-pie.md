# Fresh fruit pie

A glazed fresh-fruit pie, best with strawberries or peaches.

## Ingredients

- 1 baked pie crust
- 1 quart fresh fruit (strawberries or peaches)
- 1 cup sugar
- 3½ tablespoons cornstarch
- 3 tablespoons corn syrup
- 1½ cups water
- 4 tablespoons Jell-O, flavor to match the fruit
- 2 drops food coloring
- Cool Whip, for serving

## Instructions

1. Mix the sugar and cornstarch in a saucepan, then add the corn syrup and water.
2. Bring to a boil and cook 6 minutes, stirring constantly.
3. Stir in the Jell-O and food coloring and let the mixture cool.
4. Clean and slice the fruit and arrange it in the baked crust.
5. Pour the cool filling over the fruit.
6. Chill. Serve with Cool Whip.

## Notes

- For strawberry, use strawberry Jell-O and red coloring; for peach, peach Jell-O and yellow coloring.
- A spectacular company dessert, especially with fresh strawberries. Leave many of them whole for the top.
