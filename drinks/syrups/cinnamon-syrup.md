# Cinnamon Syrup

A steeped cinnamon syrup for tiki and autumnal drinks. Dissolving the sugar gently (no boil) keeps it clear, and the long off-heat steep is what pulls real cinnamon depth rather than a thin top-note.

## Ingredients

- 1 ½ cups sugar
- 1 cup water
- 4 cinnamon sticks, cracked

## Instructions

1. Over low heat, dissolve the sugar in the water without letting it boil.
2. Add the cracked cinnamon and bring to a bare simmer.
3. Off the heat, steep 6–12 hours — longer for a more assertive syrup.
4. Strain and store in the fridge.
