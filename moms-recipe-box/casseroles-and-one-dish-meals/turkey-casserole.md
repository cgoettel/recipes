# Turkey casserole

## Ingredients

- Butter, for sautéing
- ¼ cup chopped onion
- ¾ cup chopped celery
- Salt and pepper
- 1 (10.5 oz) can cream of mushroom soup
- ¼ soup can milk
- 1 cup chopped cooked turkey, chicken, or tuna
- 1 (5 oz) can chow mein noodles
- ½ cup cashew nuts

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a casserole dish.
2. Sauté the onion and celery in butter and season with salt and pepper.
3. Add the soup diluted with the milk, and the turkey.
4. In the casserole dish, layer noodles, then the turkey mixture, and sprinkle with nuts. Repeat, ending with a layer of noodles.
5. Bake for 35 minutes, until warmed through.
