# Stuffed mushrooms

## Ingredients

- Mushrooms, for stuffing
- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 1 bunch green onions, chopped
- 1 jar bacon bits

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Remove the mushroom stems.
3. Mix the cream cheese, green onions, and bacon bits.
4. Fill the mushroom caps with the mixture and set them on a baking sheet.
5. Bake until the mushrooms are tender and the filling is hot, about 15–20 minutes.
