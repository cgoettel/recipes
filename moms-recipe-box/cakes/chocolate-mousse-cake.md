# Chocolate mousse cake

A three-layer 9×13 dessert: nut shortbread, sweet cream cheese, and chocolate-vanilla pudding.

## Ingredients

### First layer

- 1 cup flour
- ½ cup chopped nuts
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter, softened
- ½ cup brown sugar

### Second layer

- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup Cool Whip

### Third layer

- 1 small (3.4 oz) package instant vanilla pudding
- 1 small (3.9 oz) package instant chocolate pudding
- 3 cups milk
- Cool Whip, for the top

## Instructions

### First layer

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Mix the flour, nuts, butter, and brown sugar together and press into the bottom of a 9×13-inch pan.
3. Bake 15 minutes, or until golden brown. Let cool completely.

### Second layer

1. Beat the powdered sugar, cream cheese, and Cool Whip with an electric mixer until smooth.
2. Spread over the cooled crust.

### Third layer

1. Whisk both puddings with the milk until thickened.
2. Pour over the cream cheese layer.
3. Top with Cool Whip and chill until serving.
