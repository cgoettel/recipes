# Lemon fruit salad

## Ingredients

### Gelatin

- 2 small (3 oz) packages lemon Jell-O
- 2 cups boiling water
- 2 cups 7-Up
- 2 cups crushed pineapple, drained, juice reserved
- 2–3 bananas, diced
- 1 cup miniature marshmallows

### Topping

- 2 tablespoons flour
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup reserved pineapple juice
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 cup heavy cream
- Grated mild cheese

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the Jell-O in the boiling water. Cool, then add the 7-Up.
2. When syrupy, add the pineapple, bananas, and marshmallows. Pour into a dish and chill until set.
3. For the topping, mix the flour, sugar, and pineapple juice in a saucepan, then add the egg.
4. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly. Let cool.
5. Whip the cream and fold it into the cooled pineapple juice mixture.
6. Spread on top of the set gelatin and sprinkle with grated mild cheese.
