# Strawberries and pudding sundae

A light, creamy dessert from a Jell-O and Dream Whip advertisement. Makes about 3½ cups.

## Ingredients

- 1 (4-serving) package Jell-O vanilla pudding and pie filling (cook-type), plus milk as the package directs
- 1 pint strawberries, plus more for garnish
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- Dream Whip whipped topping, prepared

## Instructions

1. Prepare the pudding as directed on the package for pudding and let it cool.
2. Slice the strawberries and sprinkle with the sugar.
3. Spoon the pudding into 6 parfait glasses and top with the strawberries.
4. Garnish with whipped topping and additional berries, if desired.
