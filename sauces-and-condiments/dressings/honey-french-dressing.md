# Honey French dressing

From [Aunt Kay](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/K26F-HCV)

## Ingredients

- ⅓C honey
- ⅓C oil
- ⅓C vinegar
- ⅓C ketchup
- ½t salt
- ½t pepper
- ½t celery seed
- ½t paprika

## Instructions

1. Combine in a blender. Blend.
2. Serve over mixed greens of fruits.
