# Dhalpurie (Ground Split Peas Roti)

## Serves

8 or more

## Ingredients

- 1/2 lb. split peas
- 1/2 tsp. saffron
- 2 tsp. salt
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp. vegetable oil
- 2-3 tsp. ground cumin (geera)
- 1 lb. or 4 cups flour
- 3 tsp. baking powder
- 1 1/2 cups water to mix (approx.)
- 1/4 cup oil or melted margarine or combination of both for coating

## Method

01. Boil split peas with saffron, 1 teaspoon salt and garlic until firm but cooked. Drain well.
02. Grind to a powder and fry in 1 tablespoon oil for 1 minute turning constantly to avoid sticking or burning.
03. Add geera and salt to taste. Cool.
04. Mix together flour, baking powder, 1 teaspoon salt and water to make a soft dough.
05. Knead dough until smooth.
06. Cut dough into 8 pieces or according to the size of the baking stone (tawah).
07. Shape into round balls; open each and in the centre and place about 3-4 tablespoons of prepared split pea filling. Pull the sides over and close up to form a ball with the filling inside. Dredge lightly with flour to prevent sticking.
08. Carefully roll out filled balls very thinly, using enough flour on surface to prevent sticking.
09. Heat and grease griddle or tawah and place a rolled roti on it.
10. Cook for 1 minute; turn over roti and brush with oil or melted margarine.
11. Cook for 1/2 minute and turn over and brush with oil.
12. Cook remaining filled dough in this way.

Serve with curried meat or vegetables.
