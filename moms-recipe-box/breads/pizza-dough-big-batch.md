# Pizza dough (big batch)

Enough for a crowd.

## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons yeast
- 2 quarts warm water
- ½ cup sugar, scant
- 4 teaspoons salt
- 24 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water, then mix in the sugar, salt, and flour to make a smooth dough.
2. Divide into 8 pieces and shape each into a ball. Cover with a damp towel and let rise for 1 hour.
3. Preheat the oven as hot as it goes. Roll or stretch each ball into a 16-inch round and add your toppings.
4. Bake on the lowest rack for 7–15 minutes, until the cheese bubbles in the middle.
