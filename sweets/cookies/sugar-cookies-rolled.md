# Rolled sugar cookies

## Ingredients

- 1 stick (8 tablespoons) butter - softened
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- ¼ cup milk
- ½ teaspoon vanilla
- 2¼ cups flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder

## Instructions

1. Cream butter and sugar together.
2. Add egg, milk & vanilla and beat well.
3. Blend in flour and baking powder.
4. Chill dough.
5. Roll out dough ¼ inch thickness on flour surface.
6. Cut & ...and serve with love!
7. Bake 11-12 mins at 350.
