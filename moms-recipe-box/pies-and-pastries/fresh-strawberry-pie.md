# Fresh strawberry pie

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 cup flour

### Filling

- 1 cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 3 tablespoons strawberry Jell-O
- 1 cup water
- Dash salt
- ¼ teaspoon red food coloring
- 2 pints fresh strawberries

## Instructions

### Crust

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Blend the butter, sugar, and flour, working them together with your hands.
3. Press into a 9-inch pie tin.
4. Bake 8–10 minutes. Do not let it brown. Let cool.

### Filling

1. Combine the sugar, cornstarch, gelatin, water, salt, and food coloring in a saucepan.
2. Cook until slightly thickened, boiling for 1 minute.
3. Line the crust with the strawberries.
4. Spoon the syrup over the strawberries. Cool.
