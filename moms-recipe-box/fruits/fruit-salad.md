# Fruit salad

## Ingredients

- 1 (20 oz) can pineapple chunks in juice, drained
- 2 bananas, sliced
- 2 apples, cubed
- 1 large orange, sectioned and cubed
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- Cinnamon
- Cloves
- 3 tablespoons sour cream

## Instructions

1. Toss all the ingredients together, seasoning with cinnamon and cloves to taste.
2. Chill before serving.
