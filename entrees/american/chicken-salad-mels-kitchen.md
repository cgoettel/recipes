# Mel's Kitchen chicken salad

From [Mel's Kitchen Cafe](https://www.melskitchencafe.com/).

## Ingredients

- 4 cups cooked, shredded (or small-chopped) chicken
- 1 cup small-diced celery
- 1 cup red grapes, halved or quartered
- ½ cup chopped slivered almonds or cashews
- 2–3 tablespoons finely diced green onions or chives
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley

### Dressing

- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons sour cream
- 1 tablespoon freshly squeezed lemon juice or red wine vinegar
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- ½ teaspoon coarse kosher salt (less for table salt)
- ⅛ teaspoon coarsely ground black pepper
- ⅛ teaspoon dried dill
- ⅛ teaspoon garlic powder
- ⅛ teaspoon onion powder
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Whisk the dressing ingredients together.
2. Combine the chicken salad ingredients in a large bowl.
3. Toss with the dressing. Adjust salt and pepper to taste.
