# Quick French bread

A loaf made from a can of refrigerated biscuits.

## Ingredients

- 1 can refrigerated biscuits
- 1–2 tablespoons milk
- 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
- Butter, for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a cookie sheet, or line it with foil and grease the foil.
2. Stand the biscuits on their sides in a row on the sheet. Press them lightly together and shape the ends to form a loaf.
3. Brush with the milk and sprinkle with the sesame seeds.
4. Bake for 25–30 minutes.
5. Break apart with a fork instead of cutting with a knife, and serve piping hot with butter.

## Notes

- Variation: sprinkle the loaf lightly with garlic salt instead of sesame seeds before baking.
