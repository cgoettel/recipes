# Baking powder biscuits

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 4 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons shortening
- Milk, enough to moisten the dough (about ¾ cup)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 450°F.
2. Mix the flour, baking powder, and salt. Cut in the shortening until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs.
3. Stir in just enough milk to make a soft dough.
4. Pat or roll the dough about ½ inch thick, cut into biscuits, and set them on an ungreased baking sheet.
5. Bake for 12–15 minutes, until golden.
