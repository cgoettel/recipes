# Applesauce cake (Lone Pine Farms)

## Ingredients

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1½ cups thick unsweetened applesauce
- A little cinnamon and a good shake of nutmeg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2¾ cups sifted flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 cup raisins
- ½ cup chopped nuts
- Powdered sugar, for dusting (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F. Grease and flour an 8×8×2-inch cake pan.
2. Cream the butter and sugar together until very creamy.
3. Add the applesauce, seasoned with the cinnamon and nutmeg, and the vanilla.
4. Sift 2½ cups of the flour with the baking soda and salt, and stir into the liquid.
5. Toss the raisins and nuts with the remaining ¼ cup flour and add them last.
6. When completely mixed, pour into the pan. Bake for 50 minutes, checking with a toothpick earlier to see if it's done.
7. Dust with powdered sugar, if you like.
