# Holiday sparkle punch

## Ingredients

- 1 cup water
- ⅔ cup grenadine syrup
- 3 (12 oz) cans black cherry soda
- 1 (28 oz) bottle (3½ cups) 7-Up
- 1 (6 oz) can frozen lemonade concentrate
- Ice cubes
- Lime slices
- Maraschino cherries

## Instructions

1. In a punch bowl, combine the water, grenadine, and lemonade concentrate.
2. Slowly pour the soda and 7-Up down the sides of the bowl.
3. Add ice cubes, and float lime slices and maraschino cherries on top.
