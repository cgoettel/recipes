# Whole wheat blender pancakes

Made from whole wheat kernels ground in the blender with the milk.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup whole wheat kernels
- 1 cup milk
- 2 eggs
- 4 tablespoons oil
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- About ¼ cup all-purpose flour

## Instructions

1. Blend the wheat and milk in a blender for 4 minutes, until the wheat is finely ground.
2. Add the eggs, oil, sugar, salt, baking powder, and baking soda and blend just to combine.
3. Add about ¼ cup flour, enough to thicken the batter.
4. Cook on a hot, lightly greased griddle, turning once when bubbles form and the edges look set.
