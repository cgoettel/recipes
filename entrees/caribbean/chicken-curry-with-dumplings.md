# Chicken Curry with Dumplings

## Serves

6-8

## Ingredients

- 1 chicken (3 lb)
- 2 large onions (4 oz)
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 teaspoons chilli powder
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon ground cloves
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 2 tablespoons or 1 oz creamed coconut
- 2 cups water
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup or 4 oz rice flour or plain flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ cup water (approx.)
- 2 tablespoons chopped coriander leaves

## Method

1. Joint the chicken and rinse well.
2. Slice the onion finely and fry in a little oil until golden brown.
3. Add the black pepper, chilli powder, ground cinnamon, clove and turmeric; fry for 5 minutes.
4. Add the chicken and fry for 2 or 3 minutes.
5. Add the coconut, water and salt.
6. Bring to the boil and simmer very gently for 15 minutes.
7. Meanwhile mix flour, salt and enough water to make a firm dough.
8. Shape into small dumplings and place in curry and cook for 20 minutes.

Garnish with coriander leaves.

## Cooking Tip

Remove bay leaves before eating, as it could be dangerous to eat.
