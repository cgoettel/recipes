# Seafood sauces

Three sauces from a fish-counter "take one" card: cocktail, rémoulade, and an herb mayonnaise. The herb mayonnaise is excellent with hot or cold shellfish.

## Ingredients

### Cocktail sauce

- ½ cup thick chili sauce
- 2 tablespoons horseradish
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- ½ teaspoon minced onion flakes (optional)
- Tabasco (optional)

### Rémoulade

- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1½ tablespoons minced onion flakes
- 2 tablespoons finely chopped celery
- ¼ cup horseradish
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 3 tablespoons ketchup
- ½ teaspoon salt
- Tabasco

### Herb mayonnaise

- 2 egg yolks
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons parsley
- 1 tablespoon chives
- 1 teaspoon dry mustard
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon thyme
- 1 teaspoon tarragon
- 1 dash pepper
- 1 cup salad oil

## Instructions

### Cocktail sauce

1. Mix the chili sauce, horseradish, lemon juice, and Worcestershire.
2. For added zest, mix in the onion flakes and Tabasco to taste.
3. Chill and serve.

### Rémoulade

1. Mix everything together, adding Tabasco to taste.
2. Chill thoroughly and serve.

### Herb mayonnaise

1. Put everything but the oil in a blender.
2. Blend at high speed, adding the oil a few drops at a time, until the mixture thickens.
