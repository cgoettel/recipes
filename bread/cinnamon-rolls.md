# Cinnamon rolls

## Ingredients

- 1.5 T yeast
- 1 C lukewarm water
- 1 t sugar
- 8 C flour
- 1 C sugar
- 6 T lard
- 2 C water
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 T salt

### Filling

- ½ C softened butter
- 1½ C brown sugar
- 2 T cinnamon

### Frosting

- 4 T butter
- 2 C powdered sugar
- 4 T milk
- 1 t vanilla extract

## Instructions

01. Start the yeast in a cup of lukewarm water with a teaspoon of sugar.
02. Cut the lard into the dry ingredients.
03. Combine beaten eggs, water, and yeast starter. Add half the flour and make a batter. Keep adding flour until the dough is formed. It's okay if it's a little sticky. Knead until the dough is smooth.
04. Split the dough in half. For each half:
05. Roll out half the dough.
06. Mix the filling. Spread it out onto the dough.
07. Roll, cut into segments, put into a greased 9"x13" pan.
08. Cover and let rise.
09. Heat oven to 350°F.
10. Bake for 20–30min, or until tops are golden brown.
11. Once cooled, make the frosting and evenly apply to the baked rolls.
