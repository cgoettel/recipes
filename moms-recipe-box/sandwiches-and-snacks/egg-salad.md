# Egg salad

A sandwich filling.

## Ingredients

- 10 eggs, cooked not quite to hard-boiled
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- Salt and pepper
- Paprika

## Instructions

1. Peel the eggs and chop them in a bowl.
2. Add the mayonnaise, season with salt and pepper to taste, and mix well.
3. Serve as sandwiches sprinkled with paprika.
