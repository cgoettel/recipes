# Pineapple-mint cooler

## Ingredients

- 4 cups unsweetened pineapple juice
- ½ cup lemon juice
- ¼ cup green crème de menthe
- 1 bottle lemon-lime soda, chilled
- Canned or fresh pineapple slices, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Combine the pineapple juice, lemon juice, and crème de menthe in a container and mix well. Chill.
2. Just before serving, add the lemon-lime soda.
3. Garnish with pineapple slices.

## Notes

- If you substitute something for the crème de menthe, the card says to add sugar.
