# Cashew chicken

## Ingredients

- ½ onion, chopped
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter
- 1 (16 oz) bag frozen broccoli
- 1 small bag cashews
- 4 (3 oz) packages chicken-flavored ramen noodles
- 3 lb chicken, cooked (baked, broiled, or boiled), skin and bones removed, cut into small pieces

## Instructions

1. Brown the onion in the butter in a large skillet.
2. Add the chicken, broccoli, the flavor packets from the ramen, and the cashews.
3. Simmer until the broccoli is tender and everything is hot.
4. Meanwhile, boil the ramen noodles 5–7 minutes and drain.
5. Put the drained noodles on plates and spoon the chicken mixture over the top.
