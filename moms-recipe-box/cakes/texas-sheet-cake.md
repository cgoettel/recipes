# Texas sheet cake

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sugar
- 2 cups flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 sticks (1 cup) butter
- 1 cup water
- 4 tablespoons cocoa
- ½ cup sour cream, or ½ cup evaporated milk with ½ teaspoon vinegar
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- [Texas frosting](texas-frosting.md), for finishing

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Grease and flour a sheet pan (a 15×10-inch jelly-roll pan).
2. In a large bowl, mix the sugar, flour, and salt.
3. In a small pan, bring the butter, water, and cocoa to a boil. Pour it over the dry ingredients and stir.
4. Add the sour cream (or evaporated milk and vinegar), eggs, and baking soda. Beat with an electric mixer until smooth.
5. Pour into the pan and bake 20–25 minutes.
6. Frost while the cake is still warm with Texas frosting.
