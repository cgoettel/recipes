# Domino Sugar's classic sugar cookies

## Ingredients

- 4 C flour
- 2 t baking powder
- 1 t salt
- 1 ½ sticks butter
- 1 ½ C sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 ½ t vanilla

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Sift together dry ingredients.
3. Separately, cream butter and sugar.
4. Add eggs and vanilla to butter mix.
5. Once incorporated, add dry ingredients. Should form a smooth, stiff dough.
6. NOTE: You do not need to refrigerate this dough to work with it.
7. Roll out to ¼" thick rectangle. Cut and place on greased cookie sheet.
8. Bake at 350°F for 10 minutes, or until firm and light golden brown.
