# Bread — Baguette (Paul Hollywood)

From Paul Hollywood. Makes 4 baguettes.

## Ingredients

- 500 g strong white flour
- 10 g salt
- 10 g fast-acting yeast (added to the bowl opposite the salt)
- 2 glugs of olive oil
- 370 mL cool water

## Instructions

1. Add everything to the mixer with the dough hook attached.
2. Dust the counter with flour. Turn the dough out and stretch/work for 5–10 minutes.
3. Oil a proofing container. Place the dough in, cover with a lid or plastic wrap, and proof 90–120 minutes.
4. Dust the counter. Roll the dough into a log and cut into 4 equal pieces. Flatten each into a long log.
5. Dust a couche with flour. Create space for 4 loaves and place the dough into the valleys.
6. Let proof for 60 minutes.
7. Preheat the oven to 240°C.
8. Score each loaf with 4 diagonal cuts — make sure the cuts overlap end-to-start.
9. Place 2 loaves per sheet and bake 25–30 minutes.
