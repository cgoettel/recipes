# Ann Myer's chocolate cake

## Ingredients

- 1 chocolate cake mix
- 1 chocolate instant pudding mix
- 1 C sour cream
- ½ C oil
- ½ C water
- 4 eggs
- Chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Mix everything together.
3. Fold in chocolate chips. Whatever you're comfortable with.
4. Pour into greased bundt cake pan.
5. Bake at 350°F for 50–60 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
