# Shrimp Dalkani

## Serves

4

## Ingredients

- 1 lb. large shrimp
- 1 tbsp. green seasoning
- 1/2 cup ground split peas or chick peas
- 1 tbsp. flour
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1/2 tsp. geera
- 1/2 tsp. garlic
- 1/2 tsp. pepper
- 1/2 cup water (approx.)

## Method

1. Clean shrimp and season with green seasoning, steam for 5 minutes.
2. Combine ground split peas, flour, baking powder, salt, geera, garlic and pepper with water to make a batter.
3. Dip shrimp into batter and deep fry a few at a time.
