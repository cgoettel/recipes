# English toffee

## Ingredients

- 4 sticks (2 cups) butter
- 2 cups sugar
- ½ cup water
- 1 cup chopped almonds (optional)
- 8 small plain Hershey bars
- ¾ cup ground walnuts

## Instructions

1. Grease an 11×15-inch pan, or spray it with cooking spray.
2. Cook the butter, sugar, water, and almonds in a heavy pan, stirring constantly, for about 15 minutes after it boils, until it turns medium brown and smokes a little.
3. Pour into the pan and let it stand 3 or 4 minutes.
4. Lay the Hershey bars on top until they melt, then spread the chocolate evenly with a spatula or the back of a spoon.
5. Sprinkle the ground walnuts on top.
6. Once set, break into bite-size pieces.
