# Cream of broccoli soup (mashed)

A quick, rustic version that's cooked soft and mashed rather than blended.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb broccoli, cut up
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 chicken bouillon cubes
- 3 cups water
- 1 (12 oz) can evaporated milk
- 2 tablespoons butter or bacon drippings
- A handful of dehydrated potato flakes
- Cornstarch and milk, to thicken
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Cook the broccoli, onion, and bouillon cubes in the water until very soft.
2. Mash with a potato masher.
3. Add the evaporated milk, the butter or bacon drippings, and a handful of dehydrated potatoes. Simmer 5 minutes.
4. If it needs thickening, stir in cornstarch mixed with a little milk.
5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
