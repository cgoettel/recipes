# Banana supershake

## Ingredients

- 2 bananas, cut up
- ⅓ cup ReaLemon lemon juice (from the bottle)
- 1 cup water
- 1 (14 oz) can Eagle Brand sweetened condensed milk
- 2 cups crushed ice

## Instructions

1. In a blender, combine the bananas, lemon juice, water, and sweetened condensed milk. Blend well.
2. Add the ice and blend until smooth.
3. Serve in frosted glasses.

## Notes

- Refrigerate leftovers: the mixture stays thick and creamy in the refrigerator.
