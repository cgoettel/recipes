# Chocolate Chip Cookies — Oatmeal Cranberry (Giada)

From [Giada De Laurentiis](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/giada-de-laurentiis/oatmeal-cranberry-and-chocolate-chunk-cookies-recipe).

## Ingredients

- 1 C all-purpose flour
- ¾ t ground cinnamon
- ½ t baking powder
- ½ t baking soda
- ½ t fine sea salt
- 1 stick unsalted butter, room temperature
- ½ C light brown sugar
- ½ C sugar
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- ½ t pure vanilla extract
- 2 C old-fashioned oats
- 1 C dried cranberries
- 1 (4 oz) bar 60% cacao bittersweet chocolate (Ghirardelli recommended), chopped into ¼" chunks

Special equipment: a 4 oz (½ C) ice cream or cookie scoop.

## Instructions

1. Put a rack in the center of the oven. Preheat to 350°F. Line 2 baking sheets with parchment.
2. Whisk together the flour, cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl.
3. In a stand mixer with the paddle, beat the butter and sugars until light and fluffy, ~1 minute. Add the egg and vanilla and beat until smooth.
4. With the machine running, gradually add the flour mixture. Add the oats, cranberries, and chocolate chunks. Mix until just incorporated — the dough will be stiff.
5. Using a 4 oz cookie scoop, form 12 slightly rounded mounds (2"-diameter). Arrange 6 per baking sheet, spaced evenly.
6. Flatten the tops slightly with the back of a spoon.
7. Bake 13–15 minutes until the edges are slightly golden.
8. Cool on the baking sheet 20 minutes before serving.
