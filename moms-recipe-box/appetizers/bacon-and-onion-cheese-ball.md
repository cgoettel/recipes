# Bacon and onion cheese ball

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons minced green onion
- 12 oz cream cheese, softened
- ¼ cup mayonnaise
- 5 slices crisp cooked bacon, crumbled, or 3 heaping tablespoons bacon bits
- ½ teaspoon dill weed
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper
- Chopped walnuts or pecans, for rolling

## Instructions

1. Mix everything together and roll into a ball.
2. Chill, then roll in chopped walnuts or pecans.
