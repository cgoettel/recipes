# Chili

## Ingredients

- 1 quart dried beans
- 1 large onion, grated
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter
- 1½ lb ground beef
- ½ tablespoon salt
- ½ cup ketchup
- ½ tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- ½ tablespoon mustard
- 1 pint tomato juice
- ½ tablespoon chili powder

## Instructions

1. Soak the beans overnight, then cook until tender.
2. Cook the onion in the butter until tender.
3. Add the ground beef, salt, ketchup, Worcestershire, mustard, and tomato juice, and cook 20 minutes.
4. Add the chili powder and cook 10 minutes more.
5. Add to the beans and serve.
