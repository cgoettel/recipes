# Buckaroos

Oatmeal chocolate chip cookies.

## Ingredients

### Mixture one

- 2 cups flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon baking powder

### Mixture two

- 1 cup shortening
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups oatmeal
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 (12 oz) package chocolate chips
- ½ cup pecans (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease cookie sheets.
2. Sift together mixture one.
3. Cream the shortening and sugars, beat in the eggs, then stir in the remaining mixture-two ingredients.
4. Combine both mixtures.
5. Spoon onto the cookie sheets and bake 12 minutes.
