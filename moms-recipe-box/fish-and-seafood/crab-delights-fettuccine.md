# Crab Delights fettuccine

## Ingredients

- 6 oz fettuccine
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 cup half-and-half
- 1 (8 oz) package Louis Kemp Crab Delights, flakes or chunks
- ⅛ teaspoon black pepper
- ½ cup Parmesan cheese, plus more for serving

## Instructions

1. Cook the fettuccine according to the package directions and drain.
2. Heat the butter and garlic in a skillet over medium heat until the garlic is lightly browned.
3. Add the half-and-half, Crab Delights, and pepper. Heat about 3 minutes, or until the mixture just begins to bubble, stirring occasionally.
4. Add the Parmesan and stir for 1 minute. Remove from the heat.
5. Toss the sauce with the fettuccine and serve immediately with more Parmesan, if desired.

## Notes

- Excellent. Try it with shrimp and sausage too.
