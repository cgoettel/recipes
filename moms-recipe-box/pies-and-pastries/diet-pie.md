# Diet pie

## Ingredients

- 1 large tub Cool Whip, thawed
- 1 small (3 oz) package Jell-O
- 1 container yogurt, the same flavor as the Jell-O
- 1 graham cracker crust

## Instructions

1. Mix the Cool Whip, the dry Jell-O powder, and the yogurt together well.
2. Spoon into the graham cracker crust and chill until set.
