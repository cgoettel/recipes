# Chili beans with kidney beans

## Ingredients

- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter
- 1¼ cups chopped onion
- 1 teaspoon garlic juice or minced garlic
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 1 (28 oz) can Italian-style tomatoes
- 1 cup water
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 large can red kidney beans, liquid saved

## Instructions

1. Brown the butter, onion, garlic, ground beef, and chili powder together in a large pot.
2. Add the tomatoes, water, salt, and the liquid from the kidney beans. Simmer for 45 minutes.
3. Add the kidney beans for the last 5 minutes of simmering.
4. Let it sit a day before serving.

## Notes

- Serve as chili, or as chili mac over cooked spaghetti.
