# Parmesan cheese ball

## Ingredients

- 12 oz cream cheese, softened
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- ¼ cup mayonnaise
- ½ teaspoon oregano leaves, crushed
- ⅛ teaspoon garlic powder
- Chopped walnuts or pecans, for rolling

## Instructions

1. Mix everything together and roll into a ball.
2. Chill, then roll in chopped walnuts or pecans.
