# Candy-making tips

General hints for cooked candies like caramels, toffee, and penuche.

## Tips

1. Always use a heavy pan for cooking.
2. Do not let the candy thermometer touch the bottom of the pan.
3. While cooking, wash sugar crystals from the sides of the pan with a pastry brush dipped in hot water.
4. Always cook over low heat unless the recipe says otherwise.
5. Test your thermometer in boiling water before you start. If your water boils lower than 212°F, subtract the difference from the recipe's temperature. For example, if a recipe says 236°F at sea level and your water boils at 206°F, that's 6° low, so cook to 230°F, not 236°F.
6. Try to avoid making candy on cloudy, humid, or rainy days. It doesn't turn out as well.
7. When pouring candy from the pan onto a marble slab, never scrape the sides of the pan, or the candy will turn to sugar.
8. To test candy on a marble slab for coolness, don't disturb the edges; press the palm of your hand into the center. It should leave an indentation and feel cool to the touch.
