# Dr. Pepper chocolate cake

This recipe is from [Glen and Friend's cooking](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KvVKRHlr1ig).

## Ingredients

### Cake

- 1 C Dr. Pepper/Coke/Pepsi/root beer
- 1 C white sugar
- &frac12; C brown sugar
- 4 oz (113.4 g) chocolate
- &frac12; C butter
- 2 C flour
- 2 t baking soda
- 1 t baking powder
- 1 t salt
- 1 C buttermilk
- 2 eggs
- 1 t vanilla

### Icing

- 4 oz (113.4 g) chocolate
- 1 C butter
- Pinch salt
- &frac14; C Dr. Pepper/Coke/Pepsi/root beer
- 1 t vanilla
- 4 C powdered sugar - approximate

## Instructions

### Cake

1. Preheat the oven to 375&deg;F.
1. In a saucier over medium heat, combine the soda, sugars, chocolate, and butter. Stir until fully melted and combined. Remove from the heat and let cool slightly.
1. In a separate bowl, sift together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
1. In a liquid measuring cup, stir together the buttermilk, vanilla, and eggs.
1. Grease, flour, and line two 9" cake pans with parchment.
1. Whisk the milk mixture into the slightly cooled chocolate mixture.
1. Whisk in the dry ingredients, in 2&ndash;3 additions.
1. Divide the batter between the pans. For precise layers, weigh the batter as you pour it in.
1. Bake 25&ndash;30 minutes or until a tester comes out clean.
1. Let the cakes cool completely then remove from the pans and cool on a rack.

### Icing

1. In a saucier, melt the chocolate and allow to cool.
1. Cream the butter and add in about a quarter of the icing sugar.
1. Whisk in the melted chocolate, vanilla, and salt.
1. Slowly whisk in just enough icing sugar to stiffen and attain desired sweetness.
1. Ice as a layer cake.
