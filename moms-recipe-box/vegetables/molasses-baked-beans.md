# Molasses baked beans

## Ingredients

- 2 (16 oz) cans pork and beans in molasses sauce
- ½ cup chili sauce or ketchup
- ½ cup chopped onion
- ½ cup packed brown sugar, plus 1 tablespoon for the top
- 1 teaspoon dry mustard
- 1–2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- Dash of red pepper sauce
- 1 teaspoon salt
- Pepper
- 6 slices bacon, cut crosswise into thirds

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Combine everything except the bacon and the 1 tablespoon brown sugar in a 1½-quart casserole.
3. Arrange the bacon on top of the bean mixture and sprinkle with the remaining 1 tablespoon brown sugar.
4. Bake uncovered for 1–1½ hours.
