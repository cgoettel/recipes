# Strawberry mousse

A sour cream gelatin ring, from a newspaper clipping.

## Ingredients

- 2 (6 oz) boxes strawberry Jell-O
- 2 cups boiling water
- 2 cups sour cream
- 1 (10 oz) package frozen strawberries
- 3 cups cold water
- Whole strawberries, fresh or frozen, for the center

## Instructions

1. Add the boiling water to the gelatin and stir until it dissolves completely.
2. Blend the sour cream into the gelatin mixture until smooth.
3. Stir in the frozen strawberries and the cold water.
4. Pour into a 2-quart ring mold and chill until firm.
5. To serve, unmold onto a plate and fill the center with whole strawberries.

## Notes

- Variation: use blackberry gelatin instead of strawberry, and frozen blackberries or blueberries instead of the frozen strawberries.
