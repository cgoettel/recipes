# Sugar Cookies — Jessica Martin's easy sugar cookies

## Ingredients

- 2 ¾ C flour
- 1 t baking soda
- ½ t baking powder
- 1 C softened butter
- 1 ½ C white sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 t vanilla extract
- 3–4 t buttermilk

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F
2. Mix dry ingredients
3. Mix butter and sugar. Then add vanilla and egg.
4. Mix in dry ingredients
5. Roll in balls, press down, and brush with buttermilk
6. Bake for 8 minutes
