# Sauce — Board & Brew Special

The sandwich-shop sauce from Board & Brew. Keeps 3–4 weeks refrigerated.

## Ingredients

- 1 C canola oil
- ½ C white sugar
- ½ C red wine vinegar
- 1 small red onion, peeled and cut into chunks
- 1 T water
- 2 t lemon juice
- 2 t paprika
- 2 t mustard powder
- ½ t kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Place all ingredients in a blender.
1. Start on low, then increase to medium.
1. Blend until smooth and creamy.
