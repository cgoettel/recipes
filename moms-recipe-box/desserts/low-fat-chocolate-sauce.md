# Low-fat chocolate sauce

From a magazine clipping called "Where's-the-fat chocolate sauce": cocoa and evaporated skim milk thickened with cornstarch stand in for chocolate and cream.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup sugar
- ⅓ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 4 teaspoons cornstarch
- ⅔ cup evaporated skim milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Instructions

1. In a small saucepan, stir together the sugar, cocoa, and cornstarch. Add the evaporated milk.
2. Cook and stir constantly over medium heat until thickened and bubbly. Cook and stir 2 minutes more.
3. Remove from the heat and stir in the vanilla.
4. Serve warm or cool, drizzled over ice milk, frozen yogurt, or angel food cake. Top with fresh fruit such as strawberries, raspberries, peaches, or apricots.

## Notes

- Refrigerate leftovers, covered, up to 1 week. Reheat uncovered in the microwave on high: 30–60 seconds for ¼ cup, ½–1½ minutes for ½ cup.
