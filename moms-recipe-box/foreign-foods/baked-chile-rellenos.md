# Baked chile rellenos

## Ingredients

- 5 whole green chiles
- 2 cups grated mozzarella or cheddar cheese, plus more for the top
- 5 eggs
- 1¼ cups milk
- ¼ cup flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- Dash pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a baking dish.
2. Stuff the chiles with the cheese and lay them in the dish.
3. Mix the eggs, milk, flour, salt, and pepper together.
4. Pour over the stuffed chiles and top with more cheese.
5. Bake 45 minutes.
