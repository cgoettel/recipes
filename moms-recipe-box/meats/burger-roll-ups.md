# Burger roll-ups

## Ingredients

- Your favorite biscuit dough
- 1 lb ground beef
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons ketchup or chili sauce
- 1 tablespoon chili powder (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F and grease a cookie sheet.
2. Make the biscuit dough and roll it into an oblong ¼ inch thick.
3. Mix the ground beef, salt and pepper, onion, mayonnaise, ketchup, and chili powder well in a bowl. Spread over the dough.
4. Roll up as for a jelly roll and cut into 1-inch slices.
5. Place on the cookie sheet and bake 25 minutes.
