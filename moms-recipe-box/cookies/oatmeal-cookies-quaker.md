# Quaker's best oatmeal cookies

## Ingredients

- 2½ sticks (1¼ cups) butter
- ¾ cup brown sugar
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1½ cups flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- ¼ teaspoon nutmeg
- 3 cups oatmeal

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Beat the butter and sugars together until light and fluffy. Beat in the egg and vanilla.
3. Combine the flour, baking soda, salt, and spices, and add to the butter mixture, mixing well.
4. Stir in the oats.
5. Drop by rounded tablespoons onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
6. Bake 8–9 minutes for a chewy cookie, 10–11 minutes for a crisp one.
7. Cool 1 minute on the cookie sheet, then move to a cooling rack.
