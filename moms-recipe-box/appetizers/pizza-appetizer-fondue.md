# Pizza appetizer fondue

Makes 12 appetizer servings.

## Ingredients

- 1 (29 oz) can meatless spaghetti sauce
- 1 (8 oz) package mozzarella cheese, shredded
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 teaspoons oregano leaves
- 1 teaspoon instant minced onion
- ¼ teaspoon garlic salt
- 1 long loaf Italian bread, cut in 1-inch cubes

## Instructions

1. In a fondue pot on the range over low heat, heat everything except the bread, stirring frequently, until the cheese is melted and the sauce is hot.
2. Move the pot to its stand over low heat.
3. Serve with the bread cubes, and give guests fondue forks to dip the bread into the hot sauce.
