# Stuffed peppers

## Ingredients

- 3–4 bell peppers (I prefer yellow and orange)
- 1 lb ground beef
- ½ large onion, chopped
- A small container of cherry tomatoes, halved
- A handful of mushrooms washed and quartered
- Grated cheese
- 2–3 C cooked rice
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Italian seasoning

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 350°F
2. Par boil your peppers after you have cut off the tops and hollowed them out. I use the tops to chop up and add to the other vegetables that you end up sautéing
3. Sauté your mushrooms, onions and peppers. Towards the end of the sauté process add in your chopped tomatoes, but don’t overcook.
4. Place your hamburger in your frying pan and cook that seasoned to taste. Dad used whatever seasoning he uses for tacos.
5. In a bowl, place your sautéed vegetables, your drained, hamburger meat, your cooled down rice mixture and a good two handfuls of grated cheese mixed together. Stuff the peppers with the rice hamburger cheese mixture. Place in a baking dish that you have sprayed with Pam take remaining rice mixture and place around the peppers.
6. Bake at 350°F for 35–40 minutes or until hot. Remember, everything has been pre-cooked so you’re only cooking it long enough to bring the flavors together. Enjoy.
