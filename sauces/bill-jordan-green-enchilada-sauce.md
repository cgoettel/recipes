# Bill Jordan Green Sauce

This is a green sauce for enchiladas.

- 4 cups chicken stock
- 2 jalapenos and 2 serranos, stemmed
- 1/2 of an onion
- 3 large cloves of garlic
- 4 tomatillos
- salt to taste.

## Instructions

1. Roast the peppers and the tomatillos
2. Put the onion and garlic with a bit of oil and salt in a pot, medium heat. Once the onions soften add in the stock, pepper and the tomatillos bring to a simmer for 20 mins.
3. Put that all in the blender until smooth. Back into the pot to a simmer adding in salt to your taste.

This is a bit on the hot side. If you want medium, Id drop one of the jalapenos.
