# Jan's cookies

From Aunt Jan

## Ingredients

- 1C margarine, soft
- 1C brown sugar
- 1½C sugar
- 1T milk
- 1½t vanilla
- 2 eggs
- 1½C flour
- 1¼t baking soda
- 1t salt
- ½t mace
- 1½t cinnamon
- ¼t nutmeg
- ⅛t powdered cloves
- 1C corn flakes
- 3C oats
- 4oz coconut
- 1pkg chocolate chips
- 1C nuts

## Instructions

1. Cream margarine, brown sugar, sugar, milk, vanilla, and eggs until smooth
2. Mix rest of the ingredients in
3. Bake at 350°F for 10 minutes.
