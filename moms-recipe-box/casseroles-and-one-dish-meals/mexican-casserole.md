# Mexican casserole

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 (11.25 oz) can chili beef soup, undiluted
- 1 (10.75 oz) can tomato soup, undiluted
- Garlic salt
- Pepper
- 1 cup elbow macaroni
- 9 slices American cheese

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef in an electric skillet (or a large skillet with a lid) and drain.
2. Stir in the chili beef soup and tomato soup, undiluted. Season with garlic salt and pepper to taste.
3. Meanwhile, cook the macaroni until tender and drain.
4. Stir the macaroni into the meat mixture.
5. Lay the cheese slices over the top, cover, and warm until the cheese melts.
