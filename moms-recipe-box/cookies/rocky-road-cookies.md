# Rocky road cookies

A no-bake roll, sliced like a refrigerator cookie.

## Ingredients

- 1 (12 oz) package chocolate chips
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 (10 oz) package colored miniature marshmallows
- ¾ cup chopped nuts
- 1 egg
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- Crushed graham crackers or vanilla wafers

## Instructions

1. Melt the chocolate chips and butter over hot water.
2. Beat the egg and powdered sugar together. Add the melted chocolate mixture.
3. Pour over the marshmallows and nuts and mix.
4. Divide into thirds and form each into a roll. Roll each in the crumbs.
5. Wrap in plastic wrap and refrigerate. Slice to serve.

## Notes

- The egg is not cooked. Use pasteurized eggs if that's a concern.
