# Brownies

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ⅔ cup cocoa
- 2 sticks (1 cup) butter, melted
- 4 eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9×13-inch pan.
2. Mix everything together.
3. Spread in the pan and bake for 30 minutes.
