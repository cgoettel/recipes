# Mushroom roast

## Ingredients

- 1 (2–3 lb) chuck, O-bone, or 7-bone roast
- 3 tablespoons A.1. steak sauce
- 1 (10.5 oz) can cream of mushroom soup, undiluted
- 1 (1 oz) package Lipton onion soup mix

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
2. Line a 9×13×2-inch pan with foil and set the roast in it.
3. Pour the steak sauce over the meat, then the undiluted mushroom soup. Sprinkle the dry onion soup mix over the top.
4. Make a foil tent and set it loosely over the roast.
5. Bake for about 3 hours, until tender.
