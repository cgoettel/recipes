# Beef and hot rice

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 (10.5 oz) can condensed onion soup
- Hot cooked rice, for serving

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef in a skillet and drain off the fat.
2. Add the soup, cover, and simmer over low heat for 10 minutes.
3. Serve over hot rice.
