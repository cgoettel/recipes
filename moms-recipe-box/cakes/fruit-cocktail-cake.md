# Fruit cocktail cake

## Ingredients

### Cake

- 1 egg, beaten
- 2 cups fruit cocktail, with its juice
- 1½ cups flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon ground cloves
- 1 teaspoon nutmeg

### Topping

- ½ cup brown sugar
- ½ cup chopped nuts

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9×13-inch pan.
2. Mix the beaten egg with the fruit cocktail and its juice.
3. Sift the flour, sugar, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, cloves, and nutmeg together.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the egg and fruit mixture and stir until combined.
5. Spread the batter in the pan and top with the brown sugar and chopped nuts.
6. Bake at 350°F for 25 minutes, then lower the oven to 300°F and bake 10–15 minutes longer.
