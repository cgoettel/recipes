# Hawaiian sweet bread

## Ingredients

- ½ cup pineapple juice
- ½ cup (≈115 g) milk
- 1 egg
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons, ≈55 g) softened butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- ½ teaspoon (≈3 g) salt
- ⅓ cup (≈65 g) sugar
- ½ cup instant potato flakes
- 3 cups (≈360 g) bread flour
- 1½ teaspoons (≈4.5 g) yeast

## Instructions (bread machine)

- Sweet Cycle
- 2 lb loaf
- Light Crust Setting
