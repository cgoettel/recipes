# Macadamia nut sauce for salmon

## Ingredients

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- 2-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled and grated
- ¼–⅓ cup fresh lemon juice
- ⅓–½ cup chopped macadamia nuts

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter, making sure you don't burn it.
2. Add the lemon juice, ginger, and nuts. Stir to blend; it thickens on its own.
3. Serve over salmon or any grilled poultry or fish.
