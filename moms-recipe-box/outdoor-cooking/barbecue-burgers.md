# Barbecue burgers

## Ingredients

- ⅔ cup evaporated milk (one 5 oz can)
- 1 egg
- 1½ lb ground beef
- ½ cup fine cracker crumbs
- 1½ teaspoons salt
- ¼ teaspoon pepper
- 1 teaspoon dry mustard
- ½ cup finely chopped green bell pepper
- ¼ cup finely chopped onion

## Instructions

1. Heat the grill or broiler.
2. Blend all the ingredients together until well mixed.
3. Form into 8 patties.
4. Grill or broil 5–7 minutes on each side.
