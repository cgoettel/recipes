# Spaghetti

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
- Seasoning salt and pepper to taste
- 1 package Lawry's or Schilling spaghetti sauce mix
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup sliced mushrooms
- Spaghetti
- Parmesan, for passing

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef and drain.
2. Add the onion, tomato sauce, seasoning salt and pepper, sauce mix, and water. Stir thoroughly.
3. Reduce the heat to a simmer and cook 15 minutes, adding the mushrooms for the last 10 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti until tender.
5. Serve the sauce over the spaghetti and pass Parmesan.
