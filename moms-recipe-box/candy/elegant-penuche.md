# Elegant penuche

## Ingredients

- 2½ cups granulated sugar
- ½ cup brown sugar, firmly packed
- 1⅓ cups cream
- ¼ cup light corn syrup (white Karo)
- Chopped pecans, for rolling

## Instructions

1. Combine the sugars, cream, and corn syrup in a heavy pan.
2. Cook slowly, stirring occasionally, until a little of the mixture forms a soft ball when dropped into cold water: 236°F at sea level.
3. Pour the cooked candy onto a marble slab and leave it undisturbed until cold.
4. Work it with a wide-blade putty knife or scraper until it turns creamy and thick and the color lightens.
5. Let it stand until firm.
6. Shape it by hand into long rolls, then roll them in chopped pecans until the outside is coated.
7. Wrap in foil and store in the refrigerator.

## Notes

- 236°F is the sea-level soft-ball temperature. At altitude, subtract about 2°F for every 1,000 feet, or rely on the cold-water test.
