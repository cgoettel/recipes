# Impossible cheesecake pie

## Ingredients

### Pie

- ¾ cup milk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup Bisquick
- 2 (8 oz) packages cream cheese, cut in ½-inch cubes and softened

### Cheesecake topping

- 1 cup sour cream
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons vanilla

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9×1¼-inch pie plate.
2. Put the milk, vanilla, eggs, sugar, and Bisquick in a blender. Cover and blend on high for 15 seconds.
3. Add the cream cheese. Cover and blend on high for 2 minutes.
4. Pour into the pie plate and bake 40–45 minutes, until the center is firm. Cool.
5. Mix the sour cream, sugar, and vanilla, and spread carefully over the top.
6. Garnish with fruit, if you like.

## Notes

- High altitude (3,500–6,500 feet): bake 45–50 minutes.
