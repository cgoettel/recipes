# Chicken macaroni soup

## Ingredients

- Chicken (breasts are easiest)
- 8 cups chicken broth
- 5 stalks celery, chopped
- 1 onion, chopped
- 5 carrots
- Macaroni

## Instructions

1. Cook the chicken and dice it.
2. Bring the broth to a boil. Add the diced chicken, celery, onion, and carrots, and simmer until the vegetables are tender.
3. Cook the macaroni separately, drain, and add it to the soup just before serving.
