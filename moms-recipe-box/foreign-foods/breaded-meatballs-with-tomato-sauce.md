# Breaded meatballs with tomato sauce

## Ingredients

### Sauce

- Olive oil
- 3 onions, slivered
- Salt and ground pepper
- 2 cans tomatoes
- A little tomato paste

### Meatballs

- ¾ lb ground beef
- 1 egg
- Grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 slices Italian bread
- 2 tablespoons parsley
- ½ tablespoon nutmeg
- 1½ teaspoons salt
- ½ cup bread crumbs, for coating
- Oil, for frying

## Instructions

### Sauce

1. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the onions. Sprinkle with salt and pepper.
2. Add the tomatoes and a little tomato paste. Cook 10 minutes over medium heat.

### Meatballs

1. Wet the bread slices a bit, squeeze out the excess moisture, and shred.
2. Mix the ground beef, egg, parsley, Parmesan, nutmeg, salt, and bread together like meatloaf.
3. Form into meatballs, flatten them, and dip in bread crumbs.
4. Heat oil in a pan and fry the breaded meatballs until browned and cooked through. Serve with the sauce.
