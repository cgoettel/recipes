# Homemade vanilla ice cream

A no-cook (no-custard) vanilla base for an ice cream freezer.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sugar
- 8 cups light cream
- ¼–½ teaspoon salt
- 3 teaspoons pure vanilla

## Instructions

1. Mix the salt with the sugar, then stir them into the cream until dissolved.
2. Add the vanilla.
3. Freeze in an ice cream freezer according to its directions.

## Notes

- Penciled on the card for a 3-quart batch: 1½ cups sugar, ¼ teaspoon salt, and 2 teaspoons vanilla. No cream amount is penciled; 6 cups keeps the proportion.
- "Thin cream" is light cream; half-and-half works too.
