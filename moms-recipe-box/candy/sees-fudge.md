# See's fudge

## Ingredients

- 3 (12 oz) packages chocolate chips
- 1 (8 oz) jar marshmallow creme
- 2 teaspoons vanilla
- 2 cups chopped nuts
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- 4½ cups sugar
- 1 (15 oz) can evaporated milk

## Instructions

1. Butter a 9x13x2-inch pan or a cookie sheet.
2. Mix the chocolate chips, marshmallow creme, vanilla, and nuts together in a large bowl.
3. In a saucepan, bring the butter, sugar, and evaporated milk to a full rolling boil, then cook over low heat for 10 minutes. Time the full 10 minutes.
4. Pour the hot syrup over the chocolate mixture and blend thoroughly with a wooden spoon.
5. Pour into the buttered pan and let it set. Cut into pieces.

## Notes

- Evaporated milk cans now run 12 oz; use 1¼ cans, or a 12 oz can plus 3 oz from a second.
