# Meat loaf with Italian dressing mix

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 2 eggs
- ¼ cup bread crumbs
- 1 envelope Good Seasons Italian dressing mix

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Mix everything together and shape into a loaf in a loaf pan.
3. Bake for about 1 hour, until cooked through (160°F in the center).
