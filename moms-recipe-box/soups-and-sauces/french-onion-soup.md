# French onion soup

## Ingredients

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- 6–7 onions, half chopped and half sliced
- 3 beef bouillon cubes
- 3 cups boiling water
- 2 (10.5 oz) cans beef bouillon (condensed beef broth)

## Instructions

1. Sauté the onions in the butter until soft.
2. Dissolve the bouillon cubes in the boiling water, then stir in the canned beef bouillon.
3. Add the broth to the onions and butter and heat through.
