# Pasta sauce — Marie Peters

Sunday-gravy-style sauce with stewing beef, pepperoni, and hard-boiled eggs simmered in. Goes with rotini.

## Ingredients

- 1 (12 oz) can tomato paste
- 1 (30 oz) can tomato sauce
- 1 lb stewing beef chunks
- 1 stick pepperoni, sliced
- Hard-boiled eggs, peeled
- Salt, garlic powder, basil
- Rotini (spirals)

## Instructions

1. Add a little oil to a large pot and brown the beef chunks.
1. Add the sauce and paste. Fill each can with water and add to the pot — twice.
1. Add salt, garlic powder, and basil.
1. Slice the pepperoni and add to the pot.
1. Add the hard-boiled eggs.
1. Simmer on low for 4 hours, stirring occasionally.
1. When the sauce is thick and reduced, cook the pasta per box instructions.
1. Drain the pasta, put the meat and eggs in a bowl.
1. Plate pasta, sauce, meat. Most importantly — invite the Peters over for dinner.
