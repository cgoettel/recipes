# Meatballs with grape jelly sauce

## Ingredients

### Meatballs

- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 cup milk
- ¾ cup saltine cracker crumbs
- ¼ cup chopped celery
- 1 tablespoon parsley
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon ground sage
- Dash of pepper
- 1 lb ground beef
- ½ lb ground sausage

### Sauce

- Grape jelly
- Chili sauce

## Instructions

### Meatballs

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Mix everything together and shape into meatballs.
3. Bake for 25–30 minutes.

### Sauce

1. Heat equal parts grape jelly and chili sauce together.
2. Add the meatballs and heat through to serve.
