# Spanish rice

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice
- 4 slices bacon, chopped
- 1 onion, chopped
- 4 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
- Hot sauce, to taste

## Instructions

1. Cook the rice.
2. While it cooks, fry the bacon, then add the onion and cook until soft. Drain the grease.
3. Add the mushrooms and cook until tender.
4. Add the rice and mix together.
5. Stir in the tomato sauce and season to taste with hot sauce.
