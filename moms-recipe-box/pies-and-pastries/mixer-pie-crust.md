# Mixer pie crust

## Ingredients

- 1 cup flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 6 tablespoons shortening
- 2–3 tablespoons cold water

## Instructions

1. Measure the flour, salt, and shortening into a large mixing bowl. Blend 1 minute on low speed with an electric mixer, scraping the bowl constantly.
2. Add the water and mix until all the flour is moistened and the dough begins to gather on the beaters.
3. Gather the dough into a ball and shape it into a flattened round on a lightly floured, cloth-covered board.
4. With a floured rolling pin, roll the dough 2 inches larger than the inverted pie pan.
5. Fold the pastry into quarters, unfold, and ease it into the pan.
6. Trim the overhanging edge 1 inch from the rim of the pan. Fold and roll the pastry under, even with the pan, and flute.
7. For a baked pie shell, prick the bottom and side thoroughly with a fork. Bake at 475°F for 8–10 minutes. Cool and fill.

## Notes

- For a two-crust pie, double all the ingredients and follow the same directions.
