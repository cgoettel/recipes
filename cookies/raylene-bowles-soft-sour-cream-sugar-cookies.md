# Soft Sour Cream Sugar Cookies (Raylene Bowles)

## Ingredients

- 2/3 c. butter or margarine
- 2 c. sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 c. sour cream
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. soda
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 5 c. flour
- 1/4 tsp. nutmeg

## Instructions

1. Gradually add sugar to butter, beating until creamy. Beat in eggs, blend until fluffy. Add vanilla and sour cream.
2. Sift dry ingredients together and add to cream mixture. Mix well.
3. Cover and let chill overnight or four hours.
4. Roll a large portion of dough out on floured surface until 1/3-inch thick.
5. Hint: Keep other dough refrigerated until you use it. Cut out and place on greased cookie sheet.
6. Bake at 375 for 9 minutes or until barely brown. The amount of time varies with each size of cookie. For a Soft Cookie Do not overcook!
