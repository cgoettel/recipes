# Taco salad

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 package taco seasoning
- 1 head lettuce
- 2 tomatoes
- 1–2 cups grated cheese
- 1 bag corn chips
- Taco sauce, as the dressing

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef, drain off the excess grease, and add the taco seasoning.
2. Chop the lettuce and tomatoes and mix them in a serving bowl.
3. Add the corn chips, then the beef, and top with the grated cheese.
4. Use taco sauce as the dressing.
