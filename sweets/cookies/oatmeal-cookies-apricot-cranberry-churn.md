# Apricot cranberry oatmeal cookies (Churn knockoff)

Reverse-engineered from the cookie at Churn (Camelback & Central, Phoenix). The likely lineage is the Dahlia Bakery apricot-cranberry oatmeal cookie — cinnamon, ginger, and cloves — adapted here toward soft, dark, and spice-forward: lower oven temperature, all dark brown sugar plus molasses, and the fruit cut back so the oats and warming spices carry the cookie.

## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (1 cup) butter
- 1¼ cups dark brown sugar, packed
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon molasses
- 1 egg
- 1 egg yolk
- 1 tablespoon vanilla
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1½ teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- ½ teaspoon ground cloves
- ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1¾ cups flour
- 2 cups oats
- 2 tablespoons steel cut oats
- ½ cup dried apricots, diced fine (¼" or smaller — they should melt into the crumb, not announce themselves)
- ⅓ cup dried cranberries, roughly chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
2. Cream butter, sugars, and molasses.
3. Beat in egg, egg yolk, and vanilla.
4. Mix in salt, baking soda, and spices.
5. Mix in flour.
6. Add oats, then fold in apricots and cranberries.
7. Rest the dough 30 minutes at room temperature (or overnight, covered, in the fridge). This hydrates the oats and rounds out the spices; it also limits spread for a thicker, softer cookie.
8. Drop onto greased cookie sheet.
9. Bake 13–15 minutes. Pull while the centers still look slightly underdone — carryover heat finishes them and keeps them soft.

## Notes

- The spice blend is built to read on the back of the palate: cinnamon up front, ginger and cloves arriving late. To make the spice land harder, increase ginger before cinnamon.
- Calibrating "dark": if the target cookie reads molasses-dark (toward gingerbread), bump molasses to 2 tablespoons and cloves to ¾ teaspoon If it reads caramel-dark, the recipe as written should be close.
