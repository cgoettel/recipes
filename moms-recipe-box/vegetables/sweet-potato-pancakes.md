# Sweet potato pancakes

An unusual side dish. Sprinkled with sugar or maple syrup, they could even be served for breakfast.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb sweet potatoes
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup flour
- ½ teaspoon baking powder
- Salt, to taste
- Fresh white pepper, to taste
- Freshly grated nutmeg, to taste
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 3 tablespoons oil
- Maple syrup, for serving

## Instructions

1. Peel the sweet potatoes and cut them into ½-inch slices. Steam about 8 minutes, until soft, and drain.
2. Purée the sweet potatoes in a food processor, gradually adding the remaining ingredients except the butter, oil, and syrup. The mixture should be highly seasoned.
3. Heat half the butter and oil in a skillet or griddle over medium heat.
4. Ladle the batter into the pan in 3-tablespoon batches to make 3-inch pancakes. Cook about 2 minutes per side, until golden brown.
5. Use the remaining butter and oil for the rest of the batter. Serve at once with maple syrup.
