# Soda cracker cheesecake

A crisp meringue-and-cracker crust under a cream cheese layer and pie filling.

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 3 egg whites
- 1 cup sugar
- 14–20 soda crackers (the little squares), crushed
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 tablespoon vinegar
- ½ cup chopped pecans
- 1 teaspoon baking powder

### Topping

- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup Cool Whip
- ½ cup powdered sugar
- 1½ teaspoons vanilla
- 1 (21 oz) can pie filling

## Instructions

### Crust

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Beat the egg whites stiff, then mix in the sugar, crackers, vanilla, vinegar, pecans, and baking powder.
3. Spread in an ungreased 9×13-inch pan and bake 30 minutes.
4. Cool before adding the topping.

### Topping

1. Mix the cream cheese, Cool Whip, powdered sugar, and vanilla together, and spread over the cooled crust.
2. Spread the pie filling over the top.

## Notes

- The margin says to make the crust 1½ times, and "(2)" is written beside the cream cheese and Cool Whip, so doubling those makes a thicker layer.
