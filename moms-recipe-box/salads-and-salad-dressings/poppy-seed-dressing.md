# Poppy seed dressing

## Ingredients

- ½ cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon dry mustard
- 1 tablespoon dried onion flakes
- ½ cup white vinegar
- 1 cup salad oil
- 1 teaspoon poppy seeds

## Instructions

1. Combine everything in a jar, cover, and shake for a minute.

## Notes

- For a creamy version, use ¾ cup vinegar and replace the oil with ¾ cup mayonnaise.
