# Cherry cheesecake cookies

Mini cheesecakes baked in muffin liners.

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 1¼ cups crushed graham crackers
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- ⅓ cup butter, melted

### Filling

- 1 lb cream cheese, at room temperature
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 (21 oz) can cherry pie filling

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Line muffin tins with 24 paper liners.
2. Mix the graham crackers, sugar, and butter and set aside.
3. Blend the cream cheese and eggs together. Gradually add the sugar, then the vanilla.
4. Spoon a rounded teaspoon of the graham cracker mixture into each cup, then a well-rounded tablespoon of the cream cheese mixture on top of the crust.
5. Bake 10–12 minutes.
6. Cool, then top each with a spoonful of cherry pie filling.
