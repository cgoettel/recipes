# Lavender Honey Syrup

Honey syrup infused with culinary lavender — does double duty in a drink:
sweetens *and* carries the floral body that lavender bitters (aroma only) can't.
Built for the Lavender Matcha / Lavender Haze cocktails. Go light on the
lavender — it turns soapy fast.

## Ingredients

- 1 part honey
- 1 part warm water
- ~1 tsp dried culinary lavender per cup of finished syrup

## Instructions

1. Warm the water (don't boil) and steep the lavender in it for 5–8 minutes —
   taste at 5; pull it the moment it reads floral, before it goes soapy.
2. Strain out the lavender, then stir the warm lavender water into the honey
   until fully dissolved.
3. Store in the fridge; keeps ~2 weeks.
