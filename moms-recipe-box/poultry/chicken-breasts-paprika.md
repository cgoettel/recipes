# Paprika chicken breasts in mushroom cream

## Ingredients

- Chicken breasts
- Paprika (a lot)
- Garlic salt
- 1 (10.5 oz) can cream of mushroom soup
- ½ pint (1 cup) heavy whipping cream

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
2. Shake paprika (a lot) and garlic salt over both sides of the chicken breasts. Place them in a jelly roll pan.
3. Mix the soup and cream and pour over the chicken.
4. Cover with foil and bake 1 hour 15 minutes.
