# Baked custard cups

Only the back of this card survived: the water-bath baking method for a custard baked in cups. The ingredient list was on the missing front.

## Ingredients

- Custard mixture for custard cups (ingredients on the missing front of the card)
- Boiling water, for the water bath

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 300°F.
2. Set the filled custard cups in a 9×13-inch pan. Fill the pan with water a third of the way up the cups.
3. Bake 45 minutes, until puffed.
4. Take the cups out of the hot water and set them in cold water to stop the cooking.
