# Black-eyed Susans

## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (1 cup) butter, softened
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 cups flour
- ½ teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Cream the butter, sugar, and vanilla together.
3. Mix in the flour and salt.
4. Shape into cookies on an ungreased cookie sheet.
5. Bake for 7–8 minutes.

## Notes

- The card gives only the dough. Black-eyed Susans are traditionally rolled into balls, pressed with a thumbprint, and finished with a dot of chocolate in the center.
