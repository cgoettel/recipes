# Caramels

## Ingredients

- 2 cups white sugar
- 15 oz evaporated milk
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter, plus more for the tray
- 1 cup white Karo (light corn syrup)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Instructions

1. Butter a tray.
2. Cook the butter, sugar, and Karo over low heat, stirring constantly, for 10 minutes after the candy comes to a boil.
3. Slowly add the milk, about a tablespoon at a time.
4. Cook, stirring constantly, until the mixture darkens and thickens to the soft-ball stage, 236°F at sea level (test a drop in cold water).
5. Add the vanilla and pour into the buttered tray.
6. Let it cool, scoring it into squares while still warm.
7. Cut into pieces once cold. Wrap in waxed paper, or dip in chocolate.

## Notes

- The card calls for 1 cup Karo minus 1 tablespoon.
- The card's evaporated milk is an old 15 oz large can; today's large cans are 12 oz, so use one and a quarter cans.
- At altitude, lower the target temperature; see the candy-making tips.
