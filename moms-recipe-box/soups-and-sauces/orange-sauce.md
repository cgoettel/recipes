# Orange sauce

## Ingredients

- ½ cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 cup orange juice
- 1 tablespoon butter

## Instructions

1. Blend the sugar, cornstarch, and salt in a saucepan.
2. Stir in the orange juice and cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until it thickens.
3. Turn the heat to low for 2 minutes.
4. Remove from the heat and stir in the butter.
