# Orange Julius

## Ingredients

- 1 (6 oz) can frozen orange juice concentrate
- ½ cup milk
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1½ cups water
- 1 tray ice cubes

## Instructions

1. Put the orange juice concentrate, milk, sugar, vanilla, and water in a blender and blend until smooth.
2. With the blender running, add the ice cubes one at a time, blending until each is crushed and the drink is thick and frothy.
3. Serve right away.
