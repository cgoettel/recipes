# Pie crust (egg and vinegar)

## Ingredients

- 3 cups flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon baking powder
- 1¼ cups shortening
- 1 egg
- 6 tablespoons cold water
- 1 tablespoon vinegar

## Instructions

1. Combine the flour, salt, and baking powder, and cut in the shortening.
2. Beat the egg, water, and vinegar together and add to the dry ingredients. Blend well.

## Notes

- The dough freezes well.
