# Sweet potato casserole

## Ingredients

- Crushed pineapple, for the bottom of the dish
- Yams (sweet potatoes), cooked or canned
- Butter
- A handful of brown sugar
- ½ teaspoon ginger

### Topping

- 1 small can pineapple
- Brown sugar
- Nuts
- Miniature marshmallows

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Layer the bottom of a casserole dish with crushed pineapple.
3. Heat the yams first, then add the butter, a handful of brown sugar, and the ginger, and bring to hot.
4. Remove from the heat and beat with a mixer until fluffy. Spoon into the casserole dish over the pineapple.
5. Mix the small can of pineapple, brown sugar, and nuts together and layer it on top.
6. Bake for about 30 minutes, until the pineapple and brown sugar are bubbly.
7. Take it out right before serving and arrange the marshmallows in a design on top. Return to the oven until the marshmallows are golden brown.
