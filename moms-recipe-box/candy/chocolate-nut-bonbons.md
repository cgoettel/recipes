# Chocolate nut bonbons

## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (1 cup) butter, softened
- 1 (14 oz) can sweetened condensed milk
- 2 (1 lb) boxes powdered sugar
- 2 (14 oz) packages flaked coconut
- 1 lb walnuts or pecans, finely chopped
- 3 (12 oz) packages chocolate chips
- 1 block household paraffin

## Instructions

1. Mix the butter, condensed milk, powdered sugar, coconut, and nuts.
2. Roll into small balls (about 150), place on a cookie sheet, and chill.
3. Melt the chocolate chips and paraffin slowly in a saucepan over the lowest heat.
4. Stick a toothpick in each ball and dip it in the melted chocolate. Set back on a wax-paper-lined cookie sheet to harden.
5. Freeze or refrigerate until ready to use.

## Notes

- Chop the nuts very fine; the card suggests a meat grinder.
- Use food-grade paraffin wax, sold for canning. It helps the chocolate set glossy and firm.
