# Creamy chile con queso

## Ingredients

- 1 small onion, minced
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 cup canned tomatoes
- 1 (4 oz) can green chiles, peeled and chopped
- Salt and pepper
- ½ cup Monterey Jack cheese, cubed
- 1 cup cream

## Instructions

1. Sauté the onion in the butter until soft.
2. Add the tomatoes, chiles, and salt and pepper to taste, and simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Add the cheese. When it begins to melt, stir in the cream.
4. Keep warm in a chafing dish to serve.
