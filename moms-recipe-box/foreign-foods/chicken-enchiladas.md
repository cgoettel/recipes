# Chicken enchiladas

## Ingredients

- 8 flour tortillas
- 2–3 cups cooked chicken, boned and shredded
- 1 (8 oz) container sour cream
- 2 (10.5 oz) cans cream of mushroom or cream of chicken soup
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 cups grated cheese
- 1 (4 oz) can diced green chiles

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Combine the broth and soup. Spread a small amount in the bottom of a 9×13-inch pan.
3. Dip each tortilla in the soup mixture, fill it with chicken, onion, and cheese, roll it up, and place it in the pan.
4. Sprinkle the remaining cheese on top, then the green chiles to taste.
5. Cover with the sour cream and the remaining soup mixture.
6. Bake 30–35 minutes.
