# Bubble bath salts

A homemade gift: layer several colors in each bottle.

## Ingredients

- 1 (5 lb) package Epsom salts
- 2 cups powdered detergent (such as Vel or Dreft), plus more as needed
- 3 tablespoons rubbing alcohol
- 2 tablespoons cologne (odds and ends work)
- Food coloring

## Instructions

1. Mix the Epsom salts and detergent well.
2. Stir the food coloring into the alcohol or cologne first, then add both to the salts.
3. Mix well with your hands.
4. To make several colors, divide the recipe into batches. Put some of each color in each bottle.
