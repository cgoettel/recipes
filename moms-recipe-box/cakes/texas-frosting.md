# Texas frosting

The frosting for [Texas sheet cake](texas-sheet-cake.md).

## Ingredients

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- 6 tablespoons milk
- 4 tablespoons cocoa
- 1 (1 lb) box powdered sugar
- 1 cup nuts, chopped
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Instructions

1. Boil the milk, butter, and cocoa until bubbly.
2. Mix in the nuts, powdered sugar, and vanilla.
3. Spread over the Texas sheet cake.
