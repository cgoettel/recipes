# Honey French dressing

Serve over mixed greens or fruit.

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup honey
- ⅓ cup oil
- ⅓ cup vinegar
- ⅓ cup ketchup
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon pepper
- ½ teaspoon celery seed
- ½ teaspoon paprika

## Instructions

1. Combine everything in a blender and blend until smooth.
2. Serve over mixed greens or fruit.
