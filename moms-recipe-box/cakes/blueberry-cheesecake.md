# Blueberry cheesecake

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter, melted
- ½ cup sugar

### Filling

- 2 (8 oz) packages cream cheese, softened
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 (21 oz) can blueberry pie filling

## Instructions

### Crust

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Mix the graham cracker crumbs, butter, and sugar together and press into the bottom of a 9×13-inch pan.

### Filling

1. Mix the cream cheese, eggs, sugar, and vanilla until smooth. Pour over the crust.
2. Bake 15 minutes. Don't overcook it.
3. Let it cool, then pour the blueberry pie filling over the top.
