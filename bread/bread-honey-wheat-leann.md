# Bread — Honey Wheat (LeAnn's)

From LeAnn Goettel. Makes 3 loaves.

## Ingredients

- 3 C hot water
- ¼ C oil
- ¼ C honey
- 2 T dough enhancer
- 2 t salt
- 4 C whole wheat flour
- 2–4 C all-purpose flour (added gradually)

## Instructions

1. Combine wet ingredients, salt, dough enhancer, and the whole wheat flour in a mixer. Mix on high for 2 minutes.
1. Add AP flour slowly until the side of the mixer is clean.
