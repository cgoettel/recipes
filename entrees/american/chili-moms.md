# Chili — Mom's

From LeAnn. The longer it simmers, the better it gets. Sausage, green chiles, garlic, and diced tomatoes are all good additions — flavor it to your liking.

## Ingredients

- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1½ lb hamburger
- ⅔ cup ketchup
- ½ cup water
- ¼ cup celery, finely chopped
- 1 (16 oz) can kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (15 oz) can pinto beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1½ teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 teaspoon white vinegar
- ½ teaspoon seasoned salt
- ¼ teaspoon dry mustard
- Chili powder (see Notes)

## Instructions

1. Sauté the onion in butter.
2. Add the hamburger and brown it. Drain off excess fat.
3. Add all remaining ingredients except the beans. Mix well and heat thoroughly.
4. Add the beans, an additional large can of diced tomatoes, and 3–6 teaspoons of chili powder to taste.
5. Cover and simmer ~30 minutes.

## Notes

- Mom likes it thick — adjust water accordingly.
