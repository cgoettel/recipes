# Banana nut bread

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sifted flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon baking powder
- ½ teaspoon nutmeg
- 1 stick (½ cup) butter, softened
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs, slightly beaten
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 3 ripe bananas, mashed
- ½ cup milk
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 1 cup finely chopped nuts (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9×5×3-inch loaf pan.
2. Sift together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and nutmeg.
3. In a bowl, beat the butter and sugar well.
4. Add the eggs, vanilla, bananas, milk, lemon juice, and nuts.
5. Add the flour mixture a little at a time, beating well after each addition.
6. Turn into the pan and bake for 1 hour.
7. Cool 5 minutes, turn out of the pan, and let stand several hours before slicing.
