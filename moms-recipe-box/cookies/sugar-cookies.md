# Sugar cookies

## Ingredients

- 1⅓ cups shortening
- 1½ cups sugar
- 1 teaspoon orange peel
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 8 teaspoons milk
- 2 eggs
- 4 cups flour
- 3 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Cream the wet ingredients together: shortening, sugar, orange peel, vanilla, milk, and eggs.
3. Stir together the flour, baking powder, and salt and add them.
4. Shape and bake 6–8 minutes.

## Notes

- The card doesn't say how to shape them. The dough is stiff enough to roll out and cut, or to roll into balls and flatten.
