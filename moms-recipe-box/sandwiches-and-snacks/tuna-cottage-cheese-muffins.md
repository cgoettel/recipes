# Tuna and cottage cheese English muffins

## Ingredients

- 8 oz tuna, drained and flaked
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 4 teaspoons mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons chopped green onion
- 2 tablespoons chopped celery
- 3 radishes, chopped
- Salt and pepper
- 2 English muffins, split

## Instructions

1. Mix the tuna, cottage cheese, mayonnaise, green onion, celery, and radishes. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
2. Spoon onto the muffin halves.
