# Coffee — Cold Brew

## Ratio

- 1:8 coffee:water (ground at a 9.1 on the Fellow Ode Gen 2)

## Instructions

### Hario Mizudashi cold brew maker

The goal with this method is to use an entire 2 qt glass milk jug for cold brew + creamer. For our ratio (in the French press it was 433:750 coffee:total), we need 1017:1762 grams.

- 127 g coffee
- Should be ~1017 g water in... but we want 1017 g water out. And I think the Mizudashi only handles 1,000 g water total. Let's revisit this.

### French press

- 70 g coffee
- Should be about 560 g water in
- Stir for 30 seconds
- Let sit for 20–24 hours
- Pour into a container; should yield around 433 g out
- Add creamer until you're right around 750 g (about 320 g creamer)
