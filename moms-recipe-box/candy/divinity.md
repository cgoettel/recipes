# Divinity

## Ingredients

- 1 cup light corn syrup (white Karo)
- 4 cups white sugar
- 2 cups water
- 2 egg whites
- Nuts, coconut, candied fruit, or flavorings (optional)

## Instructions

1. Line a counter or sheet with buttered wax paper.
2. Combine the corn syrup, sugar, and water and cook without stirring to 260°F at sea level.
3. Beat the egg whites stiff. Add the hot syrup slowly while beating.
4. Add nuts, coconut, candied fruit, or flavorings as you like.
5. Beat until the candy holds its shape.
6. Drop by teaspoonfuls onto the buttered wax paper and let cool.

## Notes

- 260°F is the sea-level hard-ball temperature. At altitude, subtract about 2°F for every 1,000 feet.
