# Cinnamon rolls (LeAnn's)

From LeAnn Goettel. Distinct from the [other cinnamon rolls](cinnamon-rolls.md) — this version uses oil instead of lard and gets an overnight counter rise before baking.

## Ingredients

- 3 C hot water (as hot as the tap will run)
- 1 T salt
- ½ C oil
- 1 C sugar
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 10 C flour
- 1 packet instant yeast

### Filling

- Brown sugar
- White sugar
- Cinnamon
- Melted butter, for brushing

### Frosting and glaze

- Your favorite — see [the other cinnamon rolls recipe](cinnamon-rolls.md) for a simple butter/powdered sugar/milk/vanilla frosting.

## Instructions

1. Place the water in the mixing bowl. Layer in salt, oil, sugar, eggs, and half of the flour. Sprinkle the yeast packet over the top. Mix on high for 90 seconds to activate the yeast and develop gluten.
1. With the mixer running, add the remaining flour 1 cup at a time. The dough has enough flour when it pulls away from the side of the bowl and the dough hook — adding too much makes the dough stiff and hard to handle. Mix on high ~3 minutes to knead.
1. Remove the dough and knead briefly by hand on a greased surface. Cut in half; return one half to the mixer.
1. Working on a greased surface, roll the dough into a large rectangle (~10" × 13"). Brush with melted butter. Distribute white and brown sugar across the surface (more brown than white), getting close to the edges. Sprinkle generously with cinnamon.
1. Starting at the bottom of the width, roll into a tight jelly roll. With a bench scraper or serrated knife: even the ends, cut in half, in half again, then each section into thirds — 12 rolls per half batch.
1. Place rolls in greased jelly roll pans and cover with a light cloth.
1. Repeat with the second half of dough.
1. Leave covered on the counter overnight.
1. In the morning, preheat oven to 375°F and bake 15 minutes. Frost and glaze.
