# Waffles

## Ingredients

- 1¾ cups flour
- 3 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 eggs, separated
- 1¼ cups milk
- ½ cup oil

## Instructions

1. Preheat the waffle iron.
2. Mix the egg yolks, milk, and oil together.
3. Stir the flour, baking powder, and salt together, then add them to the yolk mixture.
4. In a separate bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff, then fold them into the batter.
5. Bake in the hot waffle iron until golden.
