# Ginger cookies

Rolled and cut ginger cookies made with butter and lard.

## Ingredients

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter
- 4 oz lard
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- ½ cup sour cream
- ½ cup molasses
- 2 teaspoons baking soda, divided
- 4 cups flour
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ginger

## Instructions

01. Cream the butter and lard thoroughly.
02. Add the sugar gradually, beating until light.
03. Add the egg and beat well.
04. Add the sour cream and beat well.
05. Stir 1 teaspoon of the baking soda into the molasses and keep stirring vigorously until it has risen into a frothy mixture, then mix it in.
06. Combine the flour, the remaining 1 teaspoon baking soda, the cinnamon, and the ginger.
07. Add the dry ingredients about ½ cup at a time, mixing well at low speed. When the dough gets too stiff and heavy for the mixer, stir in the rest by hand.
08. Chill the dough thoroughly, several hours or overnight.
09. Roll the dough out about ¼ inch thick, or a little less, on a well-floured pastry cloth with a well-floured sleeve on the rolling pin.
10. Cut into shapes and place on lightly greased cookie sheets.
11. Bake at 350°F for 9–12 minutes: 9 minutes for soft cookies, 12 for crisp.
