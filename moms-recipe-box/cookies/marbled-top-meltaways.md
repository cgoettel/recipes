# Marbled top meltaways

## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (1 cup) butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 cups flour
- ½ cup walnuts, chopped
- ½ cup chocolate chips
- ½ cup butterscotch chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
2. Cream the butter and sugar. Blend in the milk and vanilla, then add the flour and nuts.
3. Spread in an ungreased 9x13-inch pan.
4. Bake 30–35 minutes.
5. Sprinkle immediately with the chocolate and butterscotch chips. Let stand 5 minutes, then spread the melted chips to make a marbled frosting.
