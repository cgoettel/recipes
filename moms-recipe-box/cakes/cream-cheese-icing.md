# Cream cheese icing

The back of a cake card. Its cake bakes at 350°F for 40–45 minutes, which makes the [poppy seed cake](poppy-seed-cake.md) the likely match.

## Ingredients

- 1 stick (½ cup) butter, softened
- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 1 lb powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Instructions

1. Cream the butter and cream cheese together until smooth.
2. Add the powdered sugar gradually, beating after each addition.
3. Beat in the vanilla.
4. Spread on a cooled cake.
