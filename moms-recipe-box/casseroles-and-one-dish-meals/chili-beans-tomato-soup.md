# Chili beans with tomato soup

## Ingredients

- 2 cups dried beans
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 cup condensed tomato soup
- ¼ cup ketchup
- 1 lb ground beef
- ⅓ onion, chopped
- Garlic
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1–3 teaspoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Cook the beans with the bay leaf in water until soft.
2. Once the beans are soft, add the tomato soup and ketchup.
3. Brown the ground beef with the onion and garlic, drain, and add to the beans.
4. Stir in the sugar and chili powder and simmer until done.
5. When done, add the salt.
