# Unleavened bread for Passover

## Ingredients

- 4 cups (≈480 g) flour
- 1½ cups (≈340 g) water
- 2 tablespoons (≈25 g) olive oil

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
2. Combine ingredients and knead for ten minutes.
3. Roll into a ball, cut in half, then cut each half into 8 pieces.
4. Roll out each piece as thinly as possible into ovals.
5. Pierce with a fork several times to eliminate air holes.
6. Place on baking sheet covered with parchment paper.
7. Bake at 450°F for about 5 minutes, or until slightly browned.
