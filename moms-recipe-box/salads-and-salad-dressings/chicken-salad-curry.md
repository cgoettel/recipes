# Curried chicken salad

## Ingredients

- Chicken
- Salt
- Walnuts, or any other nut
- 2 cups chopped celery
- 3 tablespoons chopped onion
- 1 can crushed pineapple, with juice
- Mayonnaise
- Curry powder
- Sage or poultry seasoning

## Instructions

1. Boil the chicken in salted water until cooked through. Let it cool, then dice it.
2. Add the walnuts, celery, onion, and crushed pineapple with its juice.
3. Mix with mayonnaise and curry powder to taste, and season with salt and a little sage or poultry seasoning.

## Notes

- Be generous with the celery; the card says "lots."
