# Tropical salsa

Great with blue corn chips.

## Ingredients

- ½ ripe avocado, peeled
- ½ ripe papaya, peeled
- 1 ripe tomato, seeded
- ¼ fresh pineapple, peeled and cored
- 1 fresh jalapeño, seeded
- 2 scallions, trimmed and sliced
- Juice of ½ lemon
- ¼ cup olive oil

## Instructions

1. Finely chop the avocado, papaya, tomato, pineapple, and jalapeño, and combine them in a bowl.
2. Add the scallions, lemon juice, and olive oil, and stir well.
3. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
