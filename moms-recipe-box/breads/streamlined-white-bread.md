# Streamlined white bread

A quick, one-rise loaf. The same dough makes a pizza crust.

## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups warm water
- 1 tablespoon yeast
- 2 tablespoons shortening
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 3 cups sifted flour

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water.
2. Add the shortening, salt, sugar, and half the flour, and beat for 2 minutes.
3. Add the rest of the flour and mix well.
4. Let rise for 30 minutes.
5. Punch down and beat for 1 minute. Put the dough in a greased loaf pan.
6. Bake at 375°F for 40 minutes.

## Notes

- For pizza, spread the dough in a greased pan, top it, and bake at 400°F for 20–25 minutes.
