# Short cut cookies

Cake mix cookies.

## Ingredients

- 1 small package cake mix, any flavor
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter, softened
- 2 tablespoons water
- ½ teaspoon vanilla
- 1 egg

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Empty half the cake mix into a bowl and add the butter, water, vanilla, and egg. Mix well.
3. Add the rest of the mix and blend.
4. Drop onto ungreased cookie sheets and bake.

## Notes

- Spice variation: add ½ teaspoon cinnamon and ¼ teaspoon nutmeg to a white or yellow cake mix.
- Stir in ¼ cup chopped nuts or ¼ cup raisins.
- Or sprinkle sugar on top of the cookies before baking.
