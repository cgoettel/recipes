# Almond pudding loaf

## Ingredients

### Loaf

- 1⅓ cups toasted, finely chopped whole natural almonds
- 2½ cups Bisquick
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 (3.4 oz) package vanilla instant pudding
- ⅔ cup milk
- ¼ cup vegetable oil
- 4 eggs

### Creamy glaze

- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon rum extract, almond extract, or vanilla
- 1–2 tablespoons milk

## Instructions

### Loaf

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Generously grease a 9×5×3-inch loaf pan.
2. Sprinkle ⅓ cup of the almonds over the bottom and sides of the pan, pressing them on if needed.
3. Put the remaining almonds and all the other loaf ingredients in a large mixer bowl. Beat on low speed for 30 seconds, scraping the bowl constantly, then on medium speed for 3 minutes, scraping occasionally.
4. Pour into the pan and bake 50–55 minutes, until a wooden pick inserted in the center comes out clean.
5. Remove from the pan right away and cool completely on a wire rack.

### Creamy glaze

1. Beat the powdered sugar, extract, and milk until smooth and as thick as you like.
2. Spread over the cooled loaf and garnish as you like.

## Notes

- High altitude (3,500–6,500 feet): heat the oven to 375°F. Use 2 cups Bisquick plus ½ cup flour, leave out the sugar, and increase the milk to ⅞ cup. Beat on medium speed for 1 minute and bake 45–50 minutes.
