# Pork and beans

## Ingredients

- 2 (21 oz) cans pork and beans
- ½ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup ketchup
- 1 teaspoon dry mustard
- 2 tablespoons dark molasses
- 4–6 slices bacon, slightly browned
- 2–4 slices onion

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Drain the juice off the beans.
3. Mix the beans with the brown sugar, ketchup, dry mustard, and molasses in a baking dish. Lay the bacon and onion slices on top.
4. Cover and bake 20 minutes, then uncover and bake another 20 minutes.
