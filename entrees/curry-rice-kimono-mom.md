# Curry Rice (Kimono Mom)

## Servings

4

## Ingredients

- 1 onion
- 1 clove of garlic
- 1 clove of ginger
- 300g chicken legs
- 300g potatoes
- 1 carrot
- 20g butter
- 4 tbsp of flour
- 3 tbsp of curry powder
- 1 bouillon cube
- 2 tbsp of ketchup
- 1 tsp of instant coffee
- 1 tsp of sugar
- 1 tbsp of Worcestershire sauce (Lea & Perrins sauce or BBQ sauce)
- 200 ml vegetable juice
- 600 ml water
