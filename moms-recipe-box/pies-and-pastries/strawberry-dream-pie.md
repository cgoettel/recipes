# Strawberry dream pie

From a Jell-O and Dream Whip advertisement.

## Ingredients

- 1 (4-serving) package Jell-O vanilla pudding and pie filling (cook-type)
- 1 (3 oz) package strawberry Jell-O
- 2 cups water
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 1½ cups prepared Dream Whip whipped topping, plus more for garnish
- 1 cup sliced strawberries, plus more for garnish
- 1 (9-inch) baked pie shell, cooled

## Instructions

1. Combine the pudding mix, gelatin, water, and lemon juice in a saucepan. Cook and stir over medium heat until the mixture comes to a boil.
2. Pour into a bowl and chill until thickened. To hurry it, set the bowl in a larger bowl of ice and water and stir until thickened.
3. Blend in the whipped topping, then stir in the strawberries.
4. Pour into the pie shell and chill until set, 1–2 hours.
5. Garnish with more whipped topping and strawberries.
