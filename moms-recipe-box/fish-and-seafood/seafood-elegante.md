# Seafood elegante

## Ingredients

- 2 (6 oz) packages cream cheese
- 2 (10.5 oz) cans cream of mushroom soup, undiluted
- Worcestershire sauce, to taste
- 1 can fancy shrimp, drained
- 1 can fancy crab, drained and flaked
- Cooked rice, for serving

## Instructions

1. In a double boiler, stir the cream cheese and soup together until smooth.
2. Add Worcestershire sauce to taste.
3. When heated through, stir in the crab and shrimp.
4. Serve over fluffy rice.
