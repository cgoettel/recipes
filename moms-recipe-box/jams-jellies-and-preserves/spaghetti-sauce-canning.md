# Spaghetti sauce for canning

A big-batch sauce for bottling.

## Ingredients

- 8–9 onions, chopped
- ½ cup olive oil
- 9 quarts peeled and chopped tomatoes
- ¼ cup sugar
- 3 tablespoons basil
- 3 tablespoons oregano
- 7–9 bay leaves
- ⅓ cup salt
- 1 tablespoon pepper
- 10–12 cloves garlic, chopped or minced
- 6 cups tomato paste (about 9 [6 oz] cans)
- Lemon juice, 1 teaspoon per quart jar

## Instructions

1. Sauté the onions in the olive oil until soft.
2. Stir in the tomatoes, sugar, basil, oregano, bay leaves, salt, pepper, garlic, and tomato paste.
3. Simmer for 2 hours. Remove the bay leaves.
4. Add 1 teaspoon lemon juice to each quart jar as you bottle the sauce.
5. Process 10 minutes in a water-bath canner.

## Notes

- The card's 10-minute processing time is far shorter than current USDA guidance for tomato sauce in quarts (about 40–45 minutes in a boiling-water canner, longer at altitude). Check a current tested recipe before canning.
