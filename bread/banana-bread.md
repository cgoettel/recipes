# Banana bread
## Ingredients
- 2 C flour
- 1 t baking soda
- &frac14; t salt
- &frac12; C butter
- &frac34; C brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 &frac13; C mashed bananas (like 4-6 bananas)
- (optional) chocolate chips

## Directions
- Preheat oven to 350&deg;F
- In a large bowl, cream butter and sugar. Add eggs and beat. Add baking soda and salt. Beat. Add mashed bananas and beat. Finally, add flour and beat.
- (optional) Add as many chocolate chips as you think is reasonable. Dark and semi-sweet work best.
- Pour batter into a greased 9"x5" loaf pan.
- Bake for 50-60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
