# Chile relleno quiche

## Ingredients

- 2 (4 oz) cans chopped green chiles, drained
- 10 oz longhorn cheese, shredded
- 1 cup milk
- 8 eggs
- ¼ cup flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- Guacamole, for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Butter an 8-inch square casserole.
2. Beat the eggs, milk, flour, and salt together with a mixer or blender.
3. Make alternate layers of chiles, cheese, and egg mixture in the casserole.
4. Bake 35–45 minutes, until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
5. Cut in squares and top with guacamole.
