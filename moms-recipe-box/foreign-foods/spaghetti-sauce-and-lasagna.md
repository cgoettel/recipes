# Spaghetti sauce and lasagna

A meat sauce for spaghetti that doubles as the sauce for a lasagna.

## Ingredients

### Sauce

- 1½ lb ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 green pepper, chopped
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
- ⅛ teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt and pepper
- ½ teaspoon oregano
- Sweet basil, to taste
- Italian seasoning, to taste
- 1 (14.5 oz) can tomatoes
- 2 (8 oz) cans tomato sauce
- 1 can water

### Lasagna

- Lasagna noodles, cooked
- White cheese (such as ricotta or cream cheese), softened
- Yellow cheese (such as cheddar), grated

## Instructions

### Sauce

1. Brown the ground beef with the onion, green pepper, and mushrooms, and drain.
2. Add the garlic powder, salt, pepper, oregano, basil, and Italian seasoning.
3. Stir in the tomatoes, tomato sauce, and water, and let simmer.

### Lasagna

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Layer noodles, sauce, white cheese, and yellow cheese in a baking dish. Repeat the layers 2–2½ times.
3. Bake 30 minutes.
