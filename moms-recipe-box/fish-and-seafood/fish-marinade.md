# Fish marinade

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup orange juice
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 2 tablespoons ketchup
- 2 tablespoons snipped parsley
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- ½ teaspoon pepper

## Instructions

1. Whisk everything together.
2. Marinate the fish 30 minutes before cooking.
