# Panna Cotta

A classic Italian set custard. It's really a ratio to remember: one envelope of gelatin sets about 3 cups of dairy. Don't let the cream come to a hard boil — you want it just to the point of steaming with bubbles at the edge, enough to dissolve the sugar and marry the gelatin, no more. Yields 8 servings.

## Ingredients

- 1 envelope unflavored gelatin (about 1 Tbsp)
- 2 Tbsp cold water
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1 cup half-and-half
- ⅓ cup sugar
- 1½ tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. In a very small saucepan, sprinkle the gelatin over the cold water and let it stand about 1 minute to soften.
2. Heat the softened gelatin over low heat just until it dissolves, then remove from heat. Don't let it boil.
3. In a large saucepan, bring the cream, half-and-half, and sugar just to a boil over moderately high heat, stirring. Pull it off the heat the moment it reaches a boil.
4. Stir in the gelatin mixture and the vanilla.
5. Divide the mixture among eight ½-cup ramekins and cool to room temperature. Chill, covered, at least 4 hours or overnight.
6. To unmold, dip each ramekin into a bowl of hot water for 3 seconds, run a thin knife around the edge, and invert onto a plate.

## Notes

- To serve in the ramekin instead of unmolding, skip the hot-water dip. A spoonful of [strawberry syrup](../../drinks/syrups/strawberry-syrup.md) or fresh berries over the top is the usual finish.
