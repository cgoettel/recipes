# Campers' hot chocolate

A make-ahead hot chocolate mix to keep in a big canister.

## Ingredients

- 1 box nonfat dry milk (enough to make 8 quarts)
- 1 (6 oz) jar Coffee-mate powdered creamer
- 1 (16 oz) box Nestlé Quik
- ¼ cup powdered sugar

## Instructions

1. Mix everything together and store in a large airtight canister.
2. To serve, stir ⅓ cup of the mix into 1 cup hot water.
