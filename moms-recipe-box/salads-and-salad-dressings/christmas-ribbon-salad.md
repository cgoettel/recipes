# Christmas ribbon salad

Three layers: lime, a creamy pineapple middle, and cherry on top.

## Ingredients

- 1 large (6 oz) package lime Jell-O
- 1 small (3 oz) package lemon Jell-O
- 1 cup hot water
- ½ cup marshmallows, cut in small pieces
- 1 (20 oz) can crushed pineapple, drained, juice reserved
- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup heavy cream, whipped
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1 large (6 oz) package cherry Jell-O

## Instructions

### Lime layer

1. Prepare the lime Jell-O according to the package directions.
2. Pour into a 15×10×2-inch glass or enamel pan and chill until set.

### Pineapple layer

1. Dissolve the lemon Jell-O in 1 cup hot water in a double boiler.
2. Add the marshmallows and stir until melted. Remove from the heat.
3. Add 1 cup of the reserved pineapple juice and the cream cheese. Beat with a rotary beater or mixer until well blended.
4. Stir in 1 cup of the drained pineapple and let cool.
5. Fold in the mayonnaise and whipped cream.
6. When cool and thickened, pour over the lime layer. Chill until set.

### Cherry layer

1. Prepare the cherry Jell-O according to the package directions and chill until cool and syrupy.
2. Pour over the pineapple layer and chill until set.
