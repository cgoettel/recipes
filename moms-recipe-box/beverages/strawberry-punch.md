# Strawberry punch

## Ingredients

- 1 (10 oz) box frozen strawberries, thawed and sliced
- 1 (6 oz) can frozen orange juice concentrate, undiluted
- 1 (6 oz) can frozen lemonade concentrate, undiluted
- 1½ quarts water
- 1 pint ginger ale, chilled

## Instructions

1. Crush the strawberries.
2. Mix the undiluted orange juice and lemonade concentrates with the water and chill.
3. Just before serving, add the ginger ale and the crushed strawberries.
