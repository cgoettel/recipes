# Lollipops

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup light corn syrup
- ½ cup water
- Food coloring and flavoring

## Equipment

- Lollipop sticks or skewers

## Instructions

1. Butter baking sheets and lay the sticks on them about 4 inches apart.
2. Combine the sugar, corn syrup, and water.
3. Cook over medium heat without stirring until a small amount of the mixture separates into hard, brittle threads when dropped into very cold water (the hard-crack stage, about 300°F at sea level).
4. Cool slightly, then blend in the coloring and flavoring.
5. Drop the syrup from the tip of a teaspoon onto the end of each stick.
6. If the syrup hardens before you finish, return it to low heat to melt.
