# Banana Oleo-Saccharum

A no-heat oleo-saccharum made from banana peels — sugar draws the aromatic oil and banana essence straight out of the peels over a couple of days, giving a clear, intensely banana syrup for tiki drinks. It puts the peels you'd otherwise toss to work, so save them when a recipe uses the fruit.

## Ingredients

- 250 g banana peels
- 250 g caster sugar

## Instructions

1. Combine the peels and sugar in a sealed plastic bag and macerate for 48 hours.
2. Strain off the liquid, bottle it, and refrigerate.
