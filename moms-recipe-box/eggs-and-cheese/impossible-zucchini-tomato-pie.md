# Impossible zucchini-tomato pie

## Ingredients

- 2 cups chopped zucchini
- 1 cup chopped tomato
- ½ cup chopped onion
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1½ cups milk
- ¾ cup Bisquick
- 3 eggs
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Grease a 10-inch quiche dish or pie plate.
2. Sprinkle the zucchini, tomato, onion, and cheese in the plate.
3. Beat the remaining ingredients until smooth, 15 seconds in a blender on high or 1 minute with a hand beater. Pour into the plate.
4. Bake until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean, about 30 minutes. Cool 5 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- At high altitude, bake about 40 minutes.
