# Grits casserole

## Ingredients

- 1 cup instant grits, cooked
- 1 cup grated sharp cheese
- ½ stick (4 tablespoons) butter
- ½ teaspoon Tabasco sauce
- 2 eggs, well beaten
- 1 (4 oz) can chopped green chiles
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a casserole dish.
2. Combine the hot cooked grits with the cheese, butter, Tabasco, eggs, and green chiles. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Pour into the casserole and bake uncovered for 1 hour.
