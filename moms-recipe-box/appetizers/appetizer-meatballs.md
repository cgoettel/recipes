# Appetizer meatballs

## Ingredients

### Meatballs

- 1 lb ground beef (ground round)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon pepper
- ¼ cup ketchup
- ½ cup cornflake crumbs
- ½ cup evaporated milk
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- ¼ cup finely chopped onion

### Sauce

- ½ large bottle ketchup
- 2 teaspoons hot horseradish

## Instructions

### Meatballs

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Mix all the meatball ingredients and shape into tiny meatballs.
3. Bake for 12–15 minutes. Baking them is part of the secret of their special flavor.

### Sauce

1. Mix the ketchup and horseradish.
2. Simmer the meatballs in the sauce in a chafing dish to serve.

## Notes

- The meatballs can be frozen and reheated.
- One pound of ground round makes about 41 small meatballs.
