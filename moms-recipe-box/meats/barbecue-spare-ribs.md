# Barbecue spare ribs

## Ingredients

- 3 lb spare ribs
- 1½ cups ketchup
- 1 teaspoon celery salt
- ½ teaspoon onion salt or onion juice
- Salt and pepper to taste
- ⅔ cup brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon mustard
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce or A.1. steak sauce

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
2. Mix everything except the ribs together to make the sauce.
3. Cut the ribs into individual servings.
4. Dip each piece into the sauce and lay it in a covered roaster, then pour the remaining sauce over the ribs.
5. Cover and bake 2½ hours.
