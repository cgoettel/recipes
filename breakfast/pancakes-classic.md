# Classic pancakes

## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups flour
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 egg
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons oil

## Instructions

1. Sift together flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
2. Add egg, milk, and oil. Beat.
3. Bake on medium griddle (350-375°F).
