# Crema — Lime Cilantro

For wraps, tacos, or eating with a spoon.

## Ingredients

- 1 C sour cream (plus up to ¼ C more)
- Handful of cilantro, stemmed and finely chopped
- Juice of 1 lime (roll it before halving)
- ¼ t cumin
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Start with 1 C of the sour cream and mix in the cilantro. Add most of the lime juice and the cumin. Mix well.
1. If it's too thick, add more lime juice. If it's too thin, add a bit more sour cream.
1. Salt and pepper at the end, if at all.

## Notes

- A few chopped pickled jalapeños add a kick — pat them dry before chopping. Will make it less kid-friendly.
